# Review two (Oct 17)

The second full review, done right after the loose-ends and placeholder work.
Five reviewers covered October, November–December, January–February,
March–May and the code. They read for bugs first, then continuity, then the
voice. Everything they found that had a clear fix is fixed on branch
`review-two`. Nothing needed a decision from you.

## Code

- **Buttons no longer steal Space and Enter.** A button you'd clicked kept
  keyboard focus. Pressing Space for the next line pressed it again: after
  "Take", every Space took another $20 from the till.
- **A soft lock in walk-around scenes.** If you clicked past a line that
  changes the room while the room was still fading, you were left with no
  spots. Night scenes were fixed last week; visit scenes are fixed now.
- **New stock can be put on an occupied spot.** Whatever was there goes back
  on the counter.
- **The back shelf has 20 spots** (4 rows of 5), up from 12. Up to 26 held
  things come in over the season, and with 12 spots some had nowhere to go by
  spring.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - The back door's hotspot no longer covers the new sink.
  - The title's key hint drops "Esc: menu", since Esc does nothing there.
  - Esc closes the pockets before opening the menu.
  - The Mac build is signed ad hoc, so it runs on Apple Silicon.

## The story
