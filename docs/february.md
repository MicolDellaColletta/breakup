# Breakup: February (plan)

Key days 31 to 34, from `season.md` (which had two; the revised December
showed that more days, with Daniel in every one, work better). A plan to
agree before building. **Leans** are my recommendations; "Your call" marks
the decisions.

February is the month the town decides who carries it. The sermons are all
Leviticus 16 now; on the burned tower there's a new pine post with a
crosspiece, and nobody will say what it's for. The second goat is chosen in
February. Everything MC has kept or broken comes due on three people: Ezra
(whose hand is broken), Ruth (whose lie comes out), and Olstad (who needs the
truth about the John night). And Sam, who the town has started to call the
man with the lights on the ice.

## How the town gets weirder in February

1. **The post.** The pine thing on the tower stump is finished: a tall post,
   a crosspiece near the top, an iron ring. Two explanations: a bell frame
   for a new bell; something to tie a goat to.
2. **The goat**, from January, led round the town on a red rope by the
   children every afternoon, house to house. It stops at the shop door every
   day and won't move until somebody comes out.
3. **The scale**, from the first Sunday in October, brought out of storage and
   set up in the church hall. Everyone weighed again, every Sunday, written
   in the book. MC is asked.
4. **Fasting.** Nobody eats on Fridays. The Gleaner shuts on Fridays. Mrs.
   Hollis stops bringing dishes.
5. **The countdown.** Nine weeks. Eight. Painted on the hall door, crossed
   out each Monday.

## Key day 31, Saturday February 5: the scapegoat

- **Morning:** Sunday's sermon on the radio again: the second goat, *"who
  goes out into the wilderness carrying all of it, and is never asked to come
  back."* The post on the tower, finished, against the sky.
- **Ezra** (kept faith, Ezra; `forks.md`): Daniel brings him in, an arm round
  his shoulders. Ezra's right hand in a cast to the elbow: *"An accident at
  the hall door."* He missed Thursday, the first in twenty years; he can't
  load his .22. **Load the shells for him** and keep his line in the ledger
  (`keep=ezra`), or **turn him away** (`betray=ezra`). Daniel watches which.
- **Mrs. Hollis**, thinner, fasting, asking MC to come and be weighed on
  Sunday. *"Everyone is. It's only to know."*
- **The goat** at the shop door at three. It won't move. Go out (it follows
  you back in and lies down by the stove; the town sees) or don't (it stands
  there till dark, and the children wait with it).
- **Night 31:** the radio counts weeks. Out of place: the coat on the shop
  door (if hung) with its buttons done up and the collar turned up, as if
  someone were inside it, keeping warm.

## Key day 32, Friday February 11: Ruth's lie (her door 3)

- **The counter:** fasting day; nobody comes but the goat. And at closing, a
  note from Ruth: *"Come after. Back door. I'm closed."*
- **The walked night at the Gleaner**, shut, the chairs up, Ruth drunk at a
  table with her mother's blue bowl and her father's scarf.
  - **The lie (door 3):** she said the 1989 Keeper ran. **She drove him to the
    highway herself**, thirty and in love with him, the night before breakup,
    and her mother, who'd gone to the shop to take his place at the table
    the town set, died that night in the empty shop. Every Keeper she keeps
    in town since is penance.
  - **If MC found the 1989 letter** (`letter_1989`): MC knows before she says
    it. MC can lay the letter on the table first (*"She says she'll drive
    me"*), and she reads it, and says the rest.
  - **If MC saw the locked room:** she knows MC was in it. *"You saw his
    coat. I couldn't give it back. I couldn't give it to the shop."*
  - **And the car keys** (`ruth_has_keys`): she has them. She puts them on the
    table between you.
  - **Forgive her** (`door=ruth`: her third door; she gives you the keys, or
    she keeps them, and you let her) or **don't** (she puts the keys back in
    her pocket; `betray=ruth` if MC tells the town).
- **Daniel** at the end of the bar in the dark the whole time. She didn't
  know he was there. He leaves without a word when she's finished.

## Key day 33, Saturday February 19: the duel

- **The counter:** the scale in the hall tomorrow; the goat; the countdown.
- **Olstad and Daniel** (`season.md`): he's in the customer chair when she
  comes in, with the baby in a sling under her coat. Very polite. He tells
  her, once, her sister's address in Wasilla, the street and the number, and
  says how pretty the street must be in summer. She tells him, once, that
  she's not leaving, and neither is the baby, and neither is he. He smiles
  and goes.
- **Then she asks MC** for the truth about the John night, for her case
  (kept faith, Olstad; `forks.md`): **tell her** (the motel, the parking
  lot, the apartment, whichever the background says; `keep=olstad`; she
  writes it all down, and it's what makes her look away at the town line in
  April) or **lie** (she'll know; `betray=olstad`).
- **Reads:** `will_testify` (she reminds you), `asked_daniel` (she has his
  "yes" now, and can't use it), `accused_on_air` (she heard the John part on
  the radio: she wants the rest from you), `pointed_gun` (he has your gun:
  she knows, and that's a crime she *can* use), the **deed** (give it to her:
  the congregation owns the shop, which is motive; `gave_deed`).

## Key day 34, Sunday February 20: the lights on the ice

- **The hall:** everyone weighed on the scale, written in the book. MC is
  asked (be weighed: `weighed`, or refuse: the eye).
- **The booth** (in the hall): Daniel asks MC, plainly, where the lights on
  the ice come from. The town wants the man with the lights.
  - **Kept faith, Sam** (`forks.md`): don't give him up (`keep=sam`; and at
    dusk, hide his notebooks before they come: the shop's safe, or the
    locked room, or the bear), or **tell** (`betray=sam`; the congregation
    goes to the cabin at dusk with lanterns, kindly).
- **Evening, if MC kept faith:** the cabin, Sam packing the wall into boxes,
  the notebooks into MC's arms. *"You'll keep them. You keep things."*
- **Night 34: the open back door.** Every door on the road open, all night,
  and the snow blowing into every house, and the town asleep in it. Including
  yours, which the rules say is always open. Something comes in. In the
  morning, footprints through the shop, round every shelf, up the stairs to
  the bedroom door, and back down. Not a dog's.

## Night walks in February

New finds, one a week:
- **Ruth's room, the dressing table drawer** (after the locked room): her
  diary from 1989, one entry: *"Tomorrow I'm driving him. Mom says she'll go
  and sit in the shop so it's not empty. Nobody will know till morning."*

- **The bench drawer** (back room): Abraham's taxidermy notebook. The bear:
  *"Second seam, Oct. '99. For the key. And for the other thing, if it
  comes to it."* What other thing.
