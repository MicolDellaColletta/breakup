# Breakup: Mood

What the game should feel like.

## Music

Playlist: [Breakup](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3xPzyrvfdoIvyIBPbBLC6d)

**The sound:** dark Americana and gothic folk. Murder ballads, banjo and fiddle
played like a threat, the devil as a character, hymns sung by the wrong people.
The playlist leans Southern and Appalachian; the game is in Alaska, so the
writing should take the *storytelling* from it (ballads where someone always
dies, a charming devil, a debt that gets collected) and put it in the snow.

### Songs that already point at the story

| Song | What it points at |
|---|---|
| *I'm Always Walking as Somebody Else* (American Murder Song) | MC living as John |
| *Everybody Knows My Name* (Harley Poe) | The town calling every Keeper by the name they arrived with |
| *Don't Come Knocking* (Holy Locust) | The office door, the knock at night |
| *Hypothermic* (Goodnight, Texas) | The cold, the drive |
| *In the Pines / Where Did You Sleep Last Night* (Caught a Ghost) | The woods behind the shop, shivering all night through |
| *Pretty Polly*, *Murder! Murder!*, *Pretty Lavinia* | Murder ballads: what Daniel is, set to music |
| *Crooked Man*, *What's a Devil to Do?*, *The Devil's Swing* | The polite devil. Daniel again. |
| *Bury Them Deep* (Ghoultown) | The freezer |
| *Miners Song*, *Yaga* (Clyde and the Milltailers) | Rural legend, the town's folklore layer |
