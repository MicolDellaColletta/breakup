# The art to make

A checklist of every placeholder drawn in code, to paint from (`todo.md`,
"A list of the art to make"). The style is in `art.md`: 90s pixel art, a
limited palette, and the shop like Strange Antiquities.

Each line is one painting. "(code)" names where its placeholder is drawn
today. Tick a line when its painting is in.
