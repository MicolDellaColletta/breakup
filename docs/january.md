# Breakup: January (plan)

Key days 24 to 26, from `season.md`. **Decided Oct 13: all seven leans,
and Daniel on the phone in red** (see "As built" at the end).

January is the hangover. The world didn't end, and the town has to decide
what that means. He decides for them: the Lord is waiting for the ice. From
now on everything points at one day in April, and the man who gave them the
date finally speaks to MC as himself. December's choices come due: the
Reverend's fate, the freezer (Ruth will find out, and who tells her
matters), the locked romance, John's books. And the town's eye, kept
quiet till now, starts to show on faces.

## Key day 24, Monday January 3: the Disappointment

A counter day.

**Morning.** The radio, live, before light: him, not hosting now, *preaching*.
*"The end didn't come because the Lord is waiting. For the ice. For the
offering. We'll wait with Him. Breakup, friends."* The calendar from New
Year's night, still on January, the one Saturday in April.
- What December left: the locked romance in a line (Ruth's hat from the
  party on your counter; Sam's notebook page through the door; the receiver
  you left off the hook, back on it in the morning, wiped clean); John's
  books on the passenger seat (`has_books`), or the locker lost.

**1. Mrs. Hollis**, with the candles taken down: *"He says April. I've
never minded waiting."* She brings the coat in its bag and hangs it on the
back of the shop door. *"It's yours to keep here. So it's handy."* (The coat
in the shop from now on; refusing to let her hang it: the eye.)

**2. Ruth, or the news of her.** The freezer comes due.
- If MC brought her (`ruth_saw_father`): she comes in to pay for nothing,
  stands at the counter, and talks about her father for the first time as
  dead. A kept faith stays kept; she's warmer and further away.
- If MC didn't: **Daniel has told her.** He never lies. She comes in with a
  face like a shut door: *"You knew. Since the twenty-first. He said you
  stood there with your hand on the lid."* **One chance:** tell her the truth
  of it now (*"I didn't know how"*: she stays, cold, faith not broken, no
  more kept) or lie (`betray=ruth`, closed for good).
  - Lean: this is the broken faith `forks.md` names ("let her find out from
    someone else"), softened by one January chance.

**3. Him.** At closing, in the last of the light, he comes in. The good
coat. No pretence now. He sits in the customer chair by the stove and
speaks to MC as himself for the first time, by name, MC's real one, once.
- **Paid here: the notebook.** He quotes a line MC wrote in November, word
  for word, in MC's own rhythm. Whichever `notebook_*` flags are set, the
  most intimate one wins ("There you are." over "He's here." over "The dog
  came up at three."). *"You underlined it twice."*
- Without the notebook: what MC did alone (the armchair facing the door; the
  radio left on all night; the stocking you didn't throw away).
- **Paid here, too:** the books (*"You went to Anchorage on New Year's Day.
  I'd have driven you."*); the locked romance (Ruth: *"She kissed you at
  midnight. Did she taste of sorry?"*; Sam: *"Did you hear the ice?"*;
  himself: *"You said my name."*).
- He wants nothing today. *"I wanted you to hear my voice in a room. That's
  all. It's different in a room."*

**Night 24.** No radio hymn numbers ever again; the radio is all him now.
**Out of place:** the Christmas cards from under the door, stood up on the
dresser in a row, all facing the bed.

## Key day 25, Saturday January 15: Walt comes back

A counter day, then a walked night (only if the Reverend is hidden).

**The bridge before** (Tue 4 – Fri 14): Olstad's baby, born on the 8th in
Wasilla, a girl; she's off the road a week, and the town notices who isn't
watching. The cruiser parked at her house. Things happen that week that
wouldn't have (a window broken at the manse; the shop's back step trodden
flat every morning).

**1. Walt** (kept faith, Walt; `forks.md`). Old, confused, in his good coat,
coming in out of the cold as if no time had passed since October.
- **If MC took his wife's name** (`ida_pawned`): *"I can't say her name.
  I've been trying since October. I'd like it back."* Give it back (the
  stub torn up: `keep=walt`; the town can say "Ida" again: in the bridge,
  someone does) or refuse (`betray=walt`).
- **If MC took the silver dollars** (pawned the first morning): he wants
  them back and can't pay the loan. Give them back for nothing (`keep=walt`,
  the warm ones, still warm), or the price (he can't; he goes; `betray=walt`
  only if refused hard).
- **If MC refused both in October:** he comes to say thank you and doesn't
  know what for.
- **Paid here:** Walt's silver dollars, finally (`review.md` 4.10).

**2. Cody's picks, or Cody's place.**
- If Cody's alive (`gave_picks_back`): he comes for nothing; he sits by the
  stove; he says the ice is three feet thick and something knocked under it
  where he was fishing, three times, like knuckles on a door.
- If MC kept the picks (`kept_picks`; Cody went through in October):
  Mrs. Hollis, for the picks, *"for his place at the table. He'll want them
  now. He'll be cold."* Give them (`keep=hollis`) or keep them.
- **Paid here:** keeping Cody's picks (`review.md` 4.7).

**3. The Reverend, if he's hidden** (`reverend_hidden`): January is when it
breaks. Ezra's been asking; the prints in the snow; the plate twice a day.
- **The walked night:** moving him. With Olstad's trust (`faith:olstad`),
  she drives him to Wasilla the night her baby's a week old, and says
  nothing, ever (`reverend_moved`). Without it, MC walks him out along the
  lake at night, and halfway, lanterns on the shore: the congregation,
  waiting, kind. They take him home to sit with. (`reverend_taken`: his
  collar in the next envelope, as if he'd stayed out of it after all, and
  the eye +2.)
- If warned: once, between hymns on the night radio, a voice from very far
  away says a number. Then nothing.
- If gone: nothing. That's the point.

**Night 25.** The radio: him, reading the names again. **Out of place:** the
coat on the back of the shop door, through the floor; at three, the sound of
someone trying it on.

## Key day 26, Sunday January 23: the confession

A walk-around day: the church, the queue, the booth.

**The church.** After the service, nobody leaves. Not barred doors this
time: a queue. The whole town, in their coats, along the side aisle, down
the vestry passage, to the booth. **He hears confessions.** The Reverend's
microphone is unplugged and coiled on the floor.

**The town's eye shows** (for the first time, as `season.md` planned):
- Walking down the aisle, who nods to MC and who doesn't: under 2, nearly
  everyone; 2–3, the old women and Mrs. Hollis; 4+, only Mrs. Hollis, and
  she does it looking at the floor. A line for each.
- Lean: the eye becomes visible here as faces, never as a number.

**The queue** (spots: people ahead of MC). Standing in it, MC overhears:
- Mrs. Hollis (she gave Ray's teeth; she's glad),
- Ezra (his hand on the booth door before he goes in; it's shaking),
- Ruth (if she's open to MC: she leaves the queue and comes to stand by him
  instead; if closed: she goes in, and comes out with her face washed).

**The booth: go in, or not.**
- **Go in.** Knee to knee again. He asks nothing. MC chooses what to give:
  - **a small true thing** (the coat, the candle, a lie told as John):
    he listens, and says it back to MC exactly, and that's all;
  - **the John night** (refused: *"Not yet. That one I'll want in
    March."*: groundwork for fork 6, "confess");
  - **nothing** (he waits, and the waiting is the confession).
- **Don't go in.** Walk out past the queue. The eye +1. He sees, through the
  little window. He doesn't mind. *"It'll keep."*
- **Paid here:** the teeth jar (Ezra: *"Every child. All but the shop's."*,
  if MC kept it back); speaking on the air (whoever MC said "no" to in
  December, in the queue, looking at him).

**Night 26.** The radio silent for an hour after midnight, for the first time
since October; then him, very quietly, saying goodnight to people by name,
one by one, the whole town. MC's name, last, or not at all.
