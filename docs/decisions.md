## October 12 — the review

**Structure** (`pillars.md`)

- The question is **"is it real?"**, answered three ways: *it's men* (crime
  thriller), *it's the town* (folk and religious horror), *it's you*
  (psychological). The cosmic is only the gap between them.
- The spine: **belief, and the abuse of belief.**
- **The rule of two explanations:** every uncanny thing has a human or
  psychological explanation. Less supernatural, more psychological and thriller.
- One romance per strand: **Daniel** (thriller), **Ruth** (folk), **Sam**
  (psychological).
- **The calendar:** week one day by day, then four or five key days a month
  (about 30 playable days).
- The town's religious spiral, with **the millennium as its hinge**: midnight
  on December 31, nothing happens, and Daniel gives them a new date: breakup.
- MC's mind and the town mirror each other: in October MC is the paranoid one;
  by February the town is, and MC's paranoia looks like sense.

**Daniel** (`daniel.md` v6)

- A hired hand, inspired by Landa and Chigurh: a loner, well spoken, scarily
  polite, armed, violent. Not tied to the shop; tied to the town, because he
  feeds on what it believes.
- **The preacher con stays, and comes sooner:** his voice reaches the night
  radio first (a caller in early November, a guest after he arrives in late
  November, the host from December). The radio takeover stays: it pushes the
  town into its frenzy.
- He arrives in November, still wounded; the wound helps the con.
- The Reverend falls in December (Daniel tells Ezra one true thing).
- He never speaks to MC until New Year's Eve.

**Olstad** (`pillars.md`, `story.md`)

- Good is not the same as nice. Unpleasant, **homophobic, subtly but clearly**,
  never excused.
- **She lives.** She's on the shore at breakup, against the town and Daniel.
  (Daniel shows her he could kill her, and doesn't.)
- She found Abraham in the freezer on October 4 and kept quiet: **a trap**
  (watch who shows up as "John"), **and fear more than belief** (of what the
  congregation does to people who get in the way).

**The shop, the town** (`story.md`, `forks.md`)

- **Pawned names are a custom, not magic.** The town refuses to say them.
- **Abraham walked into the freezer himself** on October 3, believing the unkept
  shop would take whoever held the keys.
- **Management's envelopes are the congregation's:** the collection plate pays
  the Keeper; Ezra delivers them every Tuesday.
- **Rule 1 means anyone can come in at night.** Keys matter less than the open
  back door.
- **The tin under 0527 (proposal):** a key and a slip of paper, leading to the
  books (a bus-depot locker in Anchorage, or a safe-deposit box).
- The lake "giving back the dead" is physical: bodies surface in the thaw.

**Forks and endings** (`forks.md`)

- Many soft forks (running totals), **six hard forks**, **six endings**.
- Totals: the voices, fray, rules broken, kept faith with, closeness, the town's
  eye.
- Hard forks: the John night (Oct 12), the tin (Halloween), the sixth bullet
  (late Nov), the Reverend (Dec), New Year's Eve, Daniel's price (March).
- Endings: The season ends (*The ice* as its variant), Run, Daniel (*into the
  ice together* as its variant), Break the cycle (Ruth), The truth (Sam), Thaw.
- **Thaw is back** as the brighter ending (it had fallen out of the pillars by
  mistake). "The new owner" is cut.
- **Closeness: three doors** per romance (a true thing told, their secret
  shared, their lie forgiven); two by New Year's Eve to be available.
- **Kept faith: a count;** Thaw needs four of six. Open betrayal closes a person.
- **The town's eye:** strangeness, seen by watchers (Ezra, Olstad, the bar, the
  radio); Daniel multiplies it from December.

**The romances** (`forks.md`, "The romances, door by door")

- **None is simply good; all are deeply problematic.** Sam is complete madness
  (conspiracy, folie à deux); Daniel is wanting to be broken and rebuilt (the
  darkest); Ruth is complicity and desperate attachment.
- Ruth drove the 1989 Keeper to the highway herself; this winter she keeps MC's
  car keys. Her cellar holds a second Polaroid of every Keeper, for the church.
- Sam's list of the dead is started in October; his conspiracy wall; MC catches
  him moving the clock's hands (faked, or "kept honest": never said).
- Daniel's one lie, the only one in the game: *"I knew"* why he let MC go. Face
  to face he's a polite stranger from November; he never calls MC or speaks of
  the John night until New Year's Eve.
- "The truth" (Sam) is now folie à deux, not the rational answer.
- One kept-faith moment each for Walt, the Hollises, Ezra, Ruth, Sam, Olstad.

## October 7

- **Thaw is the game's brighter ending.** (`story.md`, "Thaw")
- **The themes:** emptiness, loneliness, mental illness written with care,
  guilt, fear, desire, not being able to communicate, meaning, self-destruction,
  rebuilding through suffering. Comforts and unsettling things once or twice a
  week. (`themes.md`)
- **The voices:** Paranoia (the main one), John, Appraisal, Warmth, Animal, and
  ???. Choices lean by tone; colored choices; Paranoia talks over the others;
  frayed is felt, not shown; the background settles at the end of the first
  week. (`voices.md`)
- **The story bible:** "John" is an alias; MC drives a dead man's rental with
  his gun; the owner Abraham is dead in the freezer; the Keeper every winter;
  Daniel; Sam (Dr. Samuel Wakeman, hydrologist); Ruth (the owner's daughter,
  the Gleaner); the Reverend Elias (the radio helper); ticket 0527 (Daniel
  5:27); subtle biblical symbols; MC male, never said outright. (`story.md`)
- **Season history:** 1958, 1989, Sam's notice in 1993, the Run ending.
  (`story.md`, "Timeline")
- Sgt. Marit Olstad, Upper Midwest, pregnant, the back-door key. (`story.md`)
