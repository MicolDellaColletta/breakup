## Halloween: where the tin goes (proposal)

The open question from weeks two and three. **I'd put the tin in the double
hour, on Saturday night**, not on Halloween itself:

- **The hour that happens twice.** 1:00 to 2:00, and then 1:00 to 2:00 again.
  Time that doesn't count. The call comes at 1:40, and then, an hour later
  that's the same hour, at 1:40 again (`daniel.md`, 6a). **Between the two
  calls, MC decides about the tin.** The fork lives in a gap in the calendar.
- **Halloween becomes the consequences:** what MC found (or didn't), the
  rental reported stolen, Olstad and the plate, the children at the door. A
  day of being looked at.
- It keeps Sunday's church short (a card), and gives Halloween its own shape.

Alternative: the tin on Halloween night, after the children. Simpler, but the
double hour then only carries the calls.

## Key day 11: Saturday, October 30. The rental is due

### Morning

- The car in the lot, under a foot of snow. MC brushes it off, or doesn't.
  The Northern Lights sticker on the bumper.
- The second flash detail, if the background is settled: one more piece of
  the John night (the car, per background: what soaked the rental agreement,
  `story.md` open question; draft: rain for witness and debt, a spilled
  thermos for guilt, nobody knows for insanity).

### The counter (Saturday)

1. **Olstad.** In uniform again; the road's open. Polite, cold. *"Today's the
   day, Johnny. Northern Lights opens Monday. I'd say they file Monday
   afternoon."* She doesn't say what she'll do. She asks one question:
   *"Who's John?"* Not *who are you*. Who's John.
   - The trap she's been holding since October 4 (`forks.md`): she found the
     carbon with John's name, and Abraham. She's waiting to see who shows up
     as John, and what they do when it closes in.
   - MC can answer (a lie, a half-truth, nothing). Kept faith with Olstad
     leans on this, but the real moment is February.
2. **Mrs. Hollis.** Not a dish. A tape measure. *"For the shoulders. They're
   nearly done."* She measures MC across the back, right there at the counter,
   her hands warm through MC's shirt. She doesn't ask. (Rule? None.)
3. **Sam** (if MC knelt with him on the ice, or his door is open): he's worked
   out the trains. *"Your caller's on the railroad. Two trains, opposite ways,
   twelve minutes apart: that's the Wasilla siding. He's an hour from here.
   Less, on a good road."* Folie à deux, a step: he's right, and how could he
   be.
4. **Ruth** (if faith with her holds): a bag of candy for tonight's
   trick-or-treaters, because *"Abraham always ran out by six."* She looks at
   MC's car out the window a long moment. (Seed: November, she'll take the
   keys.)

### Evening

- The map, Saturday. The Gleaner's Halloween-eve party (costumes; Olstad
  doesn't come); the church, dark; Sam's cabin. A quiet evening before the
  night.

### Night eleven: the double hour (hard fork 2)

- The rules, the radio (the Reverend: *"Turn your clocks back tonight,
  friends. The Lord gives us an hour. Use it well."*).
- **1:40: the first call.** The room, the clock, the breathing.
- **2:00, and it's 1:00 again.** The clock on the stove jumps back. The hour
  starts over.
- **The tin.** In the hour that doesn't count, MC goes down to the back shelf.
  The tin box, tag 0527, a padlock no bigger than a thumbnail. MC has the
  ticket.
  - **Open it.** With what: the claw of a hammer from under the counter, or
    the redemption, done properly (MC writes it out of the ledger to
    themselves, as the ticket's holder: *"Tin box, redeemed. Ticket 0527."*),
    then the hammer anyway. Inside: **a small key, numbered, and a slip of
    paper**, a locker at the Anchorage bus depot (`story.md`, proposal).
    (`tin_opened`)
  - **Leave it shut.** Put it back, exactly. Turn the tag to the wall.
    (`tin_shut`)
  - **Run.** Now, in the hour nobody will count: the car, the road, the
    dark. At the town line, Olstad's cruiser, headlights on, pointing back at
    town. She doesn't stop MC. She doesn't need to: the pass is closing again,
    a white wall. MC turns around. It costs her trust (`tried_to_run`,
    `forks.md`: kept faith with Olstad closes a little).
- **1:40 again: the second call.** In it, his clock chimes: it fell back too.
  And once, very quietly, he laughs (`daniel.md`). If MC opened the tin, the
  laugh comes the moment after the lid. Coincidence. (Two explanations: he
  can't know; or he can.)

## Key day 12: Sunday, October 31. Halloween

A walk-around day, like the Sundays, ending in the dark an hour early.

- **Morning card** (a bridge line inside the day): church, the hands, the
  tooth if refused; lunch.
- **Afternoon: Olstad at the door**, in the cruiser, engine running. *"Northern
  Lights called it in this morning. Didn't wait for Monday. Stolen vehicle.
  Blue sedan. Dim headlight."* She looks at the car in the lot. *"I'm the
  only one who's read it. For now."* She doesn't take the keys. She doesn't
  arrest anyone. She's watching what MC does with a closing door.
  - If MC tried to run last night: *"You'd have hit the wall at the pass
    anyway. I was saving you the trip."*
- **16:55: dark.** An hour earlier than yesterday. All at once.
- **Trick-or-treat.** Children at the shop door in the dark. Every one of
  them dressed as the Keeper: brown blankets, brown coats cut from old ones,
  paper bags. Little Keepers, a dozen of them, holding out pillowcases.
  They knock twice on the frame. They say the rhyme instead of "trick or
  treat". *"...and nine's the Keeper, in he goes."* Then they wait for candy.
  - Give them candy (Ruth's bag, if she brought it). They say thank you,
    politely, and go on to the next house.
  - Don't open the door. They stand there anyway, knocking twice, until
    their parents call them. In the morning, on the step, a little paper
    coat, cut out and crayoned brown.
- **Night twelve:** Olstad's cruiser at the end of the road all night,
  headlights on the shop (`daniel.md`, the duel: "the way they were on
  Halloween"). The dog. No call: he's had his two.
  - **If the tin's open:** the key and the slip, under the mattress, in the
    dresser, or in MC's pocket. MC can't sleep. Anchorage is three hours
    away, and the road's open, and Olstad's at the end of it.
