# Breakup: Layout

Reference for where everything is, so scenes stay consistent with each other.
Positions on the maps are rough; what's next to what matters more than exact sizes.

## Setting

- A pawn shop alone on a road in the woods, Alaska, October 1999.
  The nearest town (Kettle Lake) is somewhere down the road. No neighbors.
- Snow, dark trees on every side, one road out front.
- The neon sign over the door reads **PAWN, GUNS, FURS, TAXIDERMY**, with one letter dead.
  At night it is the only light outside.
- "Breakup" is when the ice breaks on the lake in spring. The letter says the shop is
  yours "until breakup". The calendar upstairs has a date circled at the end of April.

|                                            |
+---[window]-------[ DOOR ]-------[window]---+
       PAWN  GUNS  FURS  TAXIDERMY  (neon)
```

### Shop floor (front)
- **Front door** in the middle, with a **bell** above it. Glass **windows on both sides**.
- The neon light comes through the windows and reaches only a few feet inside.
- **Low glass display cases and freestanding racks**, not tall shelving, so the counter
  can always see the front windows (needed for rule 8 and for customers arriving).
- **Fur coats on racks along the side walls.** In the dark they look like people standing.
- Guns and antlers on the walls. The **stuffed bear** stands in a front corner.
- Items out front are **for sale**.

### Counter (back of the shop floor)
- Faces the front door and windows. This is where the player spends most of the game.
- On top: the **landline phone** (black, heavy, old), the **register** (drawer open, empty),
  the **ledger**.
- **Drawers** in the counter, plus **hidden drawers** to find later.

### Behind the counter
- **Shelves of pawned items.** These were left as security for a loan and are *held*, not sold.
  Take / leave / use gameplay happens here and at the counter.
- **Office door** on one side. **Hallway** opening on the other side.

### Office
- Small. Wooden desk, office chair, a bare bulb on a cord. The only lit room on the first night.
- A smell nobody names.
- First night: the letter (envelope "FOR THE KEEPER") is on the desk.

### Hallway
- **Stairs** up to the apartment, running along the hallway. The bottom step is at the back,
  next to the metal door; they climb toward the front of the building.
  (So waking "at the bottom of the stairs, one hand on the heavy door" works.)
- **Fuse box** (idea): explains why the shop lights are off when the neon, the bulb and the
  freezer all have power, and lets the lights fail later.
- **Heavy metal door** at the far end, leading to the back room.

### Back room / storage
- The **freezer**. Its compressor is the hum you hear everywhere in the shop.
- The **back door**. Rule 1 says to leave it unlocked, so checking it every night means
  walking past the freezer.

     the road and the woods

  :  door      #  locked door
```

Simple and a little too lived-in: someone was here recently (wet toothbrush,
a coat in your size).

### How the two floors line up
Both maps have the front of the building at the bottom and the same column split.

| Upstairs | is directly above |
|---|---|
| Main room (front, windows) | Shop floor and counter |
| Bathroom + hall | Office |
| Stairwell | Shop hallway |
| Bedroom + locked room (back) | Back room (freezer, back door) |

The stairs climb from the back of the shop hallway toward the front, so you arrive
at a **landing at the front of the stairwell** that opens straight into the main room.

### Main room
- The **stairs** come up from the shop hallway onto a landing that opens into this room.
- **Coat hook** by the landing (the coat, with the Polaroid in its pocket).
- **Living area:** an armchair, an old **TV**, the **radio** (amber dial; rule 7 says to
  keep it on at night).
- **Kitchen corner:** small stove, sink, a table, the **calendar** on the wall.
- **Windows at the front, over the shop entrance.** They look down on the neon sign,
  the snowy lot, your car, the road and the tree line. The neon tints the ceiling red at night.
  Rule 1 says to close them before dark.

### Hall
- A short hall from the main room toward the back. Bathroom and bedroom doors on the left,
  the locked door at the end on the right.

### Bedroom
- At the back, over the back room. Bed, a dresser. Cold sheets that smell of someone
  else's soap. At night you can hear the freezer through the floor.

### Bathroom
- Small. Sink with a toothbrush, the **mirror** with the silver going black at the edges.

### The locked door
- At the end of the hall. The room behind it is directly **above the back room and the
  freezer**. Locked on the first night.
- What's behind it, and where the key is, are still open (see below).

## Open questions

- **What's behind the locked door?** The previous keeper's room? A second staircase down
  to the back room? Something that should only be opened at breakup?
- **Where is the key?** Ideas: on the pawned shelves as someone's collateral; in a hidden
  counter drawer; on the coat, found much later.
- **What does the TV show?** Only static and the one local channel? The same voice as the radio?
- **Is there a way down from the apartment besides the stairs?** The locked room sits right
  above the back room, so a hatch in its floor down to the freezer would fit. That matters
  for the "wake standing at the bottom of the stairs" ending.
