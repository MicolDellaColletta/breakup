## KD35, Sat Mar 11: the frenzy

**Morning:** a procession at dawn past the shop, with candles in daylight.
The children carry the goat's red rope. The post on the tower has a figure
tied to it now: a straw man in a coat. **Whose coat:**
- the 1989 Keeper's (from Ruth's room), if MC never took the room key;
- MC's own spare coat, if MC refused the scale.

**At the counter.** Almost nobody comes. Notes come under the door instead,
read according to the town's eye:
- **low eye:** "We're praying for you, Keeper."
- **high eye:** "We know what you are."
- **very high eye:** only a number, the weeks left.

The visitors:

- **Mrs. Hollis** brings the weigh-in book's page with MC's April number on
  it. She's proud of him.
  - **If MC refused the scale:** she brings the scale's brass weight instead,
    and leaves it on the glass without a word.
  - Pays off `weighed` / `refused_scale`.
- **The search for Sam's notebooks.** Ezra and four men, polite, with a
  warrant from no court: *"Pastor says there are papers."* What they find
  depends on where MC hid them:

  | Hidden in | What happens |
  |---|---|
  | The safe | Safe: they don't know the numbers. Ezra looks at the safe a long time. |
  | The bear | They don't touch the bear (the bear is the Keeper's). Safe, but the bear's seam is now a known place. |
  | Ruth's room | Ruth stands in the hall with the key in her fist and won't open it. She pays for it later (her eye drops), but the notebooks are safe. |
  | Under the bed | Found. Burned on the step. Sam doesn't speak to MC again unless forgiven at KD38. |

  If Sam was given up, there's no search, because they already have him.
- **Whoever hurt MC disappears** (v7 step 5). Next morning, exact change on
  the kitchen table.
  - **Who:** Ezra, if MC turned him away in February, or if Ezra ever called
    MC the goat to his face, which he did (day 11). **Otherwise Dr. Brekke**,
    who signed the lie about the fire.
  - **How:** never shown. Only an empty pew on Sunday, and the town saying
    "gone to his sister's".
  - **If v7 call 5 goes the other way:** cut this beat.

**The night:** the procession passes again at midnight, singing, and stops
outside the shop. Nobody knocks. **The rule of the night:** the back door
must be open. Lock it, and in the morning there are candle stubs set along
the threshold, lit, in daylight.

## KD36, Sun Mar 19: his room

**The service:** the anxious bench (v7 call 2). He calls up Mrs. Lund for
her son's drinking, by name, from his files. Then he looks down the hall at
MC and doesn't call him: *"Not yet. He's not ready. I'll know."*

**Then the walk-around:** while he's still in the hall with the queue, his
room above the Gleaner is unlocked, because nobody locks anything now. Ruth
says so, if her door is open: *"He's at the hall till four. I clean on
Sundays."*

**What's in the room:**

- **The suitcase:** the collection (rings, deeds, savings books, Mrs.
  Hollis's husband's ring), and the church's deed if MC didn't give it to
  Olstad.
- **His files:** a notebook per person, in his hand, with MC's page last.
  - **What MC's page says:** a date, *"July 1988,"* and nothing else.
  - **What it's for:** taking a file opens one of the three ways to beat him
    in April (showing the town the con).
- **The cigar box:** one keepsake per town. A photograph of a boy of
  seventeen, wet, on a lake shore. An empty slot, labelled in pencil:
  *Kettle Lake.*
- **John's gun**, in the drawer, if he took it in January. **Lean:** leave it
  there. He gives it back at KD37, in person; taking it now steals his scene.

The choices are what to take: a file, the photograph, the ring for Mrs.
Hollis, or nothing. Each is a flag. Taking anything raises the eye if Ruth
saw.

**That night, the first piece of the flashback** (v7 call 6, A, and call 10):
- **The scene:** summer 1988. The tent, the collection, Brother Abel
  laughing. The girl at the baptism, and MC watching from the shore.
- **How it plays:** in its own look, the only warm colour in the game. MC's
  name drops out of the sound.
- **If v7 call 6 goes the other way:** this night plays the John night from
  his side instead (option B).

## KD37, Sat Mar 25: his price (hard fork 6)

**At the counter,** in the morning, he redeems a ticket: **the sixth
bullet**, which he pawned on Nov 23 (if MC accepted it). He pays the loan and
the interest, exact to the cent. Then he takes out John's gun and loads the
bullet into the empty chamber, on the glass, in front of MC. *"Six. Now it's
a whole thing. I said March."*
- **If MC refused the bullet in November,** he has it anyway. *"I kept it.
  You didn't want it, so I kept it."*
- **If MC pulled the trigger at the face-off** (`pulled_trigger`): *"You
  pulled it. It was empty. I counted, that night, after you'd gone. I
  laughed for an hour."*
- **The gun is MC's again,** loaded with six.

**After closing, the counsel room** (the private room behind the hall), with
`red=alone`. His price, named, because he never lies. **v7 call 8:** not the
books. *"Come with me. After breakup. Wherever I go next."* The books are a
joke between them; he slides the crew's ledgers across to MC: *"Burn them.
I never cared."*

- **Say yes** → the Daniel ending opens.
  - Needs `doors:daniel>=2`. Without it, he laughs: *"You don't mean it yet."*
- **Confess** → the 1988 lake, from MC's side, told to him.
  - It's the thing he can't price. It also opens the Daniel ending, darker
    (the shore variants).
- **Refuse** → he's delighted. *"Good. Then it's a race."*
  - This opens beating him (April: Olstad, the town, the gun).

**His one lie** (door 3) comes on any choice, at the door, as MC leaves: *"I
said I didn't know why I let you go. I knew."* **The flashback's second
piece** plays that night: MC in the water, closer, Abel's hand on MC's
shoulder.

**Pays off:**
- `lied_to_daniel` / `told_daniel_hidden`: he quotes MC's December answer
  back.
- `wont_promise`: Olstad isn't there to hear any of it.

**If v7 call 8 goes the other way** (the price is the books): pay (the tin,
the locker), confess, or refuse, as in `season.md`.

## KD38, Fri Mar 31: Sam's lie (door 3)

**If Sam was kept:** a walked evening on the ice, at the tripod. MC catches
him moving the clock's hands: *"Setting it back to the true minute."* Ten
years of notebooks, kept by a man who's been moving the clock.
- **Forgive him** → `door=sam`.
- **Don't** → the truth ending closes.
- **If his notebooks were burned** (under the bed), he doesn't come out to
  meet MC unless MC brings something of his. The notebook from the bench
  drawer counts.

**If Sam was given up:** the walked evening is the hall's cellar, where they
keep him, fed and kind and locked in. **Lean:** MC can't get him out (that's
April), only talk through the hatch. He's calm, and he's lost the minute.
*"What time is it, John? Nobody here will tell me."*
