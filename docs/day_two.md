# Breakup: Day two

**Thursday, October 7, 1999.** Sunrise 8:18, sunset 19:08 (three minutes less
light than yesterday). Draft for discussion, same as `day_one.md` was.

Day one taught the counter. Day two is where **day one's choices come back**,
and where the shop starts showing what it is: the dog, the back door, and the
first name brought in to pawn.

watch** on the night radio. Thursday nights are the Reverend's. The helper voice
is clearest tonight.

## Customers

Four, plus Ruth. Order: Hollis → Ezra → Walt → Ruth.

### 1. The Hollises (branches on Cody)

**If MC sold Cody the ice picks:** Cody comes in at opening. Soaked to the waist,
lips blue, alive. He went through at the point and pulled himself out with
the picks.

- He saw something under the ice, a yard down, with its face turned up. He
  won't say it was Ray. He says *"it had his jacket"*.
- He wants to **pawn the picks**: *"I don't want them in the house."* A small
  loan, the next pawn tag. Takes them off the floor, onto the back shelf.
- Refusing to take them: he leaves them on the counter anyway.
- Layer 2 seed: the lake gives back the dead in May. Cody will be waiting.

**If MC didn't sell him the picks:** Mrs. Hollis comes in instead.

- *"Cody didn't come home. His truck's at the point."*
- She doesn't want anything. She wants MC to **write it in the book**: *"You
  write everything in there. Write that he went out."* A ledger line with no
  item and no price. **That breaks rule 3** (every item gets a price): MC
  decides whether to price it.
- Two Hollis men under the ice. The town doesn't search. (Cody said so.)

### 2. Ezra Lund: "the usual"

From the earlier ideas list: **someone who knew the old keeper.**

- Seventies. Old family, church elder, the Thursday watch volunteer. Doesn't
  look at MC, looks at the counter where Abraham used to stand.
- *"The usual."* Nothing else.
- The ledger has it: every Thursday back to 1997, *"E. Lund. .22 shells, one
  box, $6."* (Add these lines to the ledger.) The box is in the counter drawer.
- **Found it:** Ezra relaxes a fraction. *"Abraham kept them in the drawer too."*
  Leans John. Then: *"You'll come up Sunday. We pray for the Keeper."*
  (The Reverend told MC to be busy. Choice.)
- **Didn't:** *"Abraham would have known."* He leaves, and this is the first
  person who'll tell Daniel, in January, that the Keeper didn't know the shop.
- Ezra is who the congregation sounds like before Daniel gives it a voice.

### 3. Walt Pruitt: pawning a name (rule 6)

Walt comes back. Slower. He doesn't look at the shelves.

- If MC took the coins yesterday: *"The silver didn't help. I thought it would."*
- If MC refused: *"You were right not to take those. They were hers. So's this."*
- *"I'd like to pawn a name. Hers. **Ida.**"*
  *"Forty-one years I've said it. I'd like to stop."*
- He wants no money. *"Just take it. Write me a ticket."*

| Choice | What happens | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Refuse, kindly | Rule 6 kept. Walt nods: *"She'd have said the same."* He says "Ida" once more at the door, like trying it. | Warmth |
| Refuse, hard | Rule 6 broken ("be polite"). *"Then I'll carry it."* | Animal |
| Accept | **break=name.** The next pawn tag: *"One name."* MC writes it. Walt stops in the doorway and can't remember why he came in. From now on **nobody in Kettle Lake can say "Ida"**. | ??? |

After accepting, the ledger line reads *"One name. ___."* The ink is there; MC
can't read it either. A strong Appraisal can: *"Ida. You wrote Ida."*

### 4. Ruth (not a customer)

Afternoon. First time in the shop since her father "went away". She stops at the
door before she comes in, the way you stop at a grave.

- She brings **the mail** that piled up at the post office for the shop. In
  it: a window envelope from **Northern Lights Auto Rental, Anchorage,**
  addressed to *"J. —, c/o Kettle Lake Pawn"*. (The rental clock. John gave the
  shop as his address. Opening it shows the return date: blank. A note: *"please
  call to confirm return."*)
- She asks to **go upstairs**. *"I left something up there. A long time ago."*
  - Let her: she goes up alone. MC hears the locked door open, and close, and
    lock. She comes down with nothing in her hands and her eyes wet.
  - Don't: *"It's my father's building."* *"It's my building this winter."*
    She goes. She's made a decision about you.
- On her key ring, a small brass key with a **pink plastic tag**, a child's.
  Appraisal: *"That's the one."* Warmth: *"She's carrying her childhood around
  on a key ring."*
- If MC fed the dog, she sees the bowl when she passes the stairs (in the
  "let her" branch, upstairs). She stops. *"Where did you find that?"* Nothing
  else. It's the most frightened she ever looks.

## Night: the Thursday watch

- **Radio:** on Thursday the helper voice is clear for a whole minute, the
  longest so far. A rule, read as a hymn number. If MC was at the church, the
  voice adds *"...and thank you to our visitor this evening."*
- **The dog:** if fed tonight, MC lies in bed and **hears it come up the
  stairs.** Slow. One step at a time, with a rest between, the way an old man
  climbs. The bowl scrapes. Then the stairs again, going down.
- **The phone (rule 4).** 23:00, the shop phone rings downstairs. If MC answers
  and doesn't speak first:
  - If MC accepted Ida's name: an old woman's voice, a neighbour, asking for
    Walt: *"Is he there? He's not home. I'm calling about his wife, about...
    about..."* She can't find the name. She hangs up crying.
  - If not: the open line again, a clock ticking in the room. (Daniel. He says
    nothing until New Year's Eve.)
