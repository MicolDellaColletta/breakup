# Breakup: night walks, the gun, and facing him (plan)

Three things asked for together: nights that are worth exploring, the gun as
something MC puts somewhere (and that matters later), and one chance to
confront Daniel. **Decided Oct 13: all five leans. Built** (see "As built"
at the end).

## What's there now

- **Nights** are the four rules, bed, and one thing out of place
  (`nights.md`). The shop downstairs only opens on a few early nights (the
  phone on nights 2-10, the water on night 3). The office, the back room and
  the freezer are explorable after closing by day, never at night.
- **The gun:** night 7 asks, at the kitchen table, what to do with John's
  revolver (pillow, dresser, snowbank, try to load it, sit with it). It's a
  menu at bedtime, so it's easy to miss, and nothing afterwards ever asks
  where it is.
- **Daniel:** MC can break his pretence once ("It came out of you", day 15)
  and stay or walk out in his booth. MC never goes *at* him.

## 1. Night walks: the shop is yours after dark

**The idea:** every night from now on, after the rules, the stairs go down.
The shop at night is a place with secrets, and each night has **one new
thing to find** somewhere in it, never announced. Skipping it costs nothing;
finding them builds a second story under the first.

**The rooms at night** (reusing the shop's rooms, drawn dark, lit by the
neon through the front window and a torch MC carries):
- **The shop floor:** the shelves, the bear, the counter, the drawer (a false
  bottom), the register.
- **The office:** Abraham's desk (a locked drawer), the calendar, the safe
  (a combination nobody's told MC).
- **The back room:** the bench, the crates, the furnace, the freezer.
- **The hall upstairs:** **the locked door** (Ruth's old room, the light
  under it on night 6).

**The finds**, one or two a week, from December on (and some from the
earlier nights' rooms, backdated where the story allows):

| What | Where | Opens with | What it says |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The counter drawer's false bottom** | Shop floor | The loupe (a seam in the wood) | Abraham's own rules, an older list. Rule 9: *"If he comes back, give him what he asks for."* |
| **The desk drawer's key** | Taped under the register | Looking | Opens the desk |
| **Abraham's desk** | Office | The key | Letters from previous Keepers, never posted. 1989's, unfinished: *"Ruth says she'll drive me. If she does—"* |
| **The safe** | Office | The combination: the hymn numbers from the Reverend's last Thursday (on Sam's wall, or the bulletin) | The church's deed to the shop. The shop has always been the congregation's. And 0527's first ticket, 1989 |
| **A crate** | Back room | A crowbar from the bench | Stock that was never shelved: a Keeper's suitcase, initials J.W., 1989; a brown-eyed photograph |
| **The furnace ash pan** | Back room | The poker | Burned paper; a corner survives: Thomas's handwriting |
| **The locked door's key** | In Abraham's coat pocket, in the freezer | Opening the freezer (Dec 21) | Opens the locked door |
| **The locked room** | Upstairs | The key (January) | Ruth's room as it was in 1989: a girl's bed, the 1989 Keeper's coat on the back of the door, and his name on the wall, hundreds of times, in her hand |

- Lean: **finds are documents and objects** (readable in your pockets, like the
  letter; objects with properties, like the shop's stock), and some open
  choices later (the deed for Olstad's case; the 1989 letter for Ruth's lie
  in February; the rules list for Daniel's price).
- **The rule of two explanations holds:** Abraham's rule 9 could be a frightened
  old man's, or advice.
- **Cost:** walking the shop at night is quiet unless the town's eye is high
  (someone sees the torch in the window: `eye=+1`, once a week at most), and
  it uses the night's time: the dog comes at three either way.

## 2. The gun: something you put somewhere

**The idea:** the revolver stops being a bedtime menu and becomes a thing in
MC's hands on night 7 that MC carries to a place in the apartment or the shop
and leaves there. Where it is decides who finds it, and whether MC can reach
it when it matters.

- **Night 7, rebuilt:** MC brings it in from the car (as now) and holds it
  (`has:revolver`). The hiding places are **spots** the gun can be used on
  (the use-items mechanic that already exists): under the pillow, the back
  of the dresser, the snowbank by the back door, **inside the bear's seam,
  behind the furnace, in the freezer's shadow, under the floorboard in the
  hall** (a loose one, found that night). Or keep it on you.
- **Where it is, later:**

| Place | Who finds it | Can MC reach it at the confrontation? |
|---|---|---|
| On you / under the pillow | Nobody, but Mrs. Hollis, changing the sheets after your burned hands (Dec 17), feels it, and says nothing | Yes |
| The dresser | Daniel knows (he's read the notebook in the same drawer, Jan 3: *"Under the flannel, with your papers"*) | Yes |
| The snowbank | The thaw, in April | No |
| The bear | Whoever opens the second seam for the freezer key (Dec 21): MC, or Olstad | Yes, if MC opened it |
| Behind the furnace | Nobody. It gets hot. One night it goes off by itself (a crack in the dark; the sixth chamber) | Yes |
| The floorboard | Nobody, ever | Yes |

- **Move it** later: any night walk, MC can take it from where it is and put
  it somewhere else (once a week).
- Lean: **yes, rebuild night 7 this way**, and keep the old choices' moods as
  the lines each place plays.

## 3. Facing him: one chance

**The idea:** once, MC goes *at* Daniel. Not a fork with a right answer: the
one time MC stops being the goat and acts. It costs something whatever MC
does.

- **When:** a night walk, any night **between the ruling (Dec 17) and the
  confession (Jan 23)**: a door that's open for five weeks. On those nights a
  spot appears on the road outside the shop: *the Gleaner's back window, lit
  red*. Go to it once. After Jan 23, it's gone; he's too surrounded.
- **The room:** the Gleaner's back room, the transmitter, the teeth in their
  jars on the shelves, him at the microphone, off air, alone.
- **What MC can do** (one of):
  - **Ask him.** *"Did you bolt the door?"* He never lies: *"Yes."* That's all.
    Nobody else will ever hear him say it. (`asked_daniel`: MC knows, and he
    knows MC knows; if MC tells Olstad, she believes it and can't use it.)
  - **Accuse him, on air.** Switch the transmitter on and say it into the
    microphone, to every kitchen in town. He doesn't stop you. He listens,
    and then, gently, on air, tells the town about John, and the motel, and
    whose gun it was. (`accused_on_air`: the eye goes as high as it goes;
    Olstad hears it; February starts early.)
  - **The gun** (only if MC can reach it, per the table): point it at him. He
    stands up, and steps closer, until it's against his coat. *"There you
    are."* The empty chamber is the next one; it clicks. Or MC lowers it.
    Either way he takes it from MC's hand, gently, and puts it in his coat.
    (`pointed_gun`: the gun's his now, until March.)
  - **Walk away** from the window without going in. (No flag; the door stays
    open till Jan 23.)
- **What it changes:** his January visit (Jan 3) and the confession (Jan 23)
  read it; Olstad's evening at the ruins reads it; fork 6 in March reads it
  (if he has the gun, his price is different).
