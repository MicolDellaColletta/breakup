## Week three's job

- **The lake freezes shore to shore.** The season turns: the ice that's been
  "two inches, wouldn't hold a dog" since day one is suddenly a floor. The
  town walks out onto it, for the first time, together.
- **The first road closure.** Overnight Friday the pass drifts in; the road
  out is shut till Sunday. MC learns what "the roads close" means: for a
  night, there's no out at all (`story.md`, "If MC drives away").
- **Something under the ice** (proposal, below): the coat answers a question
  the player hasn't asked yet: what happens to it.
- **The thriller:** the caller's room gets a sound outside its window. A
  train. The Alaska Railroad runs through Wasilla: the first clue to where he
  is (`daniel.md`: the November money order is postmarked Wasilla).
- **Faith and the romances:** the collision from week two plays out. Whoever
  MC kept faith with is at the lake with MC; whoever MC broke it with is there
  and doesn't come near.

## Under the ice (proposal)

New ice is black and clear, the first few days; you can see down through it
like a window. On key day 10, out past the bonfire, **a shape under the ice,
face up, a yard down: a figure in a long brown coat.**

- **The town's answer (never said aloud):** Thomas. Last winter's Keeper. "Coat
  not returned" (the vestry book, week two). The lake gives back the dead in
  May; until then, sometimes, in the first clear week, you can see them.
- **The two explanations:** a waterlogged tarp, a log with weed on it, the
  bonfire's light on black ice and a mind that's been shown a brown coat
  every day for a week. Or a body, in cold water, which keeps (a human answer
  too: bodies in near-freezing water don't rot; they wait).
- **Nobody looks down.** The whole town skates over it. Children play the
  rhyme in a ring right on top of it. That's the *Horses* register: the
  horror is that it's normal.
- **What MC can do** (below): skate over it like everyone, show Olstad, kneel
  with Sam, or leave.
- It pays off in May, and in every ending that has a shore.

## Bridge: Monday 18 to Friday 22

Extends the built Monday bridge (`bridges.txt`, `bridge_monday`): its last

- **"WEDNESDAY. THE KEEPER IS PAID."** The envelope. In it, with the money,
  a strip of brown wool, two inches long, as if snipped off a hem. No note.
- **"THURSDAY. THE CIRCLE SEWS."** Ezra's shells (if the ritual held: one
  line; if MC refused the tooth: he buys them without a word). The Lunds'
  windows lit all evening. The call: footsteps in the room now, slow, and a
  cane, or a stick, on a wooden floor. He's up.
- **"FRIDAY. THE ROAD CLOSES."** Snow all day. At four, the radio: the pass is
  closed till Sunday. Kettle Lake is an island. MC stands at the window and
  understands that for two days there's no out, not even the bad one.

## Key day 10: Saturday, October 23. The ice

### Morning

- Thirty below in the night. The pipes upstairs frozen again; the kettle on
  the shop's hot plate.
- From the front window: the lake, white to the far shore. No open water
  anywhere. A line of people already walking out on it with augers and
  buckets, the way you'd walk across a field.
- The road out, drifted to the tops of the fence posts.
- **The notebook** (if MC has it): a third line, in MC's hand: *"It's warm.
  It's not for you yet."* MC still doesn't remember writing any of them.

### The counter (Saturday)

1. **Olstad, stuck in town by the road.** Not working; she can't get out to
   anything that's hers. Irritable, bored, pregnant, in the warmest place she
   could think of. She reminds MC the rental's due in a week (*"Saturday
   next, Johnny. Roads permitting."*). She's heard what the kids are saying
   about the ice off the point. *"Kids say a lot."*
2. **Cody** (if MC gave the picks back): he went out on the thin ice, all week,
   with them. He found it. He won't say what. He puts something on the
   counter: a brass button, big, the kind on a heavy coat, with a scrap of
   brown wool still caught in its shank. *"It was in the ice. Frozen in, at
   the top. I chipped it out."* Pawn it, buy it, give it back to him. If MC
   kept the picks: Mrs. Hollis instead, with a dish, saying Cody hasn't been
   home for two nights.
3. **Hanna**, with an invitation: the first-ice party tonight, the whole town,
   on the lake. *"The Keeper always comes. It's for you, sort of."*

### Evening: the lake (the set piece)

The map has one place that matters tonight: the lake. (The Gleaner is shut:
everyone's on the ice.)

- **The first-ice party.** A bonfire on the shore by the poles. Lanterns on
  the ice. The whole town skating, old men with their hands behind their
  backs, children shrieking, the church ladies with thermoses. Somebody has a
  fiddle. It's lovely. It's the happiest MC has seen the town.
- Mrs. Hollis puts a cup of cocoa in MC's hands. Ruth is there (warm, or
  keeping her distance, by the collision). Sam is out past the lanterns,
  alone, on his knees, with a flashlight pointed down.
- **The shape.** Out where the light runs thin. Face up. A yard down. The
  long brown coat. The children skate a ring over it, playing the rhyme.
  *"...nine's the Keeper, in he goes."* Nobody looks down.
- **Choices:**
  - **Skate over it like everyone.** Someone takes MC's arm and pulls them into
    the ring. The town's eye: the Keeper joined in. (`skated_over`)
  - **Show Olstad.** She looks a long time with her flashlight. *"Log."* Then,
    quieter: *"Don't you say that word to anybody else. Not tonight."* (She
    knows. She's afraid of the crowd.) (`showed_olstad_ice`, kept faith with
    Olstad leans)
  - **Kneel with Sam.** He doesn't look up. *"October twenty-third. Four
    hundred meters off the point. Face up."* He writes it down. *"That's
    Thomas. I have his date."* The two of them on their knees on the ice while
    the town skates around them. Folie à deux, a step. (`knelt_with_sam`)
  - **Walk away.** Up the bank, past the poles. Behind MC the fiddle, the
    rhyme, the town on the ice. MC is the only person in Kettle Lake not
    there.

### Night ten

- **The radio:** the Saturday request hour. A request "for the Keeper, who
  came out on the ice tonight" (or "who didn't", if MC walked away). Number 9.
- **The call:** the room, the clock, and outside his window, far off, a train
  horn. Long. Twice. Then a second train, the other way. MC has no way to
  know what it means. Sam would.
- **The dog:** the stairs, and in the morning, on the line by the bowl, a
  brass button. The same kind. (If Cody brought one: MC checks the shelf. His
  is still there.)
