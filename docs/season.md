# The rest of the season (plan)

From Daniel's arrival (Nov 23, built) to breakup (end of April): every key day
left, what each one is for, which fork or door it carries, and where the
review's unpaid promises (`review.md` section 4) and missing groundwork
(section 7) get paid. A roadmap to agree before building, month by month,
each month planned in its own doc first, as before.

**Decided Oct 13: all four leans.** Everything here follows `forks.md` (the six hard forks, the doors, kept
faith, the endings) and `pillars.md`.

---

About **eighteen more key days** (to about 33 in all, as `story.md` plans),

## Thanksgiving week (built: `thanksgiving.md`)

**KD16, Wed Nov 24: the man in the good coat, at the counter.**
- Daniel comes in again, polite, a stranger: buys something small and exact
  (a box of shells? Ezra's Thursday box, the day before Thursday). He looks
  at the tin; he doesn't ask.
- **Paid here:** "There you are" (if MC broke the pretence: he says
  nothing of it, and that's worse); refusing the bullet (he mentions the
  matches kept him warm); telling Olstad or Sam (Olstad parked across the
  road all day; Sam not answering his door, the curtains drawn).
- Mrs. Hollis: the extra place at tomorrow's potluck. *"For whoever."*
- Night 16: no call (the payphone was the last). The radio: him, rehearsing,
  or the Reverend reading his name.

**KD17, Thu Nov 25: Thanksgiving.** A walk-around day (`visit.tscn`).
- **The potluck** in the church hall: the town at long tables, and **one
  place set facing the lake window**, a plate, a glass, nobody sits there
  (`underneath.md`). MC's seat is next to it.
- **The pageant** (June's): a child in a long brown coat and a red flannel
  shirt walks to the end of the stage, which is painted like ice. The
  children sing the rhyme to nine. *"And then the lights go down."* They do.
  In the dark, applause.
- **The Thursday watch:** Daniel on the radio for the first time as a guest,
  from the vestry booth. The whole hall listens on a radio on the pie table.
  He reads Leviticus 16 (the two goats) like a bedtime story.
- **Groundwork:** Olstad, at the door, watching him; then watching MC
  standing near Sam: a line, half a joke, about "the two bachelors", that
  the room laughs at and she doesn't take back (her prejudice, for the duel
  in February). Ruth's mother: Ruth at the potluck sets out a bowl, her
  mother's, and says so.

## December (built: `december.md`)

**KD18, Wed Dec 1: the envelope at dawn.**
- MC wakes before light and watches, through the door's glass, the boots on
  the step: **Ezra**, pushing the envelope under. Management is the
  congregation (`forks.md`, settled). He sees MC see him. Neither says
  anything.
- The money order: **postmarked Kettle Lake.** He walks it to the post
  office himself.
- **Paid here:** the rental. Northern Lights wrote to Olstad; the car is
  reported stolen. Olstad: *"I could tow it. Or I could forget."* What
  you did with the rental letter, and whether you tried to run, decides.

**KD19, ~Sat Dec 4: Sam's wall (his secret, door 2).**
- The cabin. The six unopened envelopes, one every September since 1993:
  *"You're sitting in my chair."* The wall: string, pins, the clock's
  minute, the collection totals, hymn numbers. MC adds a pin.
- **Groundwork for his lie:** Sam says, flatly, he never touches the
  clock. *"Nobody does. That's the point of it."*
- **Paid here:** crossing out "John" for him (he's written MC's line with
  the real name, or with "John" in quotes, depending); the human pair of
  eyes (Sam knows whose: the 1989 Keeper had brown eyes; it's on the wall).
- If MC hasn't opened Sam's doors, a smaller version: the curtains, a light,
  a figure at the wall through the window.

**KD20, ~Sun Dec 12: the Reverend (hard fork 4).**
- Daniel has told Ezra one true thing (the radio help: the Reverend gave
  the Keepers hints). After the service the congregation stays behind with
  the doors shut. The Reverend's car won't start.
- **The fork:** warn him (he has the night); hide him (the locked room
  upstairs, the back room, Sam's cabin: the town's eye rises); stay out of
  it.
- **Paid here:** speaking on air at church (`said_on_air`: you were on the
  radio, so the Reverend once helped you too, and they know); the human
  eyes in the jar (the church's), the teeth jar (the church asks for it now).

**KD21, Tue Dec 21: the shortest day. The freezer.**
- Light from 10:15 to 15:33. The furnace has run hot all month (or been fed
  for you). The bear's second seam, opened with the seam ripper: the spare
  key (shop step 8 builds the bear; until then, the key's found here).
- **The freezer opens.** Abraham, sitting on a crate, coat buttoned, frost
  on his eyebrows, as if he'd sat down to wait. Two explanations: an old
  man who believed it; the shop that took him.
- **Kept faith, Ruth:** tell her (take her to the freezer: the cruellest
  kind thing), tell Olstad (she already knows; she says so), or close the
  door and lock it again.
- **Paid here:** listening at the freezer; the padlock (Olstad's); the
  calendar circled October 4.

**KD22, Fri Dec 24: Christmas Eve.**
- With closeness to Daniel (two doors): **his secret, door 2.** The
  confession booth, roles reversed: he takes MC apart, every lie as John,
  until nothing of the costume's left; then his own count (*"Thirty-one.
  Thirty-two with John. You'd have been thirty-three."*) and his father's
  kitchen table.
- Without: Christmas Eve at Mrs. Hollis's table, three places and Ray's
  chair; or the Gleaner, Ruth, the wall of faces with tinsel on it; or the
  shop alone, and the radio: Daniel reading the Christmas story to the town.
- **Paid here:** taking your envelope off Ruth's wall (Ruth knows; she
  gives you the empty envelope back, or the church does, with your face
  drawn in by hand).

**KD23, Fri Dec 31: New Year's Eve (hard fork 5).**
- The party at the Gleaner. Midnight, and nothing happens: the town's
  millennium, disappointed.
- **The fork:** where MC is at midnight: the Gleaner with Ruth; the ice with
  Sam, at the tripod; alone at the shop, when the phone rings: *"Happy New
  Year, sweetheart."* (The call comes in every branch; only alone hears it
  live; the others find it on the machine, or hear the phone ringing through
  the floor.) Locks the romance, or none.
- **Paid here:** Locker 114, paid through tonight (if MC opened the tin: he
  can drive to Anchorage the next day, if the car's still his).

## January (plan: `january.md`)

**KD24, ~Mon Jan 3: the Disappointment.**
- Daniel, on the radio, now the host: *"The end didn't come because the Lord
  is waiting. For the ice. For the offering."* The new date: breakup.
- **He speaks to MC** for the first time as himself, on the phone or at the
  counter. **Paid here:** the notebook. He quotes a line MC wrote in
  November, word for word (`notebook_*` flags). Or, without the notebook,
  what MC did alone (the armchair facing the door, the radio left on).

**KD25, ~Sat Jan 15: Walt comes back.**
- **Kept faith, Walt:** he comes for the name (if MC took it) or for the
  silver dollars (if MC took those); give them back so the town can say
  "Ida" again, or refuse. **Paid here:** the silver dollars, finally.
- Olstad's baby is born (a bridge card; she's off the road a week, and the
  town notices who's not watching).

**KD26, ~Sun Jan 23: the confession.**
- Daniel hears confessions in the vestry; the town queues. MC can go, or
  not. **The town's eye** shows for the first time: who nods to MC, who
  doesn't.
- Cody (if alive, and if MC kept his picks): the picks, asked for again,
  through the ice that's three feet thick now. **Paid here:** keeping the
  picks.

## February

**KD27, ~Sat Feb 5: the scapegoat.**
- Sermons on Leviticus 16. **Ezra's hand** broken (Daniel, "an accident at
  the vestry door"). **Kept faith, Ezra:** he can't load his .22 on
  Thursday; load the shells for him, or turn him away.
- **Ruth's lie (door 3):** drunk, she says the 1989 Keeper didn't run; she
  drove him to the highway herself, and her mother died that night in the
  empty shop. And she has MC's car keys. Forgive her, or don't.

**KD28, ~Sat Feb 19: the duel.**
- **Olstad and Daniel:** at the counter, in front of MC, very polite. He
  shows her he could (her family's address, said once). She shows him she
  isn't leaving. Then she asks MC for the truth about the John night, for a
  case. **Kept faith, Olstad:** tell her, or lie (she'll know).
- **Kept faith, Sam:** the town wants the man with the lights on the ice;
  Daniel asks MC, in confession, where the lights come from. Don't give him
  up (hide the notebooks), or tell.
- **The open back door:** the night after, every door on the road is open.

## March

**KD29, ~Sat Mar 11: the frenzy.**
- Processions past the shop; notes under the door (the town's eye, high or
  low); customers who stop coming.
- **Sam's lie (door 3):** at night, at the tripod, MC catches him moving the
  clock's hands. *"Setting it back to the true minute."* Forgive him, or
  don't.

**KD30, ~Sat Mar 25: his price (hard fork 6).**
- Daniel names it, because he never lies: the books. **Pay** (the tin, the
  locker), **confess** (in his booth: the John night, the thing he can't
  price), or **refuse**.
- **His one lie (door 3):** *"I said I didn't know why I let you go. I
  knew."*

## April

**KD31, ~Sun Apr 2: the light comes back.**
- Clocks spring forward; sunset at 20:46. The worst news in the game.
- **Run** becomes possible: the road open, the plate (Olstad's choice in
  December), the keys (Ruth's). Who MC leaves holding the shop's keys.

**KD32, the night before breakup.**
- The shop at night. **The fire**, if it's open (no romance locked, free of
  Daniel, four of six kept faith, someone who lets MC go): the ledger, the
  freezer, the bear. Or the last ordinary night.

**KD33, breakup.**
- The ice goes out. The endings (`forks.md`), read in order: Run, a locked
  romance, the fire, the season ends (or the ice). Olstad on the shore in
  every one where MC is. Daniel's four outcomes inside them.
