## Key day 16, Wednesday November 24: the counter

**Morning.** Reads what happened yesterday:
- Told Olstad: her cruiser across the road, all day, engine running.
- Told Sam: his cabin's curtains drawn at noon, a light on behind them.
- Told no one: the church lights still on from last night, in daylight.
- "There you are": you wake with the two words in your mouth, in his rhythm.
- Took the bullet: on the back shelf, next to the tin, still warm. You check.
- The envelope (Wednesday): a hundred and fifty, faced, and a card in it, a
  turkey drawn by a child: *For the Keeper's table.*

**1. Mrs. Hollis: a place for whoever.** (A browse: the first request since
week one that's a need, not a name.)
- *"Tomorrow there's a place set for whoever. There always is. I'd like
  something on it from the shop. Something of his. You'll know."*
- She means Ray. The plain answer: anything that was Ray's. The folk answer:
  something that will call him to the table.
- **Hand over:** the duck call (if still in the shop: *"He'll hear that."*);
  the ice picks (if held, back on the shelf: *"No. Not those. He won't need
  those where he is."*); anything else (*"That's kind. That's not his."*);
  or nothing (*"Then the place stays bare. That's all right. It usually
  does."*).
- What's given shows on the empty place tomorrow.

**2. The man in the good coat.** Polite, a stranger, exactly as on Tuesday.
- He asks for *"whatever Mr. Lund buys on Thursdays. I'd like to bring him
  something tomorrow. He's been kind."* Ezra's box of .22 shells, in the
  drawer, THURS.
- **The choice:** sell it to him (Ezra goes without his Thursday box: a small
  broken faith, the first, and Daniel knows the shop's routines now); or
  *"That's spoken for."* (He smiles: *"Of course. Everything here is
  someone's."*)
- He looks at the tin, once. He doesn't ask.
- **Paid here:** "There you are" (he says nothing of it, and looks at you a
  moment too long, the way you'd look at a word you'd taught someone);
  refusing the bullet (*"The matches kept me warm. Thank you."*); taking it
  (his eyes go to the back shelf: *"Keeping well?"*).

**3. Whoever MC told** (or June, if no one):
- **Olstad** (told her): *"I looked at him. Real close. He's got a permit
  for everything he owns, and a hole in his side nobody's stitched right.
  He told me a hunting accident. He told me the truth about something else
  in the same breath, so I'd believe the lie."* She doesn't say what. Then:
  *"Stay where I can see you, Johnny."*
- **Sam** (told him): he doesn't come in. A note under the door: the hymn
  numbers from the radio, a circle around tomorrow's date, and *"Don't eat
  the pie."* (Two explanations: Sam's paranoia; or Sam knows something.)
- **June** (told no one): the program for tomorrow, typed, the Keeper's name
  printed as "JOHN" in capitals, and the boy who'll wear MC's shirt (if lent)
  waving from the truck.

**Closing; the evening** (the map, with its places):
- **The Gleaner:** Ruth polishing a big blue bowl for tomorrow. *"Mom's.
  She'd have wanted it out."* (Her mother's first mention: groundwork for
  February.) Above, in the room nobody rents, a floorboard.
- **The church:** the pageant rehearsal through the window: children in a
  row on a stage painted white, practising walking out onto it.
- **Sam's cabin:** dark, or the curtains (if told).

**Night 16.** The routine. No call (the payphone was the last). The radio:
the Reverend, *"Tomorrow, friends, our guest will be with us on the Thursday
watch. Bring your radios to the hall."* **Out of place:** across the road,
the payphone's receiver off its hook, hanging, turning slowly in the wind.

## Key day 17, Thursday November 25: Thanksgiving

A walk-around day (`visit.tscn`): the church hall, the stage, the vestry.

**Morning.** The shop shut for the holiday (a sign in Abraham's hand, kept
for this day: CLOSED FOR THANKSGIVING. THE KEEPER IS AT TABLE.) The walk up
the hill, every house's chimney going.

**The hall: the potluck.**
- Long tables, the whole town, paper tablecloths, a radio on the pie table.
- **The empty place**, set facing the lake window: a plate, a glass, a
  chair. On it, whatever MC gave Mrs. Hollis (or nothing). **MC's seat is
  next to it.**
- Ezra says grace: thanks for the harvest, the ice, *"and for the Keeper,
  who keeps."* Everyone looks at MC.
- **Ruth's bowl** on the table, her mother's. She serves from it.
- **Olstad** by the door, not eating, watching him, then watching MC and Sam
  at the same end of the table: *"Look at that. The two bachelors."* The
  room laughs. She doesn't take it back. (Her prejudice, for the duel in
  February.)
- **Him**, at the Reverend's right hand, carving. He doesn't look at MC. (If
  "There you are": once, he passes the salt down the table without being
  asked, and it stops in front of MC.)
- **Things to look at** (hotspots): the empty place; the radio on the pie
  table; Ruth's bowl; Olstad by the door; the window, the lake.
- **The empty place** can be sat in. *"Sit in the empty place."* The room
  goes quiet; Mrs. Hollis smiles as if something's been settled; the town's
  eye on MC (once the eye is a count, this raises it). Or not.

**The stage: the pageant.**
- June's children. A boy in a long brown coat (Mrs. Hollis's sewing, cut
  down) and, if MC lent it, MC's red flannel shirt, walks out across the
  stage, which is painted like ice, while the others sing the rhyme: one,
  two, all the way to nine, *"and nine's the Keeper, in he goes."*
- The boy reaches the edge of the stage. *"And then the lights go down."*
  They do. In the dark, the whole hall applauds.
- The lights come up. The boy is bowing. Of course he is.
- (If MC said he couldn't come: June saves him the front seat anyway; the
  Keeper always sits in front.)

**The vestry: the Thursday watch.**
- At nine, the radio on the pie table: him, on the air for the first time as
  a guest, from the booth behind the vestry wall. The whole hall stops to
  listen.
- He reads Leviticus 16 like a bedtime story: two goats, one for the Lord,
  one sent into the wilderness bearing all their sins. *"Isn't that a mercy.
  To have someone who carries it for you."*
- MC can go to the vestry door: through the glass, the red ON AIR bulb, his
  back, the Reverend beside him not looking at the microphone.
- **Paid here:** "Don't eat the pie" (if Sam's note): MC can refuse the pie,
  and Mrs. Hollis's face.

**Walk home.** The town's windows, every one with a radio playing the same
voice. *end=night.*

**Night 17.** The routine. The radio: him again, the late repeat. The dog.
**Out of place:** on the back step, a plate under foil, still warm, from
the potluck: *For the Keeper's dog.* There's no dog.

## The bridge to December (Fri 26 - Tue 30)

- **Friday:** the Gleaner, full, nobody talking of anything else. *"Such a
  voice."*
- **Sunday:** he reads the lesson at the morning service. The Reverend sits
  down in the front pew and doesn't get up again until it's over.
- **Tuesday:** snow, and the envelope a day early, and with it, for the
  first time, nothing extra. Not a card. Not a hymn number.
- Then **key day 18** (December, `season.md`).
