=== morning
narration | time=07:30: Saturday, the fifth of February. Light at nine. Dark after five. Eleven weeks, the radio says, and says it again, as if you might not have heard.

# --- Ezra, and his hand. Brought in by him. ---

=== ezra_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Two men. Ezra, and the man in the good coat with an arm round Ezra's shoulders, the way you'd walk a man home from a funeral.
narration: Ezra's right arm in a cast from the knuckles to the elbow, in a sling made from a church altar cloth.
daniel: The hall door. I shut it on his hand, and then I opened it and shut it again, to be sure. Tell the Keeper what you tell the hall, Ezra.
ezra: It was the door.
daniel: He called you the goat. In the hall, out loud, twice. Nobody calls you anything. Nobody but me.
narration: Ezra looks at the counter. At the drawer.
ezra: I missed Thursday. First Thursday in twenty-two years. I couldn't load it. I sat at the kitchen table with the box and the gun and I couldn't get one in.
ezra: I'd like my box. And I'd like... I'd like you to load it for me. If you would. Five in the tube. So I've got Thursday.
narration: In the doorway, the man in the good coat waits, his hands folded. He isn't watching your hands. He's watching your face, to see which you'll do.
paranoia: Ezra. Who called you the goat. Who barred the doors. Who brought you the church's things to hold.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: His hand. Look at his hand.
> Load the shells for him. -> ezra_load | keep=ezra | lean=warmth
> "I'm sorry, Ezra. I can't." -> ezra_refuse | betray=ezra | lean=paranoia

=== ezra_load
narration | sound=drawer | sold=shells: You take the box from the drawer. He puts the .22 on the glass, an old one, the stock dark with hands. You load it. Five in the tube, one at a time, the way he shows you with his good hand.
narration | write=E. Lund. .22 shells, one box. Loaded for him. $6: You write his line in the ledger, the same line, the twenty-third year of it.
ezra: Thank you.
narration: He doesn't look at you when he says it. He looks at the gun. His eyes are wet.
narration: The man in the good coat lays six dollars on the glass before Ezra can reach for his. Exact. For the shells, or for the hand.
daniel: Five in the tube for the man who called you the goat, and your hands never shook. I was watching. Now I know what you'll do, and for whom.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: They go out together. The bell, once.

=== ezra_refuse
narration: He nods. He picks up the gun with his good hand and holds it against his chest like a baby.
ezra: No. No, that's right. That's right.
narration: In the doorway, the man in the good coat smiles, slowly, the way a man smiles when the card he's been waiting for turns over.
daniel: Come on, Ezra. I'll load it for you. I broke the hand. I always pay for what I break.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: They go out together. Through the window you watch him load it on the step, five, slowly, in the cold, while Ezra watches his hands.

# --- Mrs. Hollis, fasting. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Mrs. Hollis. Thinner. Her coat hangs on her. No dish.
widow: I'm not eating Fridays. Nobody is. It's easier than you'd think. You feel very clear.
widow: Tomorrow, at the hall. They've brought the scale out. From the first Sunday, do you remember? Everybody's being weighed again. Every Sunday till April. Written down.
widow: You'll come. You'll be weighed. It's only to know.
narration: She puts her hand on your wrist across the counter.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: She touches your thread. She smiles.
narration | if=!flag:wears_thread: Her thumb rubs your bare wrist, where a thread would be. She doesn't say anything.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. The bell.

# --- The goat. ---

=== goat_enters
narration | time=15:00: Three o'clock. The light going.
narration: At the door, outside, on the step: the goat. White, the red rope round its neck, the end trailing in the snow. Nobody holding it.
narration: It doesn't knock. It doesn't bleat. It stands with its face to the glass, its strange flat eyes on you, and waits.
narration: Across the road, the children who lead it round the town every afternoon, in a row, holding hands, waiting too.
paranoia: It's a goat. Somebody's goat. The children take it round.
> Go out to it. -> goat_out | flag=let_goat_in | eye=+1 | lean=warmth
> Stay behind the counter. -> goat_stay | lean=paranoia

=== goat_out
narration: You open the door. The goat walks in past you, as if it lived here, and goes to the stove, and lies down in front of it, its legs folded under, and closes its eyes.
narration: The children across the road watch through the window. Then they run off up the road, all of them, to tell someone.
narration: It sleeps by your stove till dark. Then it gets up, and goes to the door, and waits for you to open it, and goes.

=== goat_stay
narration: You stay where you are. It stays where it is.
narration: It stands on your step, face to the glass, until it's dark. The children stand across the road with it, until their mothers come and take them home. Then it turns, and walks away down the middle of the road, the rope trailing, and the snow fills its prints.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=17:10: Ten past five, and the light holding on longer than it did. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: Up the hill, against the last of the sky, the post on the tower, and the iron ring, and something tied to the ring that wasn't there this morning: a red rope, hanging down, moving in the wind.
