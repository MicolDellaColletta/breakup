# THE THIRTY-SECOND NIGHT: Friday, February 11. After Ruth.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, quiet and close, about
# forgiveness and the woman who drove a man to the highway, as if he'd been
# at the end of the bar. He was. Out of place:
# the blue bowl, Ruth's mother's, on your kitchen table (odd_bowl).
# Design: docs/february.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. Ruth's voice still in your ears. "So it's never empty again."
narration | if=flag:keys_returned: The car keys in your pocket. Heavy. The rental's tag.
narration | if=doors:ruth>=3: You put your hand over hers. You keep feeling it, the keys under both your hands.
paranoia: The rules. She'll set a place for you at breakup. The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Friday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, not shouting tonight. Low and close, as if he were sitting on the end of your bed, which is worse. "The heart is deceitful above all things. Jeremiah seventeen. There's a woman in this town who drove a man to the highway and left her mother to sit in the dark for it, and tonight she told it, to the one person she thought would keep it. Forgiveness isn't a gift, friends. It's a debt. Somebody agreed to carry hers tonight. Or didn't. I was there. I know which."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. You know what he'd say. You heard him get up from his chair in the dark.

=== kitchen_fed

=== kitchen_unfed

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The Gleaner dark. Every house dark. A fasting town, asleep early.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Nothing moving on the whole road, not a light, not a dog.

=== back_door

=== odd_bowl
narration | time=+5: The kitchen table. A blue bowl, old, glazed, a chip on the rim turned toward you.
narration | flag=saw_bowl: Her mother's. You saw it on the bar two hours ago. It's here. It's full: kibble, dry, a heaped bowl of it, the way you'd fill a bowl for a dog you loved.
paranoia: She brought it. She walked it up here while you were on the road. She has a key. Everyone has a key.
