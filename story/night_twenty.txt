# THE TWENTIETH NIGHT: Sunday, December 12. After the Reverend.
# Same format as the other nights. No Reverend on the radio: him, alone, at
# night, for the first time. What happened to Elias shows: praying through
# the floor (hidden in the back room), a lamp at Sam's (hidden there), the
# manse dark (gone). Out of place: the church bulletin on the kitchen table,
# every hymn number crossed out (odd_bulletin). Design: docs/december.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: Below you, behind the steel door, a man sitting on a bench in the dark. You took him a blanket and a plate. He said thank you as if you'd done something terrible.
narration | if=flag:reverend_at_sam: Down the shore, at Sam's, two shadows on the curtain now. One at the wall. One on the floor, not moving.
narration | if=flag:reverend_warned: Somewhere south, on the highway, in the dark, a man with a bag on his knees. You hope. You hope.
narration | if=flag:reverend_gone: Up the hill, the manse. You don't look. You've stopped looking at the manse.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Not the Reverend. Him. At night. At the Reverend's microphone, in the Reverend's chair.
narration: "Good evening, friends. The Reverend has gone to rest. I'll sit with you now. I'll sit with you every night, for as long as you need me."
narration: Then a hymn, and no numbers after it. The first night in twenty-two years, Sam will tell you, without the numbers.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: And under the radio, through the floor, from behind the steel door, a man praying, very quietly. The same words, as the voice on the radio says them. A half-second behind.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: In the silence, through the floor, from behind the steel door, a man praying. Very quietly. The same few words, over and over, like a man trying to remember a phone number.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twenty
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twenty | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: Past the steel door. You don't stop. You don't knock. You set the bowl down and go back up, and behind the steel door the praying stops while you pass, and starts again.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Two candles in every window on the road.
narration | if=flag:frozen_taped: The taped one holds. A white cross of frost on the black.
narration | if=flag:frozen_open: The frozen one, still open its finger's width. You've stopped trying.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. What latches.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Up the hill, at the manse, a light goes on in an upstairs window, and off again. Someone looking for something.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Anyone can come in.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: Anyone. Tonight that means them. Tonight the rule is a door you've left open on a man hiding twelve feet away.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: Behind you, on the bench, the Reverend lets out a breath. He doesn't thank you. Rule one, broken for him. He knows what it costs.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twenty
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down. It stops at the steel door on the way. A long time. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door, below. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Monday.
narration | if=flag:reverend_gone: The manse, from the window. The car in the drive, hood down now. No smoke from the chimney. Nobody says where he went.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_bulletin
narration | time=+5: On the kitchen table, the church bulletin. This Sunday's. You didn't bring one home. You didn't take one.
narration | flag=saw_bulletin: The hymn numbers down the side, the ones the Reverend chose. Every one crossed out, neatly, with a ruler. And in the margin, in pencil, a new column, in a hand you know from the money orders' memo line. One number. The same number, every Sunday till April.
paranoia: Which number. What number.
narration: You can't read it. You can read every word on the page but that one. You hold it to the light. It's a number. You'd know it if you saw it. You're seeing it.
