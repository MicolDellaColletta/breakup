# THE TWENTIETH NIGHT: Monday, December 6. After the miracle.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, reading a list of the sick
# in town, by name, slowly. Out of place: the alarm clock by the bed, stopped
# at nine (odd_clock). Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. You stand at the window a while first. Up the road, at the Gleaner, the door open, and the light falling out on the snow, and nobody closing it.
narration | if=flag:took_watch: Downstairs, on the back shelf, beside the tin and the bullet, a watch that won't go past nine. You can feel all three of them there.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Monday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. Not a sermon. A list. "Friends, I've been asked to pray for some of you tonight. I'll say your names, and you'll know."
narration: The sick. The old. Mrs. Lund's sister with the cough. The Pruitt boy's ear. A woman whose name you don't know, "for the thing she told me she can't tell her husband." Name after name, slowly, the way you'd count out change.
narration: At the end: "And for the Keeper. Who isn't sick. Who'll be well." Then a hymn.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, his voice, a list of something. Names. One of them, you'd swear, is yours.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twenty
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twenty | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The Gleaner's door still open up the road, the light lying on the snow like a rug put out.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. From up the road, through the Gleaner's open door, the sound of a whole room laughing at once.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. The clock by the bed ticking.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twenty
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Tuesday.
narration | if=flag:gun_furnace: At four in the morning, a crack from downstairs, flat and loud, one, like a board breaking. In the back room, behind the furnace, the towel smoking. The gun, the cylinder turned one place on. Nothing fired. There was nothing to fire. The empty chamber came round on its own.
narration | if=flag:saw_clock: The alarm clock by the bed, going again. You didn't touch it. It says the right time. It's a minute slow by noon, and stops again at nine that night.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_clock
narration | time=+5: The alarm clock on the nightstand. The one you wind every night without thinking.
narration | flag=saw_clock: Stopped. The hands at nine. Not quite nine: a minute before, the minute hand bent, very slightly, as if someone had pressed on it with a fingertip.
paranoia: You forgot to wind it. Tired. You forgot.
narration: You wound it last night. You remember the sound of it.
