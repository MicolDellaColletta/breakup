# KEY DAY 29: Saturday, January 15, 2000. Walt comes back.
# Same format as the other counter days: Walt, for his wife's name or her
# silver dollars (kept faith, Walt); Cody, if he's alive, or Mrs. Hollis, for
# the picks for his place, if he went through. Then a walked evening
# (story/evening_twentynine.txt): Olstad at the ruins, with the vestry bolt. See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd. Design: docs/january.md.

=== morning
narration | time=07:30: Saturday, the fifteenth. Light at a quarter to ten now. Forty minutes more than the shortest day. The radio counts it like money.
narration | if=fray>=4 or unknown>=4 | write=One coat. Held.: The ledger, open on the counter. The last line in your handwriting: One coat. Held. You don't remember writing it. You don't remember a coat coming in.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:00: You turn the sign. OPEN.

# --- Walt. ---

=== seller_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. An old man in a good coat, the coat too big for him now. Walt Pruitt. He stands just inside the door the way he did in October, as if no time had passed, and looks around for something he can't name.
narration: He puts a round blue biscuit tin on the glass. Thread in it, a thimble, bronze hairpins stuck through a pincushion like a hedgehog.
seller: Her sewing things. I don't sew. She'd want it used. I don't want anything for it.
narration | unpacked=ida_sewing_tin: He takes his hand off the lid slowly, as if it might open by itself.
-> walt_name | if=flag:ida_pawned
-> walt_dollars | if=held:silver_dollars
-> walt_thanks

=== walt_name
seller: I can't say her name.
seller: I've been trying since October. In the kitchen. Out loud. It gets to my mouth and stops, like a bird at a window.
seller: You've got it. On a ticket. I'd like it back. I don't have the money. I don't think there was any money.
narration: The stub on the back shelf, standing on its empty patch. One name, in your handwriting, the ink gone brown.
paranoia: Rule six said don't take it. You took it. Nothing in the letter says what you do now.
> Tear up the ticket. Give it back. -> walt_name_back | keep=walt | flag=ida_returned | lean=warmth
> "I can't. It's the rules." -> walt_name_kept | betray=walt | lean=paranoia

=== walt_name_back
narration | redeemed=name_ida: You take the stub off the shelf and tear it in half in front of him, and in half again, and put the pieces in his hand.
narration: He holds them. He closes his hand on them.
seller: Ida.
narration: He says it to the shop, to the shelves, to the bear. The first time since October. His face does something you'll remember for the rest of your life.
seller: Ida. Ida. Ida.
-> walt_after_name

=== walt_name_kept
narration: He nods. He doesn't argue. He was never going to argue.
seller: No. No, I suppose not.
narration | sound=bell: He goes out without his hat. You don't call after him. It stays on the counter all day, and nobody comes back for it.

=== walt_after_name
-> walt_dollars | if=held:silver_dollars
-> walt_name_goes

=== walt_name_goes
narration | sound=bell: He goes out still saying it, quietly, all the way up the road.

=== walt_dollars
seller: And the coins. Her dollars. I'd like those too, if I could. I can't pay the loan. I spent it on the stove. I'm sorry.
narration: The roll of silver dollars, on the back shelf. You pick it up.
narration: It's warm. Still. Three months on a cold shelf, and it's as warm as the morning he brought it in.
> Give them back. No charge. -> walt_dollars_back | keep=walt | lean=warmth
> "The loan first, Walt. That's how it works." -> walt_dollars_kept | lean=appraisal

=== walt_dollars_back
narration | redeemed=silver_dollars: You put the roll in his hands. He closes them around it.
seller: Still warm. She always kept them in her apron pocket. They were always warm.
narration | sound=bell: He goes out with the roll held against his chest like something alive.

=== walt_dollars_kept
narration: He nods. He knew.
seller: Of course. Of course. It's a shop.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. On the shelf, the roll goes cold under your hand for the first time since October. As if it had stopped waiting.

=== walt_thanks
seller: I don't know why I came. I wanted to say thank you. For something in October. I can't remember what.
narration: You know. You turned him away, with the coins and with her name. Gently.
seller: She'd have liked you. Ida. That's her name. I can still say it. Some can't. Ezra's wife can't say her boy's. Not since '88.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. You stand there with her name in the air of the shop, said out loud, the way it should be.

# --- Teague, with a skull. And the trap, if it's still here. ---

=== teague_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. A big man in a fur hat, stooping under the door frame though he doesn't have to. Teague. Woodsmoke and cold come in with him, and the animal smell under them.
narration: He puts a skull on the glass. A wolf's, bleached white, the long teeth all there. A hole drilled through the crown, and a loop of rawhide through the hole.
teague: Twenty-five. My dad hung one of these over every door he had. Keeps the dead from following you in.
teague: I've sold the rest. I don't need this one.
narration | if=held:leg_trap: He isn't looking at you. He's looking past you, at the floor shelf, the whole time. At the trap.
> Pay him the twenty-five. -> teague_buy | lean=appraisal
> "Hang it over your own door." -> teague_keep | lean=paranoia

=== teague_buy
narration | unpacked=wolf_skull | cash=-25: Twenty-five dollars. He folds it into the binder clip without counting it, and pushes the skull an inch toward you, teeth first.
-> teague_trap | if=held:leg_trap
-> teague_goes

=== teague_keep
teague: I haven't got a door I'm worried about. You have.
narration: He puts the skull back in his coat.
-> teague_trap | if=held:leg_trap
-> teague_goes

=== teague_trap
narration: He goes to the floor shelf and picks up the trap. Turns the tag over with his thumb. NOT FOR FOX.
teague: That's mine. Victor Number Three. I lent it to Abraham, years back. For the stairs, he said. I didn't ask what was on his stairs.
teague: He oiled it. Every year, by the look. Never set it once.
paranoia: For the stairs. Something comes up the stairs at three.
narration | redeemed=leg_trap: He hangs it over his shoulder by the chain. He doesn't offer to pay. You don't ask him to.
teague: Whatever it was for, he couldn't do it. Don't you either.
-> teague_goes

=== teague_goes
narration | sound=bell: The bell. Through the window he stops at his truck and looks back at the shop a long while before he gets in.

# --- Cody, or Cody's place. ---

=== nephew_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Cody Hollis, wind-burned, the picks round his neck, smelling of fish and the stove in his shanty.
nephew: Ice is three foot. More. You could drive a truck to Fairbanks on it.
narration: He sits on the stool by the stove without asking, holding his hands to it.
nephew: Something knocked. Under the ice. Where I was fishing. Thursday.
nephew: Three times. Like knuckles on a door. Then nothing. Then three more, further off. Like whoever it was had moved along to the next house.
paranoia: A fish. A pressure crack. The ice talks. Everybody says the ice talks.
nephew: I didn't open the hole again. I just sat there. I sat there a long time, being home.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. On the stool, a wet ring from his coat, slowly drying.

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Mrs. Hollis. She doesn't bring a dish.
widow: The picks. Cody's. Ray's. You kept them for him, in October.
widow: I'd like them. For his place at the table. I set it every night. He'll want them now. He'll be cold, where he is.
narration: The picks, on the shelf where you put them in October. R.H. on each handle.
paranoia: Ray traded them. Cody never got them back. And now his place at the table, every night.
> Give them to her. -> widow_picks_given | keep=hollis | flag=picks_for_place | lean=warmth
> "They're spoken for." -> widow_picks_kept | lean=paranoia

=== widow_picks_given
narration | redeemed=ice_picks_held: You put them in her hands. She hangs them round her own neck, under her coat, the cord against her throat.
widow: There. Now he'll find his way.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. You hear them knock together under her coat as she walks, two wooden handles, like someone tapping to be let in.

=== widow_picks_kept
narration: She doesn't argue. She looks at the shelf where they are, for a long time, the way you'd look at a grave you weren't allowed to tend.
widow: Then I'll set his place without them.
narration | sound=bell: She goes.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=16:21: Twenty-one minutes past four. Dark. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration | if=flag:ida_returned: Up the road, through the Gleaner's window, Walt at the bar, and the whole bar raising their glasses to something, and you hear it through the glass: "Ida."
