=== morning
narration | time=07:30: Saturday, the nineteenth. Seven weeks. Light at eight. The snow off the roofs at noon, dripping, and freezing again by four.

narration | if=flag:keys_returned: Your car keys on the dresser. You've moved them three times this week, from pocket to dresser to pocket. Nowhere feels like the right place for a way out.

# --- The goat. ---

=== goat_enters
narration | time=+90: The goat at the door. The red thread round its horn, and a second one now round the other.
narration: It stays an hour. Then it goes, on its own, up the hill, toward the post.

# --- Olstad and Daniel. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | time=+60: He's in the customer chair when you look up. You didn't hear the bell. Warming his hands.
narration | sound=bell: The bell. Olstad. The baby in a sling under her coat, a red face in a white hat, asleep. She sees him in the chair. She doesn't stop walking.
daniel: Sergeant. And this is her. What's her name?
trooper: No.
daniel: Of course. Of course.
narration: He looks at the baby. Then at Olstad. Very polite.
daniel: Your sister's in Wasilla, isn't she. Lakeview Drive. The yellow house, the one with the swing. It must be pretty in summer, that street.
narration: Olstad doesn't move. The baby sleeps.
trooper: I'm not leaving. She's not leaving. And you're not leaving either. I'll be on the shore at breakup, and you'll be on it, and I'll be standing right next to you.
narration: He smiles. He stands, and buttons the good coat.
daniel: I'd like that very much.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, once. Through the window he stops on the step to look at the goat's prints in the snow, as if they were a message he could read.
narration: Olstad stands at the counter a long time with her hand on the baby's back.
trooper: I need the John night. All of it. The motel, the lot, the apartment, whatever it was. I need you to tell me, and I need to write it down. It's the only thing I can take to a judge that isn't a town's word against a town's word.
trooper: It'll cost you. You know it'll cost you.
-> trooper_reads

=== trooper_truth
narration: You tell her. All of it, as far as you have it. The empty chamber. The man in the doorway. The night it was his, and you were there, or near, or somewhere you can't find.
narration: She writes it down. She doesn't look up. The baby wakes and looks at you over the edge of the sling with enormous dark eyes and doesn't cry.
trooper: Okay. Okay. That's a man I can put on a witness stand. That's a man who might be in a cell in Anchorage by June. I'm sorry. That's the deal.

trooper: Okay.
narration: She doesn't say Johnny. She doesn't say anything else.
