=== morning
narration | time=09:30 | show=hall: Sunday, the second of April. The clocks went forward in the night. You heard the town do it, a hundred little clicks at the same minute, and lay awake after.
narration: The hall. On the door, the count: 3. The scale in the middle of the floor. Nobody stands on it now. There's nothing left to weigh.
narration: He doesn't call anyone to the anxious bench. He opens the ledger, the shop's ledger, Abraham's, that nobody's seen him take, and reads the Keepers' names, from the front. 1931. One name a winter. After every name, the hall says "Amen."
narration: It takes a long time. Seventy names. The light comes in sideways through the big window and goes all the way across the floor.
narration: The last name he reads is "John."
narration: The hall says "Amen."
paranoia: You. They said amen over you.
narration: At the door, after, Mrs. Hollis takes your hand. "Sunset's at a quarter to nine now," she says. "Isn't that nice. We'll all be able to see."
