# THE TENTH NIGHT: Saturday, October 23 (after key day 10 and the ice)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the request hour, for the Keeper
# who came out on the ice (or didn't); the call, and outside his window a
# train, twice (the Alaska Railroad runs through Wasilla); and by the bowl in
# the morning, a brass button. Going down to the phone is the same walk as
# night two. Design: docs/week_three.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs, with the cold of the lake still in your feet.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. From the front window, the bonfire, burning down, and the last lanterns going out one by one.
narration | if=flag:saw_shape: You don't look past where the lanterns were. You look anyway.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Saturday request hour. The calm voice, tired, as if he'd been at the party too, and left early.
narration | if=!flag:walked_from_ice: "A request, friends. For the Keeper, who came out on the ice with us tonight. Number nine."
narration | if=flag:walked_from_ice: "A request, friends. For the Keeper, who went home early tonight. Number nine."
narration: Then a hymn, a lot of voices, far away.
paranoia: Nine again. Every week there's a nine.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, from somewhere up the shore, someone's radio, and the word nine.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_ten
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_ten | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl, the way the note says. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line. Your hand's still shaking from the cold. It's the cold.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The lake, dark now. The fire, embers.
narration | if=broken:window>=2: On the inside of one pane, a handprint in the damp, at your height. You put your hand to it. It fits.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: The cold, and from the lake, very faint, the ice talking to itself. A long low note, like a held breath let out.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=has:notebook and flag:knelt_with_sam: The notebook. You write it, finally, yourself, on purpose: "Oct 23. 400 m off the point. Face up. A yard down." Your hand is steady. That frightens you more than the rest.
narration | if=has:notebook and !flag:knelt_with_sam: The notebook. You hold the pen over the page a long time. You don't write anything. You can't decide which column it goes in.
narration | if=!has:notebook: The flannel shirts. You touch them. Wool. Not brown. Not yet.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure on the breaking ice, in its long coat.
narration | if=flag:saw_shape: Tonight you see what you never saw before: under the ice in the painting, a darker shape, faint, long, painted over, as if the painter had changed their mind.
paranoia: You can see what you want in paint. You can see what you want in ice.

=== locked_door
narration | time=+5: The door at the end of the hall. Locked. Dark. Cold air under it, colder than the hall.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, to the steel door.
narration | take=note_furnace: On the steel door, at eye height, a card, pinned with a tack. The owner's hand, small and careful: "9. Don't let the furnace go out."
paranoia: There are eight rules. There were eight rules.
narration: In the back room the furnace is low. Embers. The coal scuttle beside it, full, the way it always is.
> Feed it -> furnace_fed | flag=fed_furnace
> Leave it. There are eight rules. -> furnace_left | break=furnace | flag=furnace_out

=== furnace_fed
narration: Three shovels. The belly of it goes from red to orange to white. Your face burns. Behind you the freezer hums, as if it were glad.
-> back_door_rule

=== furnace_left
narration: You leave it. The embers tick as they cool. Behind you the freezer hums louder, as if it were working harder.
-> back_door_rule

=== back_door_rule
narration: The back door.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

# --- Bed, and the call. ---

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Your feet won't get warm. Eleven.
narration | sound=ring: The shop phone.
paranoia: Rule four.
> Go down to it -> phone_go_down | lean=animal
> Let it ring -> phone_ignored | lean=paranoia

=== phone_ignored
narration | show=bedroom: It rings. You let it.
narration | stop=ring: It stops.
-> night_dog

=== phone_go_down
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs, toward the ringing.
narration | stop=stairs: The hallway. The steel door. The phone.

=== phone_steel_door
narration: The steel door. The cold under it. Lake-cold, tonight.

=== phone_turn_back
narration | sound=stairs: You go back up.
-> phone_ignored

=== phone_down
narration: Behind the counter.
narration | stop=ring | sound=pickup | break=phone: You lift the receiver. You don't speak.
narration: The room. The clock. The breathing, easier now. He's healing.
narration: And then, outside his window, far away: a train horn. Long. Twice. The way they blow it for a crossing.
narration: A long while later, another, the other way.
paranoia: A train. There's no train here. There's no train anywhere near here.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=2: There's a railroad. Anchorage to Fairbanks, through the valley. Through Wasilla. Through Palmer. He's on the line.
> Wait -> phone_wait
> Hang up first -> phone_hang_up | lean=animal

=== phone_wait
narration: You wait until the second train's gone. He waits too. As if he wanted you to hear it.
narration | sound=hangup: A soft click.
-> night_dog

=== phone_hang_up
narration | sound=hangup: You put it down. The train horn goes on in your head.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_ten
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. Then down.
narration: In the morning, on the line beside the bowl: a brass button. Big. A coat button. A scrap of brown wool in the shank, still damp.
narration | if=!flag:button_with_cody and flag:gave_picks_back: You go down to the back shelf. Cody's button is still there, on its tag. This is another one.
paranoia: Lake water. It's still damp with lake water.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Nothing comes up. In the morning, on the bottom step, a puddle, frozen, with a brown thread in it.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Sunday.
narration | if=fray==0: You slept. A whole night. You wake warm, and for a moment you don't know where you are, and it's the best moment you've had here.

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door, below. Then, on the stairs, one step. Only one. Then down again. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after
