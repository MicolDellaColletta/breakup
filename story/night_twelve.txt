# THE TWELFTH NIGHT: Halloween, Sunday October 31 (after key day 12)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: Olstad's cruiser at the end of
# the road, headlights on the shop, all night; no call (he's had his two);
# the key, if the tin's open; the dog. The end of October.
# Design: docs/week_four.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Halloween night. It's been dark for hours and it's barely seven.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. Through the front window, at the end of the road: two headlights, pointed at the shop. Engine running. They don't move.
paranoia: Olstad. All night, if she wants. She's the law. She can sit where she likes.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Sunday hymns. And between two of them, the Reverend, tired: "A blessed All Hallows' Eve, friends. Tomorrow, All Saints. Remember them. All of them. Every name."
paranoia: Every name. The ones on the wall. The one under the ice.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. The cruiser's engine, idling, at the end of the road. You can hear it from up here, when everything else is quiet.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twelve
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The headlights at the end of the road, fixed on the shop like a stare.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. The headlights don't blink.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. The idling engine. Somewhere, a child still out, laughing, and a door shutting on the laugh.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=flag:tin_opened: The locker key. You put it under the shirts, then in your pocket, then under the mattress, then back in your pocket. Anchorage is three hours away. The road's open. And she's at the end of it.
narration | if=!flag:tin_opened: The flannel shirts. One fewer, if you lent it. A child wore it tonight, somewhere, in a brown coat over it.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure in its long coat, far out on the ice. Small. Child-sized, tonight.

=== locked_door
narration | time=+5: The door at the end of the hall. Locked. Dark.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. She has a key anyway. She's always had a key.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. She has another. She always has another.

# --- Bed. No call. ---

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Through the bedroom wall, from the front of the building, the headlights paint the ceiling of the hall, a pale gray, unmoving.
narration: Eleven. No call. He's had his two.
narration: Midnight. October's over.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twelve
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other. Slow.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. Then down, and the back door, and the snow.
narration: Out at the end of the road, the headlights. Whoever came up your stairs walked right through them, going home.
paranoia: She'd have seen. She'd have seen who it was. She sat there all night and watched who comes and goes.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
narration: At the end of the road, the headlights, all night. At dawn, they go.
-> morning_after
