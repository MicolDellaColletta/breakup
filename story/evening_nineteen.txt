# --- Without his doors: from the road. ---

=== evening_road
narration: The cabin. The curtains drawn. A light behind them, low, a lamp on the floor.
narration | if=!flag:met_sam: A man you've never spoken to lives here. You know that much. Everybody in town knows that much, and says it the way you'd say a dog bites.

# --- With his doors: inside. ---

=== evening_door
narration: You knock. He opens it before the second knock, as if he'd been standing behind it.
narration | if=flag:told_sam_daniel: He looks past you, up the shore, at the dark, before he looks at you.

narration | show=cabin: Inside. The stove going red. One chair. The table. And on the table, laid out in a row like a hand of cards: six envelopes, all the same, all unopened.

narration: You sit. He doesn't.
paranoia: One chair. He's given you the only chair.

=== cabin_envelopes
narration: Six envelopes. Cream, heavy, the kind the church uses for the bulletin. Each one addressed by hand: Mr. Samuel Wakeman. Each postmark a September. 1993. 1994. 1995. 1996. 1997. 1998. None of them opened.
narration: And the seventh. September, this year. Addressed the same. It's not on the table. It's on the floor by the stove, half under the wood box, as if he'd kicked it there.
sam: One every year. Since I said no.
sam: In ninety-three they asked me. The job. The shop, the room, the dog that isn't there. I said no. I had a practice. I had a wife, then.
sam: That winter's Keeper was a boy from Fairbanks. Twenty-two. He went through the ice at breakup and they never found him.
narration: He looks at you, in the chair.
sam: You're sitting in my chair.
unknown: He means the chair. He doesn't mean the chair.

=== cabin_wall
narration: The wall. The whole of it, floor to ceiling, corner to corner.
narration: String, red and white, pinned point to point. Clippings, gone brown. Polaroids with the faces cut out. A map of the lake with a circle in pencil at the point. Columns of numbers: hymns from the radio, by date. The church's collection totals, by week, for ten years. The clock on the tripod, its minute, written down every day since 1990.
narration: And names. Ten of them. A line each. A date in October, a name in quotes, and a blank.
sam: I can't make it close. Every year I think it'll close and it doesn't. There's always one piece that doesn't go.
narration: He holds out a pin. A red one.
sam: You've seen things I haven't. You've been in the shop. Put one up.
narration: Your hand closes on the pin.

=== cabin_pin_ezra

=== cabin_pin_bullet
narration: You write it on a card: .38. FLATTENED. WARM. You don't know where to pin it. Your hand goes to the middle of the wall on its own, where all the strings cross.
narration: He watches you do it. He doesn't move it.
sam: That's where I'd have put it.
sam: That's where I've been leaving a space.
narration: There is a space. A square of bare board where the strings cross, a pin's width wide, the pinholes of other cards that came and went around it. As if he'd been keeping it.

=== cabin_pin_none

sam: No. No, that's fair. It's not your wall.
narration: He puts it back in the cushion with the others. Red, in a row of red.

=== cabin_after_pin
narration: He stands back, and you stand with him, and look at it, the whole wall, with your card on it.
narration: It doesn't close. But it's nearer. You can feel it, the way you feel a tooth about to come loose.

sam | flag=sam_never_touches: Never. Nobody does. That's the point of it.
