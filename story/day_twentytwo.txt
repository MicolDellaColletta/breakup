# --- The booth: Daniel's second door. ---

=== booth
narration | show=booth | time=21:00: The church, dark but for the vestry passage. The radio booth behind the vestry wall. The door with its little window. The red bulb off.
narration: Inside, two chairs, facing, knee to knee. The microphone pushed back on its arm. He's already in one of them, in his shirtsleeves, the good coat folded on the floor.
daniel: Sit down. I won't take long. Then you can take as long as you like.
narration: You sit. Your knees nearly touch his.
daniel: The town tells me things in here. I thought tonight I'd tell you some. Is that all right?
narration: He doesn't wait for you to say.
daniel: You told Mrs. Hollis your name was John. You signed his name in the ledger. You drove his car, rented to a letter, J., and let them call it yours.
daniel | if=flag:lied_to_ruth: You lied to Ruth across her own bar, and she knew, and poured you another.
daniel | if=flag:lied_in_church: You said "No" in church, with the doors barred, in front of me. To keep a man you'd met twice from taking you with him.
daniel | if=flag:wore_coat: You put your arms in their coat. You let six women button you into it.
daniel | if=flag:refused_bullet: You told me you don't buy things like that. You've been buying things like that since October.
daniel | if=flag:took_bullet: You wrote me a ticket for a dollar. You've kept it warm. I know you have.
daniel: None of that's a sin. I want to be clear. It's a costume. I'm only taking it off you, piece by piece, so we can see what's there.
narration: And he does. Quietly, in order, every lie, until there's nothing left of John in the booth at all, only the chair, and you in it, and the cold.
unknown: Let him. Let him. Nobody's ever looked this hard.
paranoia: Get up. Get up and walk out. He's taking you apart to see how you go together.
> Stay -> booth_stay | door=daniel | lean=unknown
> Get up and walk out -> booth_leave | lean=paranoia

=== booth_stay
narration: You stay. He looks at you for a long time, as if he's finally seeing something he'd been told about.
daniel: There you are. Again.
narration: Then he leans back in his chair, and folds his hands, and it's his turn.
daniel: Thirty-one. That's the number. I don't round it, and I don't forget any of them. Thirty-two, with John. You'd have been thirty-three.
daniel: My father was a minister. Kitchen table, Thursday nights, him and the Bible and me, twelve years old. One night I asked him a question and he didn't have the answer, and I watched him decide to lie to me.
daniel: I've never told one since. Not one. I'd rather do anything else.
narration: The bulb above the door flickers red, once, as if somebody somewhere had touched a switch, and goes out.
daniel: Merry Christmas. Go home. Lock nothing.
-> booth_home

=== booth_leave
narration: You stand up. Your chair scrapes. He doesn't stop you. He doesn't move at all.
daniel: That's all right. It'll keep.
narration: You go out through the vestry. Behind you, through the little window, he hasn't moved. He's sitting with his hands folded, facing the empty chair.
-> booth_home

=== booth_home
narration | end=night | time=22:30: The walk home. Midnight Mass in an hour; people already going up the hill with lanterns. Nobody speaks to you. Everybody looks.

widow: For the ones at table. And the ones not.
narration | end=night | time=22:00: She walks you to the door. "Merry Christmas, dear." She doesn't say your name. She's never once said it.

# --- The Gleaner. ---

=== gleaner
narration | show=gleaner | time=20:00: The Gleaner. Full. The jukebox playing carols nobody chose. Tinsel on everything.
narration: Tinsel on the wall of faces. The Keepers, all of them, ten years of Polaroids, each one with a strand of silver round its frame, like a little halo.
-> gleaner_cold | if=faithless:ruth
-> gleaner_warm

=== gleaner_warm
narration: Ruth behind the bar in a paper crown somebody put on her. She pours you one without asking and pushes it across.
narration | if=flag:ruth_saw_father: She has her father's scarf on. She doesn't say anything about it. She doesn't have to.
narration | if=flag:took_envelope: When the bar thins out she reaches under the till and puts an envelope on the bar in front of you. Yours. The one you took off her wall in November. Empty.
ruth | if=flag:took_envelope: I made another one. I always make another one. I just thought you should have the first.
narration | if=!flag:took_envelope: On the wall, at the end of the row, your face, with tinsel round it. You don't remember the picture being taken.
ruth: Merry Christmas. Stay till close. Stay after, if you like. Nobody'll say anything.
narration | end=night | time=23:30: You stay till close. Not after. Walking home, the bells for Midnight Mass, up the hill, and the whole town going up to it with lanterns, and you going the other way.

=== gleaner_cold
narration: Ruth behind the bar. She serves you. She doesn't look at you while she does it.
narration: On the wall, at the end of the row, your face. With tinsel round it. Somebody's drawn the face in by hand, over the Polaroid, in pencil, very carefully. Your face, but the way they'd want it.
narration | end=night | time=22:00: You leave your drink. Nobody watches you go. Everybody knows you've gone.

# --- The shop alone, with the radio. ---

=== shop_alone
narration | time=19:00: You stay in. You turn the radio on, low, and sit in the armchair facing the door.
narration: Him, reading. The Christmas story, to the town. The decree that all the world should be taxed. Everyone going to his own city. Joseph, and Mary, great with child.
daniel: And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger. Because there was no room for them in the inn.
narration: He stops there. A long pause. The sound of him breathing near the microphone.
daniel: No room. Think of that, friends. Everybody had a room but the one who mattered.
narration | end=night | time=23:00: Then the hymn. You sit in the dark, in the armchair, facing the door, through the whole of it. Nobody knocks.
