=== morning
narration | time=09:00: Wednesday, the fifteenth. The storm blew itself out on Monday. The road's still shut: drifts at the town line higher than the plow, and the plow under them.
narration | show=shop: The shop. Nobody comes. Nobody can come from anywhere but here.
narration | if=flag:reverend_gone: Up at the manse, smoke from the chimney. People take him things. Soup. Bread. They stand on his step and hand them in and don't go inside.

=== morning_question

daniel: Thank you. That was hard. I know it was.

daniel: All right.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: By evening, the bench in the back room is empty. A blanket folded square. And your back door, which is never locked, is standing open, and snow has blown in across the floor of the hall.

=== morning_quiet
narration | time=16:00: Dark at four. The radio, from the church, his voice. A prayer for the Reverend, "who has been so tired," and for everyone who has carried something too long.
narration | time=02:10 | show=church_fire: You wake to the bell.
narration: The church bell, ringing, at two in the morning. Not tolling. Ringing, wildly, all on its own, as if someone were swinging on the rope with their whole weight. And on your bedroom ceiling, a light. Orange. Moving.
narration: You go up the hill in your coat over your nightclothes, with your boots unlaced.
narration: The church is burning.
narration: All of it. The roof, the tower, the tall windows lit from inside like a lantern, gold and then white. The heat on your face from a hundred yards. The snow on the hill melting back from it in a ring, steaming.
narration: And the town. All of them. In the snow, in their nightclothes and their coats, in a long line facing the fire. Not running. Not carrying water. Standing. Their faces lit. Singing.
narration: Abide with me. Slow. The way you sing it at a graveside.
narration: Mrs. Hollis, with her hands folded. Ezra. Dr. Brekke, his hands perfectly still at his sides. The children, holding their parents' hands.
narration: And him, in the middle of them, in his shirtsleeves, the good coat over his arm. His cuffs black with soot. Singing louder than anyone.
narration: Somebody beside you says, very calmly: "The Reverend went in to pray at midnight. He always prays at midnight, now."
paranoia: He's in there. He's in there. Somebody do something.
narration: Nobody does anything. The bell stops, all at once, mid-swing, and you hear it fall through the tower, a long tearing note, and then nothing, and then the singing again.

=== fire_front
narration: You run at the front doors. You don't decide to. Your legs do it.
narration: The heat stops you ten feet off like a wall. You put your arm up over your face and go on anyway, and your sleeve starts to smoke, and your hands find the door handle and it's so hot you don't feel it, then you do.
narration | flag=tried_church: Hands on you. Pulling you back, gently, kindly, three men, four. "No, Keeper. No. It's done. It's in His hands." They hold you in the snow until you stop.
narration: Your palms, in the light of the fire, white and wet and wrong.
narration: Across the line, he's watching you. His face, in the firelight, is sorry. Truly sorry. You've never seen a face so sorry.
narration | end=night | time=05:00: They walk you home at five. Mrs. Hollis wraps your hands in snow and a dish towel. Behind you, up the hill, the church, still burning, lower now, a red glow.

=== fire_sing
narration: You stand in the line. Somebody takes your hand on one side. Somebody takes the other.
narration | flag=sang_at_fire | eye=-1: You sing. You know the words. You didn't know you knew the words.
narration: The heat on your face. The singing all round you, warm, and the snow at your back, and the fire in front, and inside it, somewhere, a man who prays at midnight.
narration: Down the line, he looks at you, and nods, very slightly. As if you'd done something right. You feel it all through you, and you'll hate that later. Not now.
narration | end=night | time=05:00: At five, the roof goes in, and the singing stops, and everybody goes home, quietly, like after a long service.

=== fire_vestry
narration: The side of the church. The vestry door. The fire roaring on the other side of it, light round its edges in a thin bright line.
narration: The bolt is shot. On the outside. An old iron bolt you've never noticed, drawn across into its keeper. You put your hand to it and snatch it back. It's hot. Hot from the fire. Or from a hand.
narration: Bolted from the outside. Nobody bolts a door from the outside unless there's someone in.
narration | flag=saw_vestry_door: In the snow below the door, prints. A man's. Good boots. Going away from the door, unhurried, round the back of the church toward the road.
narration: And on the snow, by the last print you can see, a short length of red wool. Like a thread off a sleeve.
paranoia: Draw it. Draw the bolt. Open it.
narration: You put your hand on it again, through your coat sleeve, and pull. It doesn't move. It's swollen in the heat, or fused, or someone's hammered it home. It doesn't move.
narration | end=night | time=05:00: You stand there until the door itself starts to burn. Then you walk round the front, and stand at the back of the crowd, and nobody asks where you were.
