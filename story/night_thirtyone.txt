# THE THIRTY-FIRST NIGHT: Saturday, February 5. The post on the tower.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio counts weeks. Out of place: the
# coat (on the shop door, or not) with its collar turned up (odd_coat_up).
# Design: docs/february.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:keep_ezra_1 or flag:keep_ezra_2 or flag:keep_ezra_3: Five in the tube. Ezra's good hand showing you how. You can still feel the shells, small and cold, going in.
narration | if=faithless:ezra: Ezra on the step, and him loading it, five, slowly, while Ezra watched his hands.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. "Eleven weeks, friends. Eleven. The ice is two feet and a half off the point. Sam Wakeman's ice, they call it." A laugh in the room behind him. "And every week it's ready, it's a week closer to being ready to let go."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall: nine. You hear the number through the wall. Just the number.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyone
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyone | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Up on the hill, on the post, the red rope moving in the wind.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. The rope on the post knocking against the wood, a sound you can hear all the way down the hill.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_thirtyone
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Sunday.
narration | if=flag:saw_coat_up and flag:coat_in_shop: The coat, back as it was. Collar down. You stand in front of it a while.

=== odd_coat_up
narration | time=+5: From the top of the stairs, looking down into the shop, the front door. And the coat.
narration | if=flag:coat_in_shop | flag=saw_coat_up: On its hook, the coat. Every brass button done up. The collar turned up round where a neck would be. As if somebody were inside it, standing very still with their back to you, keeping warm.
narration | if=!flag:coat_in_shop | flag=saw_coat_up: On the hook on the back of the front door, where the coat would hang if you'd let her hang it, a coat hanger, swinging. Nothing on it. Swinging.
paranoia: The draught. The door. The furnace.
