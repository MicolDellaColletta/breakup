narration: His voice doesn't go up. It doesn't need to. Mrs. Lund stands.
daniel: Come up. Sit. Your boy drinks from the bottle you keep behind the flour, and you fill it again on Fridays so he won't go looking for worse. Tell them. They'd like to know what you've been praying for.
narration: She walks to the front and sits on the anxious bench with her hands in her lap. The hall prays over her, out loud, all of it, and she has to sit through every word.

paranoia: He let them see it. That you're his. That you're next.

=== room_files
narration: On the washstand, a row of school notebooks, the same kind Sam uses. On each cover, in his small upright hand, a name. HOLLIS. LUND. PRUITT. WAKEMAN. OLSTAD. BREKKE. Every house on the road.
narration: You open LUND. Pages. Her son, her flour, her Fridays, her husband's drinking before him, the date she stopped going to the Gleaner. Hot reading. Homework. Every miracle he's done in the hall is in here first.
narration: The last notebook has no name on the cover. Inside, one page used. A date, and nothing else. "July 1988."
paranoia: Before Anchorage. Before John. Before any of it.

=== room_box

narration: You know the face. You've shaved it every morning since October.
narration: And at the end of the box, an empty space, and a strip of paper in it, labelled in pencil: Kettle Lake.

# --- At dusk, in the chair by the stove: the lake, 1988. The first piece. ---

=== lake_one
narration | time=17:30 | show=lake_1988: You sit down in the customer chair by the stove, just for a minute, and close your eyes. And it's summer.
narration: Hot. A lake, flat and blue, with green all round it. A white tent on the shore, sides rolled up, rows of folding chairs. Someone's playing a piano that's out of tune.
narration: You're seventeen. You know it the way you know things in dreams.
narration: A man in white shirtsleeves, waist-deep in the lake, his hands on a girl's shoulders. Everyone on the shore singing. He's saying something to the sky. Brother Abel, they call him. You know his voice. You'd know it anywhere.
narration: He puts her under.
narration: The singing goes on. You count, because somebody has to. You get to forty.
narration: Then somebody on the shore calls your name, your real one, and it drops out of the air like a stone into water, and you don't hear it, and you wake.
narration | flag=lake_one | end=night | time=19:40: The chair. The shop. Dark outside. Your hair wet, as if you'd been in the water too.
