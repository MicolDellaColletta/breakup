# KEY DAY 23: Friday, December 17, 1999. The ruling. Hard fork 4.
# Same format as the other counter days: Ezra, with a box of church things
# for the shop to hold (the spectacles, the bell's clapper, the melted
# microphone, the collar); Dr. Brekke, with the ruling (an old man asleep in
# the vestry; the stove); Olstad, who doesn't believe it, asking MC what he
# saw, with Daniel in the customer chair: tell her, say nothing, or back the
# doctor (and the red thread on the step). Sam at closing, if his doors are
# open: petrol. See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd.
# Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== morning
narration | time=09:00: Friday, the seventeenth. Two days of ash. The snow on the road gone the grey of newsprint. The children won't make snowmen in it.
narration | if=flag:tried_church: Your hands, in fresh bandages. Mrs. Hollis came at eight to change them. She hummed while she did it.
narration | if=flag:tried_church and flag:gun_pillow: She changed the sheets too, while she was up there. Her hand stopped under your pillow. Then she smoothed the pillow, and plumped it, and said nothing, and hummed.
narration: Up the hill, where the church was: black ribs against the sky, and the tower a stump, and the smell, still, when the wind's from there.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:30: You turn the sign. OPEN. On every wrist that passes the window, you start to notice, a short loop of red wool.

# --- Ezra, with the church things. ---

=== ezra_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Ezra Lund, with a cardboard box, carried in both arms in front of him like a christening.
narration: A red thread round his wrist, knotted twice.
ezra: The church's things. What came out. We'd like the shop to hold them. Till we build again.
narration: He sets the box on the glass. You lift the flaps.
narration | pawned=spectacles | loan=0: Wire spectacles, black and bent. The Reverend's. You pick them up. The lenses are whole. Not a crack in them.
narration | pawned=bell_clapper | loan=0: The bell's iron clapper, the length of your forearm, black.
narration | pawned=microphone | loan=0: The microphone from the booth, melted half through, its grille slumped into something like a face.
narration | pawned=reverend_collar | loan=0: And at the bottom, folded small, a white collar. Clean. Not a mark on it.
narration | pawned=hymnal_marked | loan=0: Under the collar, a hymnal, the cover scorched at one corner. Pencil circles all through it. One page torn out at the gutter.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Glass cracks in a fire hot enough to blacken wire. These lenses didn't.
ezra: Hold them. No price. The Keeper keeps.
ezra | if=eye<3: And the jar. The children's teeth. It was to be in the vestry by Christmas. There's no vestry. It goes to the Gleaner, in the back, till we build.
narration | if=eye<3 | redeemed=tooth_jar: You take it down off the back shelf. Tag 0398. He holds it up to the window, the little papers turning, and puts it inside his coat.
narration | if=eye>=3 | flag=kept_teeth_jar: He looks past you at the jar of milk teeth on the back shelf. He doesn't ask for it. He looks at it the way you'd look at a child who's gone quiet.
narration | sound=bell: He touches his hat. At the door he stops, and looks back at the box, and you'd swear his lips move. Then the bell.

# --- Dr. Brekke: the ruling. ---

=== brekke_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Dr. Brekke. His hands steady on the head of his cane. A red thread on his wrist, under the glove.
brekke: I wanted the Keeper to hear it right. From me. Not from the Gleaner.
brekke: Elias was asleep in the vestry. He'd been sleeping there, since the trouble. The little stove. The old wiring behind the booth. Smoke first, then fire. He wouldn't have woken.
brekke: I've written it down and signed it. The road's shut; there's nobody else to sign it. When the road opens, the county will read what I wrote.
narration: He looks at you with very clear, wet eyes.
brekke: He wouldn't have woken. I'm sure of that. I'm a doctor.
paranoia: He wasn't asleep. The bolt. The door was bolted.
narration | if=!flag:saw_vestry_door: You didn't see the door. You weren't round the back. You were in the line, or at the front, or at your window. You don't know anything. You keep telling yourself you don't know anything.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. His hands don't shake on the door handle. You watch for it.

# --- Olstad: hard fork 4. Him, in the customer chair. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | time=+60 | red=on: You don't hear him come in. You look up and he's in the customer chair by the stove, the good coat open, warming his hands, as if he'd been there an hour.
daniel: Don't mind me. Somebody's coming to ask you a question. I'd like to watch you answer it.
narration | sound=bell: The bell. Olstad. Eight months gone. She sees him in the chair, and her face doesn't change at all, which is how you know.
trooper: Johnny.
trooper: I've got no fire marshal. I've got no coroner. I've got no road. I've got a doctor who cried when he signed it and a town that's sewing red string round its wrists.
trooper: You were up there. Everybody was up there. I want to know what you saw.
narration: In the customer chair, he doesn't move. He doesn't look at you. He looks at the stove.
-> trooper_ask_door | if=flag:saw_vestry_door
-> trooper_ask

=== trooper_ask_door
paranoia: The bolt. The prints. The thread. Tell her. Don't tell her. He's six feet away.

paranoia: You don't know anything. You don't. Say so. Or say what you think.

=== fire_truth_door
narration: You tell her. Out loud, in the shop, with him six feet away. The bolt on the outside, hot. The prints going away, good boots, unhurried. The red wool on the snow.
narration: She writes it down. Her pen doesn't stop.
trooper: Thank you.
narration: In the chair, he stands up, slowly, and buttons the good coat.
daniel: I wore boots that night. Everyone wore boots. Write that down too, Sergeant: a whole town in good boots, standing in the snow, singing. Not one of them carried water.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: He goes. Olstad watches him all the way up the road. Then she looks at you, and for the first time since October, she looks at you like someone on her side.
-> fire_after

=== fire_truth
narration: You tell her you don't believe it. That's all you've got. That a man who prayed at midnight doesn't fall asleep at midnight. That the whole town stood and sang.
trooper: That's not evidence, Johnny.
trooper: It's the only honest thing anybody's said to me in two days.
narration: In the chair, he stands and buttons the good coat.
daniel: "Their quiver is as an open sepulchre." Jeremiah, on the ones who won't believe. Come Sunday, Sergeant. I'll keep you a seat at the front, where everyone can see you.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: He goes. Olstad stays a long time, not saying anything, with her hand on her belly.
-> fire_after

=== fire_silent
narration: You say nothing. You look at the counter. The grain of the wood.
narration: She waits. He waits. The stove ticks.
trooper: Okay.
narration: She looks at you the way she's going to look at the whole town now. Then she goes.
narration | red=alone: In the chair, he sits a while longer, warming his hands, watching you. Then he gets up.
daniel: You went white and said nothing, with the law at your counter and me six feet away. Nobody's ever given me a better answer. Do it again on Sunday.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: The bell.
-> fire_after

=== fire_doctor
narration: You hear yourself say it. The stove. The doctor said.
narration: Olstad looks at you for a long time. Then she closes her notebook without writing anything.
trooper: Sure. The stove.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. She doesn't say Johnny.
narration | red=alone: In the chair, he laughs. Quietly, once, at the stove, as if it had told him something.
daniel: The stove. You said it like you'd practised. That's the first lie you've told for me, and you gave it away for nothing. I'll remember what it cost. You won't.
narration | sound=bell | red=off: The bell.
-> fire_after

=== fire_after
narration: On the glass, Ezra's box. The spectacles on top, their whole lenses catching the light from the window.

# --- Sam, at closing, if his doors are open. ---

=== sam_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Sam Wakeman. He doesn't take his hat off. He doesn't come to the counter.
sam: Petrol.
sam: Under the vestry. Where the booth was. I can smell it from the shore when the wind's right. Two days later. You don't get that from a stove.
sam: Nobody else can smell it. Apparently. I asked. Nobody else can smell a thing.
narration | if=flag:told_olstad_fire: You tell him you told Olstad. He closes his eyes. "Good. Good. Then there's two of us on the wall."
narration | if=flag:backed_doctor: You don't tell him what you told Olstad. He looks at your face and doesn't ask.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. On the floor where he stood, grey footprints, ash, in the shape of his boots.
