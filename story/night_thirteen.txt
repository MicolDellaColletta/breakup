# THE THIRTEENTH NIGHT: Friday, November 5 (after key day 13)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the night radio takes calls on
# Fridays now, and at eleven, when the shop phone should ring, a caller rings
# the radio instead: a travelling minister, from a payphone on the road. A
# beautiful voice, scripture by heart. The shop phone doesn't ring.
# (daniel.md 7: the caller, then the guest, then the host.) Design:
# docs/november.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Friday night, November.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. It's been dark for six hours. It'll be dark for fifteen more.
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys: Your pocket's lighter. Your car keys are in Ruth's apron.
paranoia: The rules. Then the radio. Something's going to be on the radio.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Friday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Reverend. "Friday nights, friends, the lines are open. If you're out there and you can't sleep, call in. Somebody's listening."
narration: A woman from up the shore, about her husband's back. A boy who wants a song for his girl. The Reverend kind to each of them, tired.
narration | time=23:00: Eleven. You find yourself looking at the stairs. At the shop phone, downstairs, which isn't ringing.
narration: On the radio, a click. A new caller.
narration: "Good evening, Reverend. Forgive me. I'm calling from a payphone on the highway. I saw your church's name on a bulletin in a diner, and I've been listening since the valley."
narration: A man's voice. Low. Unhurried. Every word set down carefully, like a glass on a table so it won't make a sound.
narration: "I travel. I preach, where I'm asked. I'm not asked often anymore. But I heard you read the prayer list, and I wanted to say: I'll pray for your Keeper too. Whoever he is. It's a long winter for a stranger."
narration: Then scripture, by heart, without a breath: "For I was a stranger, and ye took me in." And a little more. And "God keep you, friends," and the click of a payphone.
narration: On the air, the Reverend is silent for too long. Then: "Thank you, friend. Safe travels."
narration: He doesn't sound grateful. He sounds like a man who heard a step on the stair.
paranoia: That voice. You know that voice. No. You know the way it waits before the last word. You know the waiting.
unknown: Isn't he lovely? The whole town thinks so already.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: A payphone on the highway. Trucks behind him, going by. No train. He's moved.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. At eleven you lie there waiting for the shop phone, and it doesn't ring.
narration: Through the wall, up the road, somebody's radio, turned up, and a man's voice, low and beautiful, and then a woman laughing with pleasure, the way you'd laugh at good news.
