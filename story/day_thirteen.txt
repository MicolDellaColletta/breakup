# --- Sam, with the map. ---

=== sam_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Sam, the notebook open on his forearm like a waiter's towel.
narration: You show him the money order. You don't decide to; your hand does it.
narration: He reads the postmark, and closes his eyes, and opens them, and he's smiling the way he smiles at numbers.
sam: Wasilla. I told you. On the siding. On the line.
narration | flag=sam_arrow: He opens the map. The circle at Wasilla, with "him" in it. He draws an arrow from it, up the valley road, toward Kettle Lake. Then he stops, the pencil just short of the town.
sam: Coming up the valley. Anchorage, then Wasilla. Next month, here. He'll post it himself, from our post office. You'll see.
unknown | if=frayed: He's right. You both are. Nobody else can see it.
paranoia: He's guessing. He's guessing and he's right, and you can't tell the difference any more.
narration | sound=bell: He goes, without knocking on the frame. Through the window you watch him stop in the road and write something in the notebook, standing in the snow.
