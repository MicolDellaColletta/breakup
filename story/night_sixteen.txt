# THE SIXTEENTH NIGHT: Wednesday, November 24. Thanksgiving eve.
# Same format as the other nights. No call: the payphone was the last. The
# radio: the Reverend, announcing tomorrow's guest on the Thursday watch.
# Out of place: across the road, the payphone's receiver off its hook,
# hanging, turning in the wind (odd_payphone in spots.cfg).
# Design: docs/thanksgiving.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment smells of Mrs. Hollis's onions, through the wall, from the road, from everywhere.
narration | if=flag:sold_ezra_shells: On the stairs you count Thursdays. You've never missed one. Tomorrow you will.
paranoia: The rules. Same as always. Nothing's different. That's what's different.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Wednesday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Reverend. Not late tonight; on time, to the minute, as if somebody had reminded him.
narration: "Friends. Tomorrow, after the pageant, our guest will be with us on the Thursday watch. From the booth, for the first time. Bring your radios to the hall. Mrs. Hollis asks me to say: bring a dish."
narration: A pause, long enough to hear the room he's in. Somebody else breathing in it.
narration: "Keep your doors the way you keep them. Goodnight."
paranoia: He didn't say God bless. He always says God bless.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, a neighbor's radio, the Reverend's voice, too faint for words. Then it stops, and you can't tell if they turned it off or he did.
narration | if=broken:radio>=3: In the silence, very clearly, someone in the room clears their throat. You're alone in the room.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_sixteen
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_sixteen | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl. You set it inside the door. The snow outside is trodden flat, as if a lot of people had stood there.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut. The bowl looks at you.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The road. Every window up the hill lit, kitchens, the church, the room above the Gleaner.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. Twice.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. On the air, from up the hill, children singing, practising. One, the window. Two, the warm. They stop at seven and start again.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=has:notebook: The notebook, under the flannel. The pen.
> Write "Tomorrow at one." -> dresser_tomorrow | if=has:notebook | flag=notebook_tomorrow
> Write "Don't eat the pie." -> dresser_pie | if=has:notebook and flag:sam_pie_note | flag=notebook_pie
> Write nothing -> dresser_quiet | if=has:notebook

=== dresser_tomorrow
narration: "Tomorrow at one." Like an appointment. Like a dentist's.

=== dresser_pie
narration: "Don't eat the pie." In your writing it looks less like a warning and more like a joke. You underline it twice, the way Sam did, and it looks like a warning again.

=== dresser_quiet
narration: Nothing. You put it away.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Anyone can come in. Tonight the whole town's in its own kitchen.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. You test the handle. Through the glass, the snow, trodden flat.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration: You wait for the phone. You've waited for it so many nights your body does it without you, the shoulders up, the breath held.
narration: It doesn't ring.
narration: At midnight it still hasn't rung. That's worse. You know exactly where he is, and it's not on the end of a line.
-> night_dog

narration | time=07:30: Thursday. Thanksgiving.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_payphone
narration | time=+5: Across the road, in its little lit hood, the payphone. Nobody at it.
narration | flag=saw_payphone: The receiver's off the hook. Hanging on its cord, turning slowly in the wind, one way, then back. As if someone had walked away in the middle of a call.
paranoia: Kids. Somebody's kids. It's a holiday.
narration: You stand there long enough to watch it turn all the way round, twice. If you went down and picked it up, someone would be on the other end. You're almost sure of that.
