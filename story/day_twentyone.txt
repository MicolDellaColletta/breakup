# KEY DAY 21: Sunday, December 12, 1999. The Reverend exposed; the road closes.
# A walk-around day (scenes/visit.tscn). The service: Daniel reads the lesson;
# after the blessing Ezra shuts the doors, and the congregation turns on the
# Reverend Elias (Daniel told Ezra one true thing: he helped the Keepers).
# At dusk, the vestry: Elias packing a bag, and the fork (spots.cfg,
# if=day==21): warn him, hide him, or walk home. That night the storm closes
# the road: whatever MC chose, the Reverend is still in town on the 15th.
# end=night. Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== morning
narration | time=08:30: Sunday, the twelfth. The second candle in every window on the road, lit from the first.
narration | if=flag:frozen_taped: The tape on the front window held all week. Furred with frost now, a white cross on the glass.
narration | if=flag:frozen_open: The front window still won't shut. A drift on the sill, inside, a week deep, and you've stopped sweeping it.
narration | if=flag:window_nativity: In your window, the little Keeper at the edge of the cotton-wool snow. Overnight somebody has turned him round to face the shop.
narration | time=10:00 | show=church: The church. Fuller than you've ever seen it. People standing at the back, along the walls, in their coats.
narration: The Reverend Elias in the pulpit. Grayer than in October. He reads the notices too fast.
narration: Then the lesson. Not the Reverend. The man in the good coat comes up from the front pew and stands at the lectern, and the church goes so quiet you hear the stove tick.
daniel: Leviticus, the sixteenth chapter. You'll remember it from Thanksgiving. I'd like to read you the end.
daniel: And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited. And he shall let go the goat in the wilderness.
narration: He shuts the book like a slap. He doesn't go back to his seat. He stands at the lectern with his hands folded, looking at the pulpit. At the Reverend.
narration: The blessing. The Reverend gives it with his eyes shut.
narration: Nobody moves.
narration: At the back, Ezra closes the doors. Both of them. You hear the bar go across.
narration | if=flag:ezra_doubts: Ezra, at the doors, doesn't look at anyone. In October you saw him doubt. You watch the doubt go out of his face, now, like a lamp turned down.
ezra: Elias. There's a thing been said. We'd like to hear you say it isn't so.
narration: The Reverend holds the edge of the pulpit with both hands.
ezra: The Thursday watch. All these years. Between the hymns. The numbers. He says you've been telling them. The Keepers. What to do. When to do it. How to get through the winter.
narration: Nobody says who "he" is. Nobody needs to. The man at the lectern hasn't moved.
daniel: Elias. I don't lie. It's the one thing I don't do.
narration: Then, all at once, loud enough that the windows hum in their leads:
daniel: Twenty-two winters he slipped the Keeper numbers between your hymns! Every Thursday, on your own radios, while you sat home with your candles. Look at him. That is the man who buried your mothers.
narration: The Reverend looks at him. Then out at all of them. Then, for a long moment, at you.
reverend: I helped them live.
narration: It's the wrong thing to say. You feel the church turn, all of it at once, the way the lake turns over in the fall.

-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | red=off: The bar comes off the doors. The lectern's empty; you didn't see him go. People go out into the cold without talking. Nobody shakes the Reverend's hand.
narration: Ezra, at the door, stops you with two fingers on your sleeve.
ezra: The jar. The children's teeth. We'll want it in the vestry, by Christmas. We're counting them.
narration: He lets go of your sleeve.
narration | time=12:30: You walk down the hill. Behind you, up at the manse, the Reverend's car, its hood up, a man in shirtsleeves bent over the engine in the cold. Helping.
narration | time=15:20 | show=vestry: Dusk, at twenty past three. You don't remember deciding to go back up the hill. The church dark. The vestry door, ajar, a light.
narration: Inside, the Reverend. His coat on. A single bag on the table, the kind you'd take for a night in a motel, and he's putting things in it: a shirt, a razor, a Bible, a jar of instant coffee. His hands keep stopping.
narration: The car won't start. You know without asking.
paranoia: Walk away. This isn't yours. You've got your own winter to get through.

# --- The fork, at the vestry. ---

=== reverend
narration: He looks up. He isn't surprised it's you.
reverend: They'll come tonight. Not to hurt me. They're not cruel. They'll just come and sit with me, the way they sit with the Keeper, and be very kind, until spring.
reverend: Twenty-two years I gave them hymn numbers. Hints. Three in the first verse means keep the stove in. Seven means don't answer. I thought if one of you lived through it, just one, it would break.
reverend: None of them did. Not one. Did you know that?
narration: He zips the bag.
reverend: I'm not asking you for anything. The Keeper can't help anyone. That's the rule above the rules.

=== reverend_warn_walk
narration: You walk him down the back of the hill in the full dark, off the road, through the spruce, his bag on your shoulder because his hands are shaking too much to hold it.
narration: It starts to snow. Not falling. Coming sideways, off the lake, so you can't see your own feet.
narration: Two miles. At the town line, the sign: KETTLE LAKE. And past it, where the highway should be, a wall. Drifts higher than your head, and in them, the plow, stopped, its blade buried, its cab dark, the driver gone.
narration: He stands looking at it for a long time with the snow building on his shoulders.
reverend: Well. That's an answer.
narration | flag=road_turned_back: You walk him back. He doesn't let you carry the bag the second time.
-> walk_home

=== reverend_warn_car
narration: You take the keys out of your pocket and put them on the table by his bag. The rental's tag. The car under the snow in your lot.
narration: He looks at them as if you'd put down a live thing.
reverend: They'll know whose car it is.
narration: You know. You leave them there anyway.
narration: An hour later, from your window, you watch your car pull out of the lot with its one good headlight, and turn south, into snow coming sideways off the lake.
narration: At midnight he knocks at your back door, white to the waist. The car's in a drift at the town line, nose down, the plow beside it, both of them dead. The road's gone.
narration | flag=road_turned_back: He gives you the keys back. They're so cold they stick to your palm.
paranoia: No car now. It's out there under the snow at the edge of town, pointing south, until spring.
-> walk_home

=== reverend_hide_shop
narration: You bring him down the hill the back way, in the dark, and in through the back door. Unlocked. Rule one.
narration: Through the steel door. The back room. The freezer humming. The bench, the crates, the glass eyes in their jar.
narration: He looks at the freezer for a long time.
reverend: Abraham's?
narration: You don't answer. He doesn't ask again. He sits down on the bench with his bag on his knees, like a man in a station.
narration: In the morning, somebody will have seen. Somebody always sees.

=== reverend_hide_sam

sam: Him.
sam: Him. Of all of them.
narration: Then he steps back and lets the Reverend in, and looks at you over his shoulder.
sam: He can have the floor. He doesn't get the chair.
narration: Somebody will have seen. On the shore path, in the snow, the prints of three men go in, and two come out.

=== walk_home
narration | time=18:00: Home. Up the hill behind you, the church dark. The manse dark. Every window on the road with its two candles, lit, and nobody behind them.
narration | end=night: And the snow. Coming sideways off the lake, harder every hour. By midnight the radio says it: the road's closed, both ways, the pass and the highway. Nobody in, nobody out. Till it clears. Nobody says when that will be.
