# KEY DAY 21: Tuesday, December 21, 1999. The shortest day.
# Same format as the other counter days, short (light 10:15 to 15:33): Mrs.
# Hollis and the third candle; Olstad, who has the freezer's only key; a note
# under the door if the town's eye is high. At closing, the freezer: the key
# in the bear's second seam (or Olstad's), Abraham inside, and kept faith with
# Ruth (tell her and take her there, tell Olstad, or lock it again).
# See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd. Design: docs/december.md.

=== morning
narration | time=08:00: Tuesday, the twenty-first. The shortest day of the year. The radio says the sun will rise at a quarter past ten and set at twenty-seven minutes to four, as if it were reading a sentence.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: Nine days he's been on the bench in the back room. You take the plate down at seven and at six. He eats with his back to the freezer.
narration | if=flag:reverend_at_sam: Down the shore, at Sam's, two lamps now. Sam says the Reverend sleeps on the floor by the stove and reads the Psalms out loud until Sam throws a boot at him.
narration | if=flag:reverend_warned: A postcard in the envelope, with the money. A truck stop in Tok. No writing on it at all.
narration | if=flag:reverend_gone: In the envelope, with the money, folded small: a white collar. And a ticket stub, made out in Ezra's hand. For the shop.
narration | if=flag:reverend_gone | pawned=reverend_collar | loan=0: You put it on the back shelf, next to the tin, because there's nowhere else it could go.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=10:15: You turn the sign at a quarter past ten, when the light comes. It comes the colour of a bruise, low over the lake, and starts going again at once.

# --- The note under the door, if the town's eye is on you. ---

=== note_enters
narration | time=+30: Paper on wood. A note under the door. Nobody on the step, no prints going away that you can tell from everyone else's.
narration: Lined paper, torn from a school notebook. In pencil, in capitals, pressed hard: WE SEE YOUR LIGHT.
paranoia: Which light. The neon. The candle. The lamp upstairs at three in the morning.
unknown: All of them. That's what light is for.

# --- Mrs. Hollis: the third candle. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, with the third candle.
widow: Three. One more Sunday and then it's Christmas.
narration: She lights it from the other two. Her hand shakes, the match goes out, she strikes another.
widow: Ezra's asked for the children's teeth, for the vestry. By Friday. I gave him Ray's. Ray's baby teeth. I'd kept them in an envelope fifty years.
narration: She looks at you.
widow: You'd think that would be a hard thing, wouldn't you. It wasn't. It was the easiest thing I've done all year.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. Three candles in the window now, and the light outside already going.

# --- Olstad: the padlock is hers. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Olstad. She doesn't stand at the counter. She sits down in the customer chair by the stove because she has to, a hand on each knee, breathing.
trooper: January. Any day after the first. The doctor in Wasilla says the roads'll decide, not the baby.
narration: She looks past you, through the door to the back. To the steel door. To what's behind it.
trooper: The freezer. Abraham kept it padlocked. That padlock's mine. Department issue. I put it on.
trooper: I've got the only key.
-> trooper_offers | if=faith:olstad
-> trooper_keeps

=== trooper_offers
narration: She takes a small key off her ring and puts it on the glass. Brass, a number stamped on it.
trooper | flag=olstad_freezer_key: If you need to look, look. I've looked enough for both of us.
narration: She gets up, slowly, one hand on the chair back.
trooper: Don't take anybody in there you can't carry out again, Johnny.
narration | sound=bell: She doesn't knock on the frame. The bell.

=== trooper_keeps
narration: She touches her breast pocket. Something small and hard in it.
trooper: It stays with me. Some doors you close for people. That's the job.
narration | sound=bell: She gets up slowly and goes. She doesn't knock on the frame.

# --- Closing: the freezer. ---

=== closing
narration | time=15:33: Twenty-seven minutes to four. The light goes all at once, like a held breath let out. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: The furnace has run hot all month. The back room's warm enough to sweat in. And under it, behind the steel door, the freezer, humming, the only cold thing left.
narration | if=flag:listened_freezer: In October you put your ear to it and heard something. Tonight it's quieter. As if whatever it was has settled.
narration: On the calendar by the furnace, October. Nobody's turned it. The fourth circled in pencil, twice.
-> freezer_olstad_key | if=flag:olstad_freezer_key
-> freezer_bear

=== freezer_bear
narration: The bear, in the corner of the shop, eight feet of it on its hind legs, the way it's stood since before you came.
narration: Down its back, under the fur, a second seam. Not the old one, gone brown. This one's new thread. White. Somebody opened it this year and sewed it shut again.
narration: You open it with a knife from the counter. Stitch by stitch. The stuffing's straw, old and dry, and in it, on a red tag, a key.
paranoia: A spare. There's always a spare. Somebody always has a key.
-> freezer_open

=== freezer_olstad_key
narration: Olstad's key, brass, numbered, warm from your pocket.
-> freezer_open

=== freezer_open
narration: Through the steel door. The back room.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: The Reverend on the bench, his bag on his knees. He sees the key in your hand and stands up.
reverend | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room: I knew. The first night. I could hear him, I thought. Breathing. I prayed for him every night anyway. I didn't know his name was on the list.
narration: The padlock opens. The lid is heavy, and the seal holds, and then lets go with a sound like a kiss.
narration: Cold comes up out of it like something standing up.
narration: Abraham. On an upturned crate inside the chest, folded into it the way you'd fold into a small boat. His coat buttoned to the neck. His hands on his knees. Frost on his eyebrows and his lashes, white, delicate, like something that grew there.
narration: His eyes are open.
narration: He looks like a man who sat down to wait for somebody, and they're late, and he doesn't mind.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: An old man who believed it. Who climbed in himself, in October, before the Keeper came, so there'd be a Keeper. Who pulled the lid down on himself.

narration: On the crate beside him, a mug. Empty. And the ledger's pencil.
narration: Ruth's father. Ruth, who thinks he's away.

=== freezer_ruth
narration: You walk up the road to the Gleaner in the dark. She's putting chairs on tables. She sees your face and puts down the chair she's holding.
narration: You don't say it. You say: come with me. She gets her coat.
narration: In the back room she stands a long time at the edge of the freezer, with her hands in her coat pockets, looking down.
ruth: He always said he'd go out on the ice. When it was his time. Walk out on the ice like the old ones.
ruth: He couldn't even do that. He couldn't even get as far as the lake.
narration: She reaches in, and unwinds the scarf from his neck. It comes away stiff. She holds it against her chest.
ruth: Thank you. I'll never forgive you. Thank you.
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys: At the door she stops, and takes your car keys out of her pocket, and looks at them, and puts them back.
narration: She goes. She doesn't close the lid. You do.

=== freezer_olstad
narration: You walk down to the cruiser at the end of the road. You tell her. She doesn't move, the engine running, the heater roaring.
trooper: I know.
trooper: I closed that lid on October fourth. I've been closing it every day since. Every day, Johnny. You don't know what that's like.
trooper: Ruth thinks he's in Anchorage, at his sister's. I let her. I'll let her till spring.
narration: She rolls the window up. You walk back up the road. You close the lid. You lock it.
paranoia: Somebody will tell her. Somebody who never lies.

=== freezer_close
narration: You close the lid. Slowly, so the seal won't make the sound again. It makes it anyway.
narration: You lock the padlock. You put the key in your pocket. You stand there with your hand flat on the lid until it's warm under your hand.
narration: Somebody will tell her. In this town somebody always tells. The only question is who, and whether they'll be kind.
