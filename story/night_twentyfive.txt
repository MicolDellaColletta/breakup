# THE TWENTY-FIFTH NIGHT: Friday, December 24, into Christmas. After Midnight Mass.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: the Mass again, recorded in the ruins.
# Out of place: a stocking on the bedroom door handle, with a tangerine, a
# candy cane, and a milk tooth twisted in paper with a name on it that isn't
# anyone's you know (odd_stocking). Design: docs/december.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. Cold. Christmas Eve.
narration | if=doors:daniel>=2: "Lock nothing." Your knees still feel his, an inch away.
narration | if=flag:booth_left: The empty chair in the booth, facing his. You keep seeing it from his side.
paranoia: The rules. It's a night like any night. Say it.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Christmas Eve.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Mass again. Recorded in the ruins, an hour ago: the whole town's breath in a microphone in the snow, singing. Silent Night, slow, the way people sing it when they mean it.
narration: Then him, reading. Not the Reverend's reading. His own choosing. "The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light."
narration: Then, quietly, not to the congregation, close to the microphone: "Goodnight, Keeper."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Up the hill, the last candles in the ruin, going out one by one, as if someone were walking along the rows pinching them.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentytwo
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentytwo | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line. You put a piece of ham in. It isn't in the rules. It's Christmas.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Four candles in every window on the road, and up the hill, where the church was, a few candles still burning in the snow.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Singing, from up the hill. All of them. Silent night. You stand at the gap until your face goes numb.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. "Lock nothing," somebody said tonight. Or nobody. The rule says it anyway.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Midnight. Christmas.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twentytwo
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. It finds the ham. You hear it find the ham. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning, the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Christmas morning.
narration | if=flag:saw_stocking: The stocking, on the handle still. The tooth, back in its twist of paper, on the dresser. You don't throw it away. You can't think how you'd throw it away.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_stocking
narration | time=+5: On the bedroom door handle, a stocking. Red, knitted, a white band at the top with no name stitched on it.
narration | flag=saw_stocking: Inside, a tangerine. A candy cane. And at the toe, a twist of paper, and in it, a milk tooth, small and yellow.
narration: A name on the paper, in pencil, a child's careful capitals. Not yours. Not anyone's you know. And a year: 1958.
paranoia: The jar. The children's teeth. Every child in town since 1958.
unknown: One came back. One came home for Christmas.
