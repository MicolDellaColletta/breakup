# THE TWENTY-FIFTH NIGHT: Saturday, January 15.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, reading names again. Out
# of place: at three, from the shop below, someone trying on the coat on the
# back of the door, or (without it there) the hook knocking (odd_hook).
# Design: docs/january.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:ida_returned: Ida. You keep saying it to yourself on the stairs, for Walt. Ida. Ida.
narration | if=flag:reverend_moved: A cruiser with no lights, on the highway south, and an empty baby seat. You hope he doesn't talk.
narration | if=flag:reverend_taken: Up the hill, the manse lit again for the first time in a month. Somebody sitting with him.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, reading the list of names again, slowly. You know some of them now. Thomas. The boy from Fairbanks. One you heard on Sam's wall.
narration: And then a name you don't know, said the same way. And then another. More than there should be. He reads on past the ones that happened, into ones that haven't.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the slow list. You count. You lose count. You start again.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentyfive
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentyfive | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Out on the lake, among the shanties, one with no light, and a figure standing outside it on the ice, very still, facing the shore.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
narration | if=flag:reverend_in_back_room and flag:reverend_moved: The back room, empty. The bench. A blanket folded square on it, and a Bible with the place marked.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twentyfive
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Sunday.
narration | if=flag:heard_coat: In the shop, the coat on its hook. The buttons done up. All of them. You left them undone. You're almost sure.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_hook
narration | time=+5: From below, through the floor. A sound from the shop. Not footsteps. The front door's hook, the one on the back of the door.
narration | if=flag:coat_in_shop | flag=heard_coat: The rustle of heavy wool, lifted off it. The small sounds a man makes putting on a coat. One arm. The other. Buttons, one at a time, all the way up.
narration | if=!flag:coat_in_shop: The hook, knocking against the door, softly, over and over, as if something were hung on it and swaying. There's nothing hung on it. Mrs. Hollis took the coat home.
paranoia: The furnace. The wind. The building settling.
narration: Then nothing. A long time of nothing. Long enough for a man to stand very still in a coat, waiting to be let out.
