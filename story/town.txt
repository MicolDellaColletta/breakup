# THE TOWN (evenings, from the map)
# Same format as drive.txt (see the notes at the top of that file).
# Each place in story/places.cfg plays the section with its id on arrival.
# When a section ends with nowhere to go, you're back at the map.

=== map_first
narration: The road into Kettle Lake is one lane of packed snow between two black walls of spruce.
narration: The town is a gas station, a church, a post office, a handful of houses, and the lights of the bar. Beyond them, the lake, flat and white and enormous.
paranoia: Go if you have to. Come back before it's late.

=== pub
narration | time=20:00: The Tripod. Wood paneling, a jukebox nobody's feeding, a moose head wearing a Santa hat from some other year.
narration: Twenty people, maybe. The talking stops when the door closes behind you, then starts again, a little louder than before.
narration: At the end of the bar, Sergeant Olstad lifts a glass of milk an inch in your direction.
narration: Someone raises a beer. Then someone else. Then the whole room, not quite in time.
narration: "Welcome to Kettle Lake, friend."
paranoia: The radio said that. Word for word.
> Buy a round back -> pub_round | if=cash>=40 | cash=-40 | lean=john
> Take a stool at the far end -> pub_stool | lean=paranoia
> Ask who the last Keeper was -> pub_ask | lean=appraisal

=== pub_round
narration: You put two twenties on the bar. The room cheers, the way rooms cheer for anyone who buys a round.
narration: For an hour you're John, and John is easy to like. Your face hurts from it by the end.
john | if=john>=2: See? Nobody here wants to know anything. They want you to be him.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_stool
narration: You take the last stool, the one with your back to the wall. From there you can see the door and every face in the mirror behind the bottles.
narration: Nobody sits next to you. Everybody looks, once.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_ask
narration: You ask the woman behind the bar who had the shop before you.
narration: She keeps drying the same glass.
narration: "The Keeper," she says. "Same as you, hon. There's always a Keeper."
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Not a name. A job title. Like asking who the mailman was.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_leave
narration | time=+90: You leave before they can ask you anything you'd have to answer.
narration: The cold outside is almost a relief.

=== lake
narration | time=+20: The road ends at the shore. Past it, the lake: white, and flat, and so big the far side is just an idea.
narration: Out on the ice, a hundred yards from shore, something stands on three legs. A tripod of spruce poles, a wire running from its top all the way back to a shed on the bank.
narration: Further along the shore, a shack with one lit window. Smoke from a stovepipe. Somebody lives out here.
paranoia: Somebody who watches the ice all winter. Don't knock.
unknown: Not yet, sweetheart.

=== home
narration: The drive back is the same black corridor of spruce. Your one good headlight does most of the work.
narration: The shop is dark. The neon is the only thing awake.
narration: (The night at the shop is still being written. To be continued.)
