# When a section ends with nowhere to go, you're back at the map.

# evening has its own version.

=== map_day_one
narration: The road into Kettle Lake is one lane of packed snow between two black walls of spruce.
narration: The town is a gas station, a church, a post office, a handful of houses, and the lights of the bar. Beyond them, the lake, flat and white and enormous.
paranoia: Go if you have to. Come back before it's late.

=== pub

=== pub_first
narration | time=20:00: The Gleaner. Wood paneling, a jukebox nobody's feeding, a moose head wearing a Santa hat from some other year.
narration: Twenty people, maybe. The talking stops when the door closes behind you, then starts again, a little louder than before.
narration: At the end of the bar, Sergeant Olstad lifts a glass of milk an inch in your direction.
narration: Someone raises a beer. Then someone else. Then the whole room, not quite in time.
narration: "Welcome to Kettle Lake, friend."
paranoia: The radio said that. Word for word.
narration: The woman behind the bar is already pulling a beer you didn't order. Forty, maybe. Sleeves rolled. Eyes that have done this before.
narration: She sets it down in front of you before you can say anything.
ruth: You'll be John. First one's on the house. I'm Ruth, I own the place, for my sins.
narration: She tips her head toward the far end of the bar.
ruth: You're down at the end there. Don't worry, nobody's going to fight you for it.
narration: Twelve stools along the bar, every one taken. A thirteenth at the far end, a little apart from the rest, its seat worn smooth.
narration: As you sit down, a flash. Ruth lowers a Polaroid camera and waves the picture dry.
ruth: Oh, don't make that face. Everybody gets one. It goes up on the wall with the others.
narration: Behind the bar, between the bottles, a row of Polaroids. Faces you don't know, every one of them on this stool, every one with the look you can feel on your own face right now.
narration: You count them. Ten. She tapes yours up at the end. Eleven.
> Buy a round back -> pub_round | if=cash>=40 | cash=-40 | lean=john
> Turn the stool so your back is to the wall -> pub_watch | lean=paranoia
> Ask Ruth who the others were -> pub_ask | lean=appraisal

=== pub_round
narration: You put two twenties on the bar. The room cheers, the way rooms cheer for anyone who buys a round.
narration: For an hour you're John, and John is easy to like. Your face hurts from it by the end.
john | if=john>=2: See? Nobody here wants to know anything. They want you to be him.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_watch
narration: You turn the stool so your back is to the wall. From the end of the bar you can see the door, and every face in the mirror behind the bottles.
narration: Nobody sits next to you. Everybody looks, once. Then back at the new photo on the wall.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_ask
narration: You ask Ruth about the faces on the wall.
ruth: Them? The ones who had the shop before you. First night, every one of them sat right where you're sitting. Same face you're making now.
narration: She keeps drying the same glass, around and around.
ruth: Some of them stayed a while. Most of them were nice enough. Shop always needs somebody.
ruth: You'll want another of those.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Ten photos, ten winters. The tape on the oldest has gone brown. None of them look older than you.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_leave
narration: When you get up to go, Ruth leans across the bar.
ruth: Hey. John. My dad owns that shop of yours. Abraham.
ruth: He's been off somewhere since September. Doesn't tell me where, never has.
ruth: If he shows up, tell him the Gleaner's still standing. And tell him to call his daughter.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: She says "away" like a word she's had a lot of practice with.
narration | time=+90: You leave before anyone can ask you anything you'd have to answer.
narration: The cold outside is almost a relief.

=== lake

=== lake_first
narration | time=+20: The road ends at the shore. Past it, the lake: white, and flat, and so big the far side is just an idea.
narration: The ice reaches out from the shore in a gray skin, thin enough to see the black water moving under it. Further out, open water.
narration: On the bank, four spruce poles lie lashed together under a tarp, a coil of wire beside them, waiting. A shed with a padlock. Somebody means to stand them up out there, once the ice can take it.
narration: Further along the shore, a shack with one lit window. Smoke from a stovepipe. Somebody lives out here.
narration: Out on the thin ice, where nobody could be walking, a light moves. Stops. Moves again. Then it goes out.
paranoia: That ice wouldn't hold a dog.
paranoia: Somebody who watches the ice all winter. Don't knock.
unknown: Not yet.

# --- Day two: Thursday ---

=== map_day_two
narration: Thursday. The same black corridor of spruce, three minutes darker than yesterday.
narration: Up on the hill, every window of the church is lit, as if someone were expecting a crowd.
paranoia: Thursday watch. Ezra said.

=== pub_two_warm
narration | time=+60: The Gleaner, quieter on a Thursday. Half the stools empty. The jukebox playing something nobody chose.
narration: Ruth sets a beer in front of you before you ask, and then, after a moment, a second glass for herself. Clear. She doesn't say what's in it.
ruth: Thanks. For today. For letting me.
ruth: I hadn't been in that room since I was eleven. It still smells like crayons. Can you believe that?
narration: She drinks. She doesn't look at the Polaroids behind her, and you understand she never does.

> Ask what she left up there -> pub_two_left | lean=appraisal

=== pub_two_father
ruth: Dad goes away every fall. Every September since I can remember. Comes back when the ice is in. Doesn't say where.
ruth: This year he didn't even say goodbye. Just the truck in the yard and the shop locked.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: She's waiting for a phone call she's stopped expecting.
-> pub_two_leave

=== pub_two_left
ruth: Nothing. I went to see if it was still there. It was.
narration: That's all she'll say. She refills her own glass, and wipes the bar where it's already dry.
-> pub_two_leave

=== pub_two_cold
narration | time=+60: The Gleaner, quieter on a Thursday. Half the stools empty.
narration: Ruth serves you without a word and goes to the far end of the bar, where there's nothing to do, and does it.
narration: Behind the bar, between the bottles, the row of Polaroids. Yours is at the end. You look like someone you'd cross the road to avoid.
paranoia: She's decided something about you. You were there when she decided it.
-> pub_two_leave

=== pub_two_leave
narration: You leave money on the bar and go before the room can decide to be friendly.

=== church
narration | time=+20: The church at the top of the hill. White clapboard, a squat steeple, every window lit.
narration: Behind the vestry, a room no bigger than a closet: egg-carton foam on the walls, a desk, a microphone on a stand, a red bulb over the door. ON AIR, the bulb says, glowing.
narration: The Reverend Elias sits at the microphone in his shirtsleeves, reading. Ezra Lund stands at a hot plate in the corner, making coffee as if it were a sacrament.
narration: The Reverend looks up. He knows you at once. Something crosses his face, and goes, and he leans to the microphone.
reverend: ...and the forecast for the lake tonight is clear and cold. Fourteen below by morning. Keep your stoves fed, friends.
narration: His eyes on yours the whole time. He's talking about the weather. He isn't talking about the weather.
-> church_ezra_warm | if=flag:ezra_satisfied
-> church_ezra_cold

=== church_ezra_warm
narration | flag=at_church: Ezra pours a second cup without being asked and holds it out to you. The red bulb hums.
-> church_choice

=== church_ezra_cold
narration | flag=at_church: Ezra doesn't offer you coffee. He watches you over the rim of his cup, the way he watched the counter this morning.
-> church_choice

=== church_choice
paranoia: The light is on. Anything you say in this room goes out to every kitchen in Kettle Lake.
> Say nothing. Listen. -> church_listen | lean=paranoia
> "Who do you pray for, on the Thursday watch?" -> church_ask | flag=said_on_air | lean=appraisal
> "Just came to hear the watch. Don't mind me." -> church_john | flag=said_on_air | lean=john

=== church_listen
narration: You stand against the foam and listen. He reads the rules of a church potluck. A hymn number. A name for the prayer list. Another hymn number.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Three hymn numbers, and he never plays a hymn. Somebody out there is meant to look them up.
-> church_hymn

=== church_ask
narration: The Reverend's hand moves toward the switch on the desk. Not fast enough. Your question goes out over the air into every kitchen in town.
reverend: For whoever needs it, friend. Tonight, as every night, for our Keeper.
narration: Ezra sets down the coffee pot very carefully.
-> church_hymn

=== church_john
narration: Your voice in the little room sounds like somebody else's. Friendly. Easy. John's.
reverend: Our visitor is very welcome. Thursday nights get long.
narration: Ezra nods, once. Somewhere in town, a dozen people just heard the new Keeper say he was at the church.
-> church_hymn

=== church_hymn
narration: The Reverend puts a record on the turntable at his elbow. A hymn, scratched and slow. He flips the switch. The red bulb goes dark.
reverend: Four minutes. That's all this one gives us.
reverend: You shouldn't be up here. Not on a Thursday. They'll think you've come to be counted.
reverend: Go home before eleven. Keep the radio on. When the static clears, listen to the numbers, not the words.
narration: The hymn ends. The bulb comes back on. He's already leaning to the microphone, as if you'd never spoken.
narration | time=+120: You stay until the coffee's gone. Nobody asks you to leave. Nobody asks you to stay.

=== cabin
narration | time=+10: The shack on the shore, the one with the lit window. Up close it's bigger than it looked: a cabin, with a lean-to built on the side, and a stovepipe, and a second pipe that isn't for a stove.
narration: Nobody answers your knock. Out on the ice, a lamp moves.
narration: It comes toward you, slowly, straight across the gray skin of ice that wouldn't hold a dog. A man, carrying it. He walks as if it were a floor.
paranoia: That ice is two inches thick. You saw it yesterday.
narration: He steps onto the bank. Thirties, maybe forty. A beard that has given up being a decision. A parka held together with duct tape at one elbow.
sam: It'll hold me. I know where it holds.
sam: You're the Keeper. You came yesterday and looked at the poles and didn't knock. Today you knocked. That's quicker than most.
> "Who are you?" -> cabin_who | lean=appraisal
> "You were out on that ice." -> cabin_ice | lean=paranoia

=== cabin_who
sam: Wakeman. Samuel, if you insist, and please don't. Sam.
sam: I keep the clock for the breakup pool. Among other things nobody asked me to keep.
-> cabin_inside

=== cabin_ice
sam: Every night. Two inches over moving water is nothing. Two inches over a shoal is a sidewalk. You just have to know which is which, and write it down, and not get sentimental.
sam: Wakeman. Sam.
-> cabin_inside

=== cabin_inside
narration: He lets you into the lean-to without quite inviting you. Inside it's a laboratory: shelves of jars, each labelled in a tiny precise hand, a chest freezer humming in the corner, a radio receiver with its back off, books stacked to the ceiling.
narration: On the table, a notebook, open. Columns of figures. Dates. Thicknesses.
narration: He closes it before you can read it. Not hurriedly. The way you'd close a door on a room you haven't cleaned.
-> cabin_cody | if=sold:ice_picks
-> cabin_talk

=== cabin_cody
sam: Your customer went through at the point this morning. Forty-one feet from the shore. I measured, afterwards.
sam: He did very well. Most people panic. He had the picks. Somebody sold him the picks.
narration: He looks at you over his glasses, as if checking a figure.
-> cabin_talk

=== cabin_talk
sam: People will tell you I'm crazy. That's fine. It saves time.
sam: I'm the only person in Kettle Lake who writes things down and then reads them back. That's the whole of my madness.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: The chest freezer. Ice cores, the labels say. Dates going back years. He's been keeping the lake, a piece at a time.
narration: He walks you to the door. On the threshold he stops.
sam: Do you know how many Keepers there have been since I came here?
narration: He doesn't wait for you to guess.
sam: Ten. I have them in the notebook. Their names, the dates. I'll show you, one day. Not tonight.
sam: Tonight, go home and keep your rules. All of them. I'd like you to be eleven for a while.
paranoia: He counts them. Somebody in this town counts them.

=== lake_two
narration | time=+20: The shore again. The ice reaches further out than yesterday, gray and dull, with a skin of new snow on it.
narration | if=sold:ice_picks: Off the point to the north, a dark ragged hole in the shore ice, freezing over at the edges. Bootprints lead to it. Different bootprints lead away, staggering.
narration: The poles are still under their tarp on the bank. Someone has brushed the snow off it.
narration: Further along the shore, the cabin's window is lit. Out on the ice, the lamp is moving again.
paranoia: Go home. Or knock. Pick one.
