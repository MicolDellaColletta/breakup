=== map_first
narration: The road into Kettle Lake is one lane of packed snow between two black walls of spruce.
narration: The town is a gas station, a church, a post office, a handful of houses, and the lights of the bar. Beyond them, the lake, flat and white and enormous.
paranoia: Go if you have to. Come back before it's late.

=== pub
narration | time=20:00: The Gleaner. Wood paneling, a jukebox nobody's feeding, a moose head wearing a Santa hat from some other year.
narration: Twenty people, maybe. The talking stops when the door closes behind you, then starts again, a little louder than before.
narration: At the end of the bar, Sergeant Olstad lifts a glass of milk an inch in your direction.
narration: Someone raises a beer. Then someone else. Then the whole room, not quite in time.
narration: "Welcome to Kettle Lake, friend."
paranoia: The radio said that. Word for word.
narration: The woman behind the bar is already pulling a beer you didn't order. Forty, maybe. Sleeves rolled. Eyes that have done this before.
ruth: You'll be John. I'm Ruth. This is mine.
ruth: Your seat's at the end. Go on. Nobody else sits there.
narration: Twelve stools along the bar, every one taken. A thirteenth at the far end, a little apart from the rest, its seat worn smooth.
narration: As you sit down, a flash. Ruth lowers a Polaroid camera and waves the picture dry.
ruth: For the wall.
narration: Behind the bar, between the bottles, a row of Polaroids. Faces you don't know, every one of them on this stool, every one with the look you can feel on your own face right now.
narration: You count them. Ten. She tapes yours up at the end. Eleven.
> Buy a round back -> pub_round | if=cash>=40 | cash=-40 | lean=john
> Turn the stool so your back is to the wall -> pub_watch | lean=paranoia
> Ask Ruth who the others were -> pub_ask | lean=appraisal

=== pub_round
narration: You put two twenties on the bar. The room cheers, the way rooms cheer for anyone who buys a round.
narration: For an hour you're John, and John is easy to like. Your face hurts from it by the end.
john | if=john>=2: See? Nobody here wants to know anything. They want you to be him.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_watch
narration: You turn the stool so your back is to the wall. From the end of the bar you can see the door, and every face in the mirror behind the bottles.
narration: Nobody sits next to you. Everybody looks, once. Then back at the new photo on the wall.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_ask
narration: You ask Ruth about the faces on the wall.
narration: She keeps drying the same glass.
ruth: Keepers. Every one of them sat where you're sitting.
ruth: There's always a Keeper.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Ten photos, ten winters. The tape on the oldest has gone brown. None of them look older than you.
-> pub_leave

=== pub_leave
narration: When you get up to go, Ruth leans across the bar.
ruth: My father owns your shop. Abraham. He's been away since September.
ruth: If he turns up, you tell him the Gleaner's still standing.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: She says "away" like a word she's had a lot of practice with.
narration | time=+90: You leave before anyone can ask you anything you'd have to answer.
narration: The cold outside is almost a relief.

=== lake
narration | time=+20: The road ends at the shore. Past it, the lake: white, and flat, and so big the far side is just an idea.
narration: Out on the ice, a hundred yards from shore, something stands on three legs. A tripod of spruce poles, a wire running from its top all the way back to a shed on the bank.
narration: Further along the shore, a shack with one lit window. Smoke from a stovepipe. Somebody lives out here.
narration: Out past the tripod, a light moves on the ice. Stops. Moves again. Then it goes out.
paranoia: Somebody who watches the ice all winter. Don't knock.
unknown: Not yet, sweetheart.
