# THE EIGHTH NIGHT: Thursday, October 14 (after key day 8)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the Thursday broadcast, with a
# hymn number there's no rule for (nine); the call, and it's changed (a
# kettle, a pill bottle, a breath that catches: he's hurt); and by the bowl,
# a thread of brown wool. Going down to the phone is the same walk as night
# two. Design: docs/week_two.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Thursday. The second one.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. You check everything's where you left it.
narration | if=flag:saw_coat: Your own coat on its hook by the door. Thin. Cheap. You stand and look at it a while, as if it might be jealous.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Thursday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The static thins, and clears. The Thursday watch. The calm voice.
narration: "...keeping you company through the dark, friends. Tonight's hymns, for those following along at home."
narration: "Number one. Number five. And number nine."
narration: A pause. As if he'd read it wrong, and decided not to say so.
narration: "Nine."
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: One, the window. Five, the dog. Nine. There are eight rules. Nine's the Keeper. In he goes.
paranoia: That's not him. Somebody gave him that number. He read what he was given.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Up the hill, faint, through the wall, you can hear it anyway: somebody's radio, turned up, and the word nine.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The stove. The bowl.
narration | take=note_bowl: Under the bowl, a square of paper, folded once. In your handwriting. Your handwriting exactly.
narration: "5. Feed the dog. At the back door. Not upstairs."
paranoia: You didn't write that. You'd remember writing that. Rule five says feed the dog. It doesn't say where.
appraisal: People write in their sleep. Shopping lists. Apologies.
> Fill it, here, on the line -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_eight
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_eight | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down the stairs with the bowl in both hands, the kibble shifting. Through the steel door, past the freezer. You set it on the floor inside the back door, on no line at all.
narration: You leave the door the way the letter says. The cold comes in around the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. You set it on the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Up the hill, the Lunds' windows still lit. Then, one by one, not.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Somewhere up the hill, a sewing machine, very faint. Then it stops, mid-seam, as if someone had heard the window open.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=has:notebook: Sam's notebook. "3:00. On the stairs. It says there." In your handwriting. You add nothing tonight. You're afraid of what your hand would add.
narration | if=!has:notebook: The flannel shirts. You count them. You keep counting things.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure far out on the breaking ice.
narration: Tonight you see it's wearing a long coat. You're sure it wasn't, before. It's a smudge of brown paint, the length of the figure, down to the ice.
paranoia: It always was. You didn't know what you were looking at. Now you do.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

# --- Bed, and the call. ---

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration | sound=ring: The shop phone.
paranoia: Rule four.
> Go down to it -> phone_go_down | lean=animal
> Let it ring -> phone_ignored | lean=paranoia

=== phone_ignored
narration | show=bedroom: It rings. You let it.
narration | stop=ring: It stops.
-> night_dog

=== phone_go_down
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs, toward the ringing.
narration | stop=stairs: The hallway. The steel door. The phone.

=== phone_steel_door
narration: The steel door. The cold under it.
narration: On the floor, along the bottom of the door, a single brown thread, caught in the gap.
paranoia: Wool. From a sleeve. Somebody stood here. Somebody stood here close.

=== phone_turn_back
narration | sound=stairs: You go back up.
-> phone_ignored

=== phone_down
narration: Behind the counter. The phone.
narration | stop=ring | sound=pickup | break=phone: You lift the receiver. You don't speak.
narration: The room at the other end. But it's changed.
narration: A kettle, coming to the boil. Somebody lets it scream a long time before they take it off.
narration: Then a pill bottle. Shaken. The lid. Two, by the sound. Water.
narration: And the breathing. It catches, once, sharp, when he moves. As if moving hurt.
paranoia: He's hurt. Whoever he is. Something in him hurts when he moves.
unknown | if=frayed: Ask him where it hurts. Go on. You know where.
> Wait -> phone_wait
> Hang up first -> phone_hang_up | lean=animal

=== phone_wait
narration: You wait. You listen to him drink the water. You listen to him breathe around the hurt.
narration | sound=hangup: A soft click.
-> night_dog

=== phone_hang_up
narration | sound=hangup: You put it down. Your hand stays on it, as if to keep it down.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_eight
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. Then down again.
narration: In the morning, beside the bowl, on the line, a single brown thread. Wool. Laid straight, as if put there.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Nothing comes up. In the morning, at the bottom of the stairs, a single brown thread, caught on the edge of the bottom step.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Friday.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_chair
narration | time=+5: The kitchen chair. Pulled out from the table and turned to face the window.
narration: The seat's cold. Not warm, the way it would be if someone had just got up. Cold, the way it is when someone's sat very still for a long time and then gone.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Chairs are cold. It's a cold room.
narration: You turn it back to the table. It takes a moment to want to go.

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. Nothing on the stairs. From far below, through the floor, the back door, opening. A long pause. Closing.
narration: In the morning the bowl is empty, licked clean, and in the snow outside the back door: prints. A dog's, going away. And coming back, beside them, something that isn't quite a dog's, that walks upright for the last few steps.
paranoia: A dog. A big dog. A dog that stands up.
appraisal: Or a dog, and somebody walking it home.
-> morning_after
