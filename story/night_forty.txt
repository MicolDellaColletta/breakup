=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, reading the Keepers' names again. Slower. When he gets to John, he stops, and lets the silence go on so long you think the set's died.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. You know where he'd stop.

=== odd_round
narration | time=+5: On the kitchen table, standing on its end: the flattened round. You've moved it every day. It's always on the table in the evening.
> Put it in the empty chamber. -> round_load | if=has:revolver | flag=round_loaded | lean=john
> Throw it in the lake. -> round_lake | lose=sixth_round | flag=round_thrown | lean=paranoia
> Leave it there. -> round_leave | lean=animal

=== round_load
narration: You open the cylinder. Five, and the empty. You press the flattened round in with your thumb. It shouldn't fit. It goes in. You turn the cylinder so the empty-that-isn't sits at the back, where it won't come round first. You choose where it goes. Nobody else.

=== round_lake
narration: You walk it down to the shore in the dark and throw it as far as you can. It doesn't make a sound. The ice takes it like a coin in a slot.

=== round_leave
narration: You leave it standing on the table. In the morning it's moved an inch.
