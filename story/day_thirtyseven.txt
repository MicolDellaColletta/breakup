# KEY DAY 37: Saturday, March 25, 2000. His price (hard fork 6).
# Same format as the other counter days: the goat at the door, twice now;
# then him, to redeem a ticket: the sixth bullet, pawned on November 23 (or
# kept by him, if MC refused it). He loads John's gun on the glass, six of
# six, and gives it back (if he had it), or leaves the round. Then, after
# closing, the counsel room (story/evening_thirtyseven.txt): his offer.
# See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd. Design: docs/march.md.

=== morning
narration | time=06:30: Saturday, the twenty-fifth. Four weeks. Light at half past six. Water standing on the ice off the point, blue in the mornings, white by noon.
narration: The dog came up the stairs again last night. It's come up every night this week, feed it or don't.
narration | if=flag:took_from_room: Nobody has said anything about his room. Nobody needs to. On Thursday, under your door, a pencil sketch of the gap in a row of notebooks.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:00: You turn the sign. OPEN.

# --- The goat. ---

=== goat_enters
narration | time=+60: The goat at the door. Three red threads on its horns now. It stands with its face to the glass for an hour and leaves without being led.

# --- Him: the sixth bullet. ---

=== daniel_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90 | red=alone: The bell. The good coat. He comes straight to the glass.
narration | if=flag:took_bullet: He puts a pawn ticket on the glass, square to the edge. Your hand on it, from November: ONE (1) BULLET, .38, FLATTENED. LOAN $1.
-> daniel_redeem | if=flag:took_bullet
-> daniel_kept

=== daniel_redeem
narration | redeemed=sixth_bullet | cash=+2: He counts out the loan and four months' interest, to the cent, and waits while you fetch it from the back shelf. A dollar and ninety-two. He doesn't take his eyes off your hands.
-> daniel_load

=== daniel_kept
daniel: You wouldn't take it. So I kept it. I've had it in my pocket since November, like a lucky penny. It's been very lucky.
narration: He puts it on the glass. Flattened, cleaned. The sixth.
-> daniel_load

=== daniel_load
-> daniel_load_gun | if=flag:pointed_gun
-> daniel_load_round

=== daniel_load_gun
narration: He takes John's gun out of the good coat and lays it on the glass beside the bullet. He opens the cylinder. Five, and the empty.
narration | take=revolver | flag=gun_six: He presses the flattened round into the empty chamber with his thumb. It shouldn't fit. It goes in. He closes the cylinder and turns the gun round on the glass so the grip faces you.
daniel: Six. Now it's a whole thing. I said March.
daniel | if=flag:pulled_trigger: You pulled it, that night. The empty one came round. I counted, after you'd gone. I laughed for an hour.
-> daniel_watch

=== daniel_load_round
narration | take=sixth_round | flag=has_sixth_round: He stands the flattened round on its end on the glass, between you, like a chess piece.
daniel: For John's gun. Wherever you've put it. The bear, the board, the bottom drawer. One empty chamber, and now there's this. Six. It's a whole thing. I said March.
-> daniel_watch

=== daniel_watch
narration | if=held:father_watch | redeemed=father_watch | cash=+3: "And my father's." The second ticket, from inside his coat, and three dollars on top of it. You bring the watch from the back shelf. Stopped at nine.
narration | if=!held:father_watch: He takes the watch out of his waistcoat and opens the case on the glass. Stopped at nine.
daniel | if=flag:refused_watch: You wouldn't hold it. I remember.
daniel: Nine o'clock. Thursday nights your whole town sits by the radio at nine and watches the lake. My father kept the same watch. Minnesota, a lake, nine o'clock, every Thursday. The night the boy went in, he was watching.
daniel: He stopped it when they found the boy. He made me wear it.
narration: He puts it back in his waistcoat. It doesn't tick.
daniel | if=flag:ordered_daniel_out: Your shop. Say it again.
narration | if=flag:ordered_daniel_out: You don't. He waits long enough for you to know he's waiting.
-> daniel_after

=== daniel_after
daniel: After closing. The hall. The little room behind the stage. I'll leave the door open for you. You leave yours open for me every night.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, once.
paranoia: Don't go.
unknown: You'll go.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=19:00: Seven o'clock. Light. It's still light. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: Up the road, the hall. One window lit at the back, behind the stage.
