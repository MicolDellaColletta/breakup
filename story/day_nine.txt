# --- Church. ---

=== church_walk
narration | time=09:50: Up the hill. People fall in behind you again, and this time some of them nod, and one says "Morning," as if you were anybody.
narration | show=church: The front pew. Empty. Yours. Nobody walks you to it this time. You walk yourself.
paranoia: Your pew. A week, and it's yours.

=== church_board
narration: The prayer board. Your Polaroid, still pinned there. JOHN. 1999.
narration: Beside it, new, a child's drawing in crayon. A long brown coat, walking onto blue ice.

=== church_people
narration: You turn. The faces turned toward you, pleasantly. Hanna, shining. Ezra, not looking at you.
narration: Mrs. Hollis at the end of her pew, already kneeling. On the hem of her skirt, a brown thread.
narration: Up in the choir loft, the women from Thursday, in a row. They watch you the way you'd watch a cake through an oven door.

=== church_service
narration | time=10:00: The bell stops. The Reverend, in the pulpit. He doesn't find you this time; he knows where you are.
reverend: Seventy-five days, friends. Seventy-five days until the year turns.
reverend: Some of you have been stocking your cellars. Some of you have been praying. I'd say: do both, and be kind to each other while you do it.
narration: Then, again: "We pray for the Keeper." The church stands. The hands come.
narration: Fewer this time. Not everyone comes forward. The ones who come stay longer.
narration: Mrs. Hollis's hand, on the back of your neck again. Her thumb moves, once, slowly, along your hairline. Measuring something. Or comforting. Or both.
> Bear it -> church_bear | flag=prayed_again | lean=paranoia
> Stand up and walk out -> church_walk_out | flag=walked_out_again | lean=animal

=== church_bear
narration: You bear it. It's easier this time. That's the worst thing that's happened to you all week.
narration: After, Ezra at your elbow. "The vestry."
narration | show=vestry: The vestry. The scale. The doorframe. The book.

=== church_walk_out
narration: You stand. The hands fall away. They make an aisle again.
narration: Nobody's surprised this time. That's the difference. Somebody at the back says, quite gently, "Oh, there goes the Keeper," the way you'd say it about a cat.
-> church_door_tooth

=== vestry_scale
narration: The scale. Ezra slides the weight.
> Step on the scale -> vestry_weighed | flag=weighed_again | lean=john
> "No." -> vestry_refused | lean=animal

=== vestry_weighed
narration: You step up. The beam floats. He reads the number, quietly, and this time one of the women from Thursday writes it on the back of her hand, in pen, not in the book.
ezra: Up a pound.
narration: The women look at each other, pleased.
paranoia: For the cut. Up a pound. They'll let it out.

=== vestry_refused
narration: Ezra lets the weight slide back to zero.
ezra: She'll guess, then.
narration: The woman from Thursday looks you up and down, and writes a number on the back of her hand.

=== vestry_frame
narration: The doorframe. Your line, from last week. Nobody adds a new one. They don't need to; you're not going to grow.
narration: Behind the vestry door, on a hanger, under a white sheet: something long. Below the sheet, the hem shows. Brown wool. Thick. Almost to the floor.
paranoia: They brought it to church. It's in the room with you. Don't lift the sheet.
> Lift the sheet -> vestry_sheet | flag=touched_coat | lean=animal
> Leave it -> vestry_leave_sheet | lean=paranoia

=== vestry_sheet
narration: You lift the corner. The coat. Long, heavy, brown, a deep collar. Not finished: one sleeve's only tacked on, with white thread.
narration: You put your hand on it before you can stop yourself. It's warm. It's been near the stove all morning. It's the warmest thing in the room.
narration: Behind you, very softly, one of the women: "Not yet, dear. Bad luck."
narration: You let the sheet fall.

=== vestry_leave_sheet
narration: You leave it. You stand with your back to it, all through the weighing, feeling it there the way you'd feel someone standing too close in a line.

=== vestry_book
narration: The book. This winter's page. Two lines now. Oct. 10. Oct. 17.
narration: On last winter's page, the lines go on, Sunday after Sunday, to a date in April. Then nothing. Then, at the bottom, in different ink: "Coat returned." Crossed out. "Coat not returned."

=== church_door
narration: Out through the church. The Reverend at the door, shaking hands. When it's you, he doesn't say anything at all this time. He just holds on a moment, and lets go.
-> church_door_tooth

=== church_door_tooth
-> church_tooth_asked | if=flag:refused_tooth

=== church_tooth_asked
narration: On the steps, the whole congregation coming out behind you. Ezra stops in front of you. He doesn't lower his voice.
ezra: H.L. Eighty-nine. It's asked.
narration: Behind him, Hanna, red to the ears, looking at her shoes. Behind her, everybody. Nobody moves. Nobody pretends not to listen.
paranoia: In front of all of them. He waited till all of them were here.
> "I'll bring it tomorrow." -> church_tooth_yes | flag=gave_tooth | lean=john
> "No." -> church_tooth_no | flag=refused_tooth_public | lean=animal

=== church_tooth_yes
ezra: Tomorrow.
narration: He nods. Behind him, the whole church lets out a breath you didn't know it was holding, and starts talking again, all at once, about the weather.
-> church_lunch

=== church_tooth_no
narration: The silence goes on. Somebody coughs.
ezra: Then you'll be asked again. Every Sunday. Until.
narration: He doesn't say until what. He steps aside to let you down the steps. They all do.
paranoia: The Keeper refused the church, on the church steps. That's what they'll say. That's all they'll say about you, now.
-> church_lunch
