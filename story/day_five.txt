# --- Church. ---

=== church_people
narration: You turn, just once. Every face is turned toward you, pleasantly, the way you'd look at a bride.
narration: Ezra in the second row. Next to him, Hanna, her bare neck pink above her collar, so proud she's shining.

narration: Hands folded on the pew backs. Cuffs riding up. Four short lines, crossed. On that wrist, and that one, and that one.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Not everyone. Most. Count the ones without it. You'll want to know who they are.

=== church_service
narration | time=10:00: You sit. The bell stops. In the quiet you can hear the whole town breathing behind you.
narration: The Reverend climbs to the pulpit. He finds you at once, in the empty pew, and something crosses his face, and goes.
reverend: Hebrews, thirteen, two. "Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares."
reverend: We are a town that keeps its strangers, friends. We feed them. We pray for them. We do not forget them.
narration: He says it gently, looking at you. It sounds like a welcome. It sounds like a warning wearing a welcome's coat.
narration: A hymn. You don't know the words. Behind you, everyone does.
reverend: And now, as every Sunday in the season, we pray for the Keeper.
narration: Behind you, the whole church stands. You hear it: a hundred people getting up at once, the pews creaking, coats rustling.
narration: Then they come forward. All of them. Down the aisle, row by row, and they put their hands on you.
narration: On your shoulders. Your back. Your head. Your arms. Warm hands and cold hands, rough hands, a child's hand on your knee. Nobody speaks. The Reverend says the words, and nobody else makes a sound.
narration: It goes on. It goes on too long. More hands arrive than there is of you, and they rest on the hands already there.
narration: Someone's palm on the back of your neck. Rough at the knuckles. It smells of onions and dish soap. Mrs. Hollis.
paranoia: Count the hands. Count the marks. Don't move. Don't move. Don't.
animal | if=animal>=1: Stand up. Now. Throw them off and walk.

=== church_bear
narration: You bear it. You sit with your hands on your knees and your eyes on the cross and you let a hundred people hold you down by praying.
narration: When it's over, they go back to their pews the way they came, row by row, and the warmth goes off you slowly, like a coat taken away.
reverend: Amen.
narration: Amen, says the church. You find your own mouth has said it too.
narration: After, as the pews empty, Ezra is at your elbow, and two women from the scrubbing last night.
ezra: The vestry. For the records. It won't take a minute.
narration | show=vestry: The vestry. The iron scale, polished. The white doorframe. The book.
paranoia: They want you to see it now. They wanted you to see it yesterday. Now you're in it.

=== church_walk_out

narration: Nobody stops you. Nobody grabs. They step back, all of them, and make an aisle, and you walk up it, past every face.

=== vestry_frame

=== vestry_book
narration: The book on the lectern. Heavy, cloth-bound, a page for every winter.
narration: Names and weights, in the same three hands, every Sunday from October. The weights go up, week by week, till Christmas. Then they go down.
narration: The last line on each page is a date in April or May. Just the date. No weight.
narration: This winter's page is new. One line, so far, in pencil: Oct. 10.

# --- Staying in the shop. ---
