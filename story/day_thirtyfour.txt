=== morning
narration | time=09:30: Sunday, the twentieth. The service in the hall. Leviticus 16, the whole chapter, read aloud by everyone together, from memory.
narration | show=hall | time=11:00: After, the benches pushed back, and in the middle of the hall, the scale. The grain scale from the vestry, from the first Sunday in October. It didn't burn. It was in the hall all along.
narration: A queue for it. Everyone. One at a time, onto the platform, the beam settling, Ezra writing in the book with his left hand, slow and crooked, the cast on his right in its altar-cloth sling.
narration: Mrs. Hollis, light as a child now. Dr. Brekke. The Pruitt boy. Ruth, not looking at anyone.

> "No." -> weigh_no | flag=refused_scale | eye=+1 | lean=paranoia

=== weigh_yes
narration: You step up. The platform rocks under you and steadies. The beam rises, and wavers, and settles, and stays.
narration: Ezra reads it, and writes it down, and looks up at you, and for a moment his face is very tired and very kind.
ezra: Less than October. Not much less. You're holding up.

=== weigh_no
narration: You don't step up. The queue behind you goes very quiet.
narration: Ezra looks at the empty platform. He writes something in the book anyway. You can't see what.
ezra: That's all right. We have your weight from October. It won't have changed much. Things don't, here.

=== booth_sam
narration | show=booth | red=alone: The booth in the corner of the hall, the two scorched chairs. He's in one. You sit in the other.
daniel: I won't keep you. One question, and I'll take your answer as it comes.
daniel: The lights on the ice. At night, by the clock, past the shanties. Everyone's seen them. Someone out there with a torch, every night, writing things down. The town's been asking me who. They'd like to know. They'd like to go and ask him what he's writing.
narration: He waits. He knows. He knows you know. He wants you to say it.
paranoia: Sam. He wants Sam. If you say it they'll go tonight with their lanterns.

=== booth_keep
narration: He looks at you for a long time. He knows you're lying. You know he knows. He doesn't say so.
daniel: All right. That's a kind lie. I don't tell them, but I know a kind one when I hear it.
narration | time=16:30 | show=cabin | red=off: At dusk you go down the shore path. Sam's cabin. The door open. Inside, the wall coming down: string coiled, pins in a jar, the cards going into boxes. Ten years of it, into boxes.

=== booth_tell
narration: You say it. His name. In the booth, in the hall, with the whole town on the other side of a curtain.
narration: He nods. He doesn't look pleased. He looks like a man who's been handed something heavy.
daniel: Thank you. They'll be kind to him. I promise you that. I don't make promises I can't keep.
narration | time=17:00 | show=cabin_road | red=off: At dusk, from the shore path, you watch the lanterns go down to the cabin. Twenty. Thirty. A long slow line of them along the shore. They stop at his door. It opens. They go in.
narration: Nobody shouts. Nobody runs. After a while, the lanterns come out again, and go back up the shore, and among them, a man without one, walking in the middle, very straight.
narration | end=night: The cabin dark behind them. On the porch, in the lantern light as they pass, his notebooks, in a pile, and somebody bending over them with a match.
