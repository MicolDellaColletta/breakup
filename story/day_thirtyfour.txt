=== weigh_yes
narration: You step up. The platform rocks under you and steadies. The beam rises, and wavers, and settles, and stays.
narration: Ezra reads it, and writes it down, and looks up at you, and for a moment his face is very tired and very kind.

=== weigh_no
narration: You don't step up. The queue behind you goes very quiet.
narration: Ezra looks at the empty platform. He writes something in the book anyway. You can't see what.
ezra: That's all right. We have your weight from October. It won't have changed much. Things don't, here.

=== booth_sam
narration | show=booth | red=alone: The booth in the corner of the hall, the two scorched chairs. He's in one. You sit in the other.
daniel: Sit closer. They can hear through a curtain, and this is only for you.
daniel: The lights on the ice. Every soul in that hall knows whose they are. Sam Wakeman, out past the shanties with his torch and his clock, ten years of it. They don't need telling who. They're sheep; they need a say-so before they'll walk down a shore path in the dark.
daniel: They need someone who's been inside to say it. Someone who's sat at his table and seen the wall. That's you. Say his name to me, out loud, and I'll carry it out through that curtain.
narration: He leans in until the scorched chair creaks. He isn't watching your mouth. He's watching your eyes, for the moment you decide, and he's enjoying the wait.
paranoia: He doesn't need you to tell him. He needs it to be you. Say it and they go tonight with their lanterns.
> "I don't know whose lights they are." -> booth_keep | keep=sam | flag=kept_sam | lean=warmth

=== booth_keep

daniel: You've drunk his coffee, and you just lied to my face to keep him. Your mouth does a thing when you lie. I've seen it now. They'll go anyway, later, without you. I wanted it to be you. I'll wait.
narration | time=16:30 | show=cabin | red=off: At dusk you go down the shore path. Sam's cabin. The door open. Inside, the wall coming down: string coiled, pins in a jar, the cards going into boxes. Ten years of it, into boxes.

=== booth_tell
narration: You say it. His name. In the booth, in the hall, with the whole town on the other side of a curtain.
narration: He shuts his eyes for a second, the way a man does over the first mouthful of something good.
daniel: There. In a church hall, with the town on the other side of a curtain. You said it, not me. Whatever they do with it tonight, it's yours.
narration: He stands, and goes out through the curtain, and his voice fills the hall, loud enough to ring the windows: Sam Wakeman's name, and then Jeremiah, the hunters sent to hunt them from every mountain and out of the holes of the rocks.
narration | time=17:00 | show=cabin_road | red=off: At dusk, from the shore path, you watch the lanterns go down to the cabin. Twenty. Thirty. A long slow line of them along the shore. They stop at his door. It opens. They go in.
narration: Nobody shouts. Nobody runs. After a while, the lanterns come out again, and go back up the shore, and among them, a man without one, walking in the middle, very straight.
narration | end=night: The cabin dark behind them. On the porch, in the lantern light as they pass, his notebooks, in a pile, and somebody bending over them with a match.
