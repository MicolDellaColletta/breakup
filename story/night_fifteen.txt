# THE FIFTEENTH NIGHT: Tuesday, November 23. He's here.
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the call comes from a payphone
# across the street; through the open line, wind, and the church bell, which
# MC also hears through the shop window half a second later (daniel.md 6a,
# late November). The dog. Then the bridge that ends what's written.
# Design: docs/november.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. You check everything is where you left it. You check the window. You check the window again.
narration | if=flag:took_bullet: Downstairs, on the back shelf, a flattened bullet with a tag, next to a tin. You can feel it there, the way you'd feel a stove left on.
narration | if=flag:door_daniel_1: "There you are." You keep hearing it. Quiet. Pleased. A man finding a thing exactly where he left it.
paranoia: The rules. Tonight more than any night.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Tuesday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Reverend, late, unscheduled. His voice thin.
narration: "Friends. We have a guest with us this week, as most of you know. He'll speak on Thursday's watch. I'm told it's an honor."
narration: "I'd ask you to keep your doors the way you keep them. That's all. That's all I'll say."
narration: Static. Then nothing.
paranoia: That's all he'll say. That's all he can say, with them listening.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Up the hill, the church, still lit at eleven.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fifteen
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fifteen | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl, the way the note says. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Across the road, by the gas station, the payphone, in its little hood, lit.
narration: One latch is open. You closed them all last night; you remember the cold of the metal. You close it again.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. Twice. Three times.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Across the road, the payphone's light buzzing. Nobody at it. Yet.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=has:revolver: John's revolver, wrapped in the flannel. Five rounds. The empty chamber. You know now where the sixth went.
narration | if=!has:revolver: The flannel shirts. Somewhere under the snow by the back door, the revolver. You think about it more tonight than you have in a month.
narration | if=has:notebook: The notebook, under the flannel. The pen.
> Write "He's here." -> dresser_here | if=has:notebook | flag=notebook_here
> Write "There you are." -> dresser_there | if=has:notebook and flag:door_daniel_1 | flag=notebook_there
> Write "The dog came up at three. I was glad." -> dresser_dog | if=has:notebook and flag:fed_fourteen | flag=notebook_dog
> Write nothing -> dresser_quiet | if=has:notebook

=== dresser_here
narration: "He's here." You press so hard the next three pages carry it.

=== dresser_there
narration: "There you are." In his rhythm. You didn't mean it to come out in his rhythm.

=== dresser_dog
narration: "The dog came up at three. I was glad." It's the only true thing you've written in a week, and it's about a dog nobody's seen.

=== dresser_quiet
narration: You hold the pen over the page until your hand aches. Nothing. You put it away.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure far out on the ice. And, for the first time, at the very edge of the canvas, on the shore, a second small figure, standing, watching. You've never seen it. It's been there all along.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
paranoia: Tonight. With him a hundred yards away. The rule says leave it open.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Your hand on the bolt a long, long time. You leave it. Anyone can come in. That's the rule.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. You test the handle. You test it again. You know it doesn't matter. Olstad has a key. Someone always has a key.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration | sound=ring: The shop phone.
paranoia: Rule four. Don't answer. If you do, don't speak first.
> Go down to it -> call_answer | lean=animal
> Let it ring -> call_ignored | lean=paranoia

=== call_answer
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs, in the dark.
narration | stop=ring | sound=pickup | break=phone: The counter. You lift the receiver. You don't speak.
narration: Not a room. Not a car. Wind. Close, and loud, across the mouthpiece. Outside.
narration: And then, on the line, a church bell. Once.
narration: And half a second later, through the shop window behind you, the same bell. Once.
narration: You turn around. Across the road, the payphone, in its little lit hood. A man in it, in a good coat, with his back to you, the receiver at his ear.
narration: He doesn't turn around. He doesn't speak. He stands in the wind, and you stand in the dark, and between you the road, and the line.
narration | if=flag:door_daniel_1: Then, very quietly, on the line, a breath out. Like a man who's been told something he already knew.
narration | sound=hangup: A soft click. He hangs up the receiver, and walks away up the hill, not hurrying.
paranoia: Across the street. He called you from across the street.
-> night_dog

=== call_ignored
narration: It rings. You let it. You go to the front window instead.
narration: Across the road, the payphone, lit. A man in it, in a good coat, the receiver at his ear, his back to you. The phone downstairs ringing, and ringing, in time with his patience.
narration | stop=ring: Then he hangs up, and walks away up the hill. The phone stops. The silence is enormous.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. Then down, and the back door, and the snow.

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up. All night, nothing. You'd give anything for the sound of the stairs.

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Wednesday. Thanksgiving week.
narration | if=broken:back_door>=2: On the back step, a dog's bowl that isn't yours. Empty. Set square.

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door opens below and closes, and across the road, at the same moment, the payphone's light goes out. In the morning, the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after
