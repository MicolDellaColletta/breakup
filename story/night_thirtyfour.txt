# THE THIRTY-FOURTH NIGHT: Sunday, February 20. The open back door.
# Same format as the other nights, except that tonight every door on the
# road is open, all night, and the snow blows into every house, and the
# town sleeps in it. Something comes in. In the morning, footprints through
# the shop and up the stairs to the bedroom door (odd_prints).
# Design: docs/february.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:kept_sam: Sam on his porch in the dark, watching the ice, with nothing in his hands.
narration | if=flag:gave_up_sam: The notebooks on the porch, and the match. You keep smelling paper burning.
narration: Through the front window, the road. Every door on it standing open. Every one. The lights off behind them. The snow already blowing in over the thresholds.
paranoia: Rule one. The back door unlocked. Tonight the whole town keeps rule one.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, loud, the speaker rattling. "Leave them open tonight, friends. All of them. A man who bolts his door tonight is telling the Lord there's something in his house the Lord had better not see. Let it come in, whatever it is." Then, quieter: "I'll be out walking. I'll know which doors."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Down the road, through the open doors, a dozen radios, all saying the same thing, softly, into the snow.

=== odd_prints

# Locked, the town's one closed door: it doesn't come in. It waits.
=== odd_prints_locked
narration | time=+5: At three, from below, the back door. Not opening. The knob, turning. Turning back. Turning again. Slow, patient, the only locked door on the road.
narration | flag=saw_knob: It goes on until it's light. You lie and listen to it the whole time, and you don't go down.
paranoia: Everyone else's door is open. Yours is the one they'll remember.

=== odd_prints_open
narration | time=+5: At three, from below, the back door. Not the dog. The dog's at three, and this is three, and it isn't the dog.
narration: Something moving through the shop. Slow. Round the shelves, one by one, the way you'd walk round a room you were thinking of buying.
narration | flag=saw_prints: Then up the stairs. Each one. To the bedroom door. And there it stops. It stands outside your door for a long time. You don't breathe. Then it goes back down, slowly, and out, and the back door stays open.
paranoia: Somebody from town. Somebody walking the open houses. That's what it was for.
