# THE THIRTY-NINTH NIGHT: Sunday, April 2. The light comes back.
# Same format as the other nights, and the dark comes late: sunset at a
# quarter to nine. The radio: the clock, and him, counting the weeks. Out of
# place: the town on its porches in the light, watching (odd_porches).
# Design: docs/april.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. Light in it at nine at night, which is wrong.
narration | if=flag:olstad_looks_away: Go at night, she said. Don't look at me.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. "Three weeks, friends. Turn your clocks and turn your hearts." And every house on the road clicks its radio off at the same moment, so you hear it go.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the house across the road counting to three.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtynine
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtynine | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The windows.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Light, and cold, and through the crack, people talking quietly on their porches, about you.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. Still a little light in the sky. You don't sleep.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_thirtynine
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:00: Monday. The light at half past six. You didn't sleep for any of the dark there was.
narration: Downstairs, on the mat inside the front door, the ledger. Abraham's. Pushed back under the door in the night, though it shouldn't fit. The spine split down the middle where it was forced.
narration: You put it on the counter where it lives. It falls open by itself at the last page. JOHN. A sprig of spruce laid in the fold.

=== odd_porches
narration | time=+5: Out of the window: every porch on the road with someone on it. Nine at night, broad daylight, nobody doing anything. Facing the shop.
narration | flag=saw_porches: When you pull the curtain, across the road, one of them pulls theirs too. As if to be polite.
