# KEY DAY 10: Saturday, October 23, 1999. The ice.
# Same format as the other counter days. The lake has frozen shore to shore,
# and the road is closed till Sunday. Who comes in: Olstad, stuck in town;
# Cody with a brass button (if MC gave him the picks back) or Mrs. Hollis;
# Hanna, with an invitation (see VISITS in scripts/counter.gd). The evening
# is walked, on the ice (story/evening_ten.txt). Design: docs/week_three.md.

=== morning
narration | time=07:30: Saturday. Thirty below in the night. The pipes upstairs have frozen again; you boil the kettle on the hot plate behind the counter, in your coat.
narration: From the front window: the lake. White, to the far shore. No open water anywhere. No gray, no black. A floor.
narration: Already, a line of people walking out on it with augers and buckets and folding chairs, the way you'd walk across a field.
narration: The road out is drifted to the tops of the fence posts. Nothing's come in. Nothing's gone out.
-> morning_notebook | if=has:notebook
-> opening

=== morning_notebook
narration: Sam's notebook, on the dresser. Under the other lines, a third, in your hand. The pen's pressed hard.
narration: "It's warm. It's not for you yet."
paranoia: You don't remember. You don't remember any of them. Stop reading it. No. Keep reading it. Write that down.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:00: You turn the sign. OPEN. Nobody can get to town, and nobody can leave, and the shop is open anyway.

# --- Olstad, stuck. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+30: The bell. Olstad, out of uniform, in a parka that won't zip over her belly. She stamps the snow off and stands by the radiator like she's paying rent on it.
trooper: Road's shut. I can't get out to anything that's mine. Figured I'd come stand somewhere warm and look at you.
narration: She does. For a while.
trooper: Rental's due Saturday next, Johnny. Roads permitting.
narration | if=flag:gave_return_date: She says the thirtieth the way you'd say a number you'd written on your hand.
narration: She picks up the compass, sets it down, picks it up.
trooper: Kids are saying something about the ice off the point. Something under it.
trooper: Kids say a lot.
narration: She doesn't look at you while she says it. She looks out the window, at the lake, at the line of people walking out on it.
paranoia: She's heard. She's heard and she's here, telling you she's heard.
> "What kind of something?" -> trooper_ask | lean=appraisal
> Say nothing -> trooper_quiet | lean=paranoia
> [Animal] Look where she's looking. Not at her. -> trooper_animal | needs=animal | lean=animal

=== trooper_animal
narration: You look where she's looking. Out on the lake, the line of people walking. One of them has stopped. The others walk around the place where he's stopped, the way water goes around a stone.
animal: There. That's where it is.
narration: When you look back, she's looking at you, not the lake.
trooper: Ya. Kids say a lot. There's a party on the ice tonight. Keep your eyes on the fire.
-> trooper_goes

=== trooper_ask
trooper: The kind kids say.
narration: She puts the compass down. Exactly where it was.
trooper: There's a party on the ice tonight. Whole town. You'll go. Everybody goes. Keep your eyes on the fire.
-> trooper_goes

=== trooper_quiet
narration: She waits. You don't fill it.
trooper: Good. Keep doing that tonight. There's a party on the ice. Keep your eyes on the fire.
-> trooper_goes

=== trooper_goes
narration | sound=bell: At the door, her hand comes up toward the frame, and stops. She goes.

# --- Cody, with what he found. ---

=== nephew_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Cody Hollis, the ice picks round his neck. His face is wind-burned raw. He looks like he hasn't been inside in days.
nephew: I found it.
narration: That's all he says about what. He doesn't say what it is. He puts something on the glass.
narration: A brass button. Big, the kind on a heavy coat. A scrap of brown wool still caught in the shank, frozen stiff.
nephew: It was in the ice. Frozen in, right at the top. Off the point. I chipped it out with these.
nephew: There's more under it. Down. I could see.
paranoia: Brown wool. The coat. The coat is in the ice.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Two explanations. A button off somebody's coat, lost on the ice last winter. Or a button off somebody, under it.
> Write him a ticket for it. A few dollars. -> nephew_pawn | lean=appraisal
> "Keep it, Cody. It's yours." -> nephew_keep | flag=button_with_cody | lean=warmth

=== nephew_pawn
narration | pawned=brass_button | loan=3: Three dollars. He takes the ticket without looking at it, and the money without counting it.
nephew: Good. I don't want it in the house. Aunt can't see it.
-> nephew_goes

=== nephew_keep
narration: He looks at it on the glass. Then he picks it up and closes his hand on it.
nephew: Yeah. Somebody should keep it.
-> nephew_goes

=== nephew_goes
nephew: You going tonight? The party?
nephew: Don't skate out past the lanterns.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. Not toward home. Toward the lake.

# --- Mrs. Hollis, if Cody didn't have the picks. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, with a dish, though it's not Thursday.
widow | if=sold:ice_picks: Cody's not been home two nights. He does that. He's always done that.
narration | if=sold:ice_picks: She says it to the dish, not to you.
widow | if=sold:ice_picks: He's out on the ice. He says he saw something under it. He was always seeing things. Since he was little.
widow | if=!sold:ice_picks: I set three places last night. Ray's, and Cody's, and mine. I do it without thinking.
narration | if=!sold:ice_picks: She says it to the dish, not to you. Her voice is perfectly ordinary.
widow | if=!sold:ice_picks: They'll find him in May. They find them all in May. That's what everyone tells me, and they mean it kindly.
narration: There's a brown thread caught in the lining of her glove. She doesn't see it.
widow: There's a party tonight. On the ice. You'll come. Eat this first.
-> widow_goes

=== widow_goes
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. The bell.

# --- Hanna, with the invitation. ---

=== hanna_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Hanna Lund, red-cheeked, a scarf wound to her nose. Her hair's growing back, a soft fair fuzz at the neck.
hanna: The first-ice party's tonight. On the lake. The whole town. There's a fire and cocoa and Mr. Teague brings his fiddle.
hanna: The Keeper always comes. It's for you, sort of. It's for the ice, and the ice is for... you know.
narration: She doesn't finish. She smiles as if she had.
paranoia: The ice is for you. She was about to say it.
> "I'll be there." -> hanna_yes | flag=invited_ice | lean=john
> "Maybe." -> hanna_maybe | lean=paranoia

=== hanna_yes
hanna: Good! Wear warm things.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame, and runs.

=== hanna_maybe
hanna: Everybody says maybe. Everybody comes.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame, and runs.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=18:20: Twenty past six, and the light goes. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: Down on the lake, the bonfire's already lit. You can see it from here: a point of orange on the white, and lanterns going out from it, and small dark figures, gliding.
narration: The Gleaner is dark. Everyone's on the ice.
paranoia: Everyone. That's the point. You'll go because everyone goes.
