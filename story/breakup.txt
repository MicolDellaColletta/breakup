# --- Escape alone: the fire. The true one. ---

=== fire
narration | show=highway | time=06:00: Morning. The highway south. John's car, or a truck that stopped for a man walking on the shoulder with his coat over his arm; you don't remember which. The wipers going, the sky clear.
narration: The radio says the ice went out on Kettle Lake at five forty-one this morning. It says nothing else about Kettle Lake. Not a fire. Not a shop. Not a town. It plays a song, and then the weather.
narration | if=flag:olstad_looks_away: The last thing you saw of it, in the mirror, at the town line: a cruiser on the shoulder, lights off, not moving. A woman at the wheel looking straight ahead, at nothing, the way she said she would.
narration | if=flag:coat_in_shop: The brown coat was on its hook by the door when the fire took the hall. The sleeves went first. Nobody will ever wear it onto the ice again.
narration: Your coat smells of smoke. Or it doesn't. You put your face in the collar and you can't tell.
narration: PREPARE TO MEET THY G... goes by on the other side of the road, from behind. You'd forgotten it was there. You don't look round.
narration: You don't know if any of it happened. The shop, the rules, the bowl, the bell. The lake. For the first time since October, it doesn't matter.
narration: Nobody's waiting for you. Nobody's paying for you. There's nothing left to pay.
-> may

# --- Escape with someone. ---

=== run_ruth
narration | show=highway | time=06:00: Morning. The highway south. Ruth asleep against the window with her mother's blue bowl in her lap, wrapped in a bar towel.
narration: At five forty-one she wakes, all at once, as if somebody had said her name. She doesn't say anything. She looks at her watch, and then out of the back window, north, for a long time.
ruth | if=flag:gave_ruth_letter: It wasn't my fault. He wrote it down. I've got it in my coat.
narration: In June, in a rented kitchen in Washington State, she sets the table for three. Every night. You don't ask who the third place is for. You know who was holding the shop's keys when you left. Mrs. Hollis, with the key she's had since Christmas. So does Ruth.
-> may

=== run_sam
narration | show=highway | time=06:00: Morning. The highway south. Sam with a box of notebooks on his knees and his watch held up to the window. At five forty-one he writes it down. "It went," he says. "On the minute. I knew it would." You don't ask how he knows from two hundred miles away.
narration: In a motel in Anchorage, the second night, he buys a corkboard and a box of pins from the drugstore across the road.
narration: The first card he pins up has a name on it in his square capitals. Yours. The real one. You never told him. He worked it out.
narration: "Just to keep it," he says. "So it's written down somewhere."
-> may

=== run_cody
narration | show=highway | time=06:00: Morning. The Parks Highway north, in the back of a pickup that smells of dog and coffee. Cody asleep against you with the ice picks round his neck. His mother driving, not looking in the mirror.
narration: At Fairbanks, at the aunt's house, there's a bed made up for you in a room with a window that faces south.
narration: In May the phone rings. Ray's come up through the ice off the point. Cody's mother sits down on the kitchen floor with the phone in her lap.
narration: Cody goes back to bury him. You drive him to the bus. At the bus he gives you the ice picks, cord and all, and gets on without them.
narration: You don't go back. You drive on alone after all, south, with a pair of ice picks on the seat beside you where a revolver used to be.
-> may
