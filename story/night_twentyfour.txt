# THE TWENTY-FOURTH NIGHT: Tuesday, December 21. The power cut.
# Same format as the other nights, but at the radio the power's gone (docs/
# nights.md 7): the radio, the neon and the freezer all dead. Down to the fuse
# box in the dark, and one switch only: the upstairs (the radio: rule seven
# kept), the neon (the Keeper's light, for the town), or the freezer.
# Design: docs/december.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:ruth_saw_father: Ruth's face, at the edge of the freezer. You'll see it every night now. You knew you would.
narration | if=flag:freezer_closed: The freezer key in your pocket. You take it out and put it on the dresser and then put it back in your pocket.
narration | if=flag:told_olstad_freezer: "Every day, Johnny." You can hear the heater in her cruiser still.
paranoia: The longest night. Fifteen hours of dark. Get through it.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. You turn the dial. Nothing. Not static. Nothing.
narration: The lamp. The lamp's out too. Through the window, the neon's out. For the first time since you came, the road outside the shop is dark.
narration: And under your feet, a sound you've stopped hearing because it's always there, stops. The freezer.
paranoia: Rule seven. The radio on. You can't turn it on.
> Go down to the fuse box -> radio_fuse
> Leave it. Sit in the dark. -> radio_dark | break=radio | flag=power_left

=== radio_fuse
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs in the pitch dark, one hand on the wall. The shop hall. The fuse box, its little door, by touch.
narration: Three switches, thrown down. Labels in Abraham's hand on masking tape, gone soft: UPSTAIRS. SIGN. FREEZER.
narration: You push the first one up. It snaps back down. You try another. It holds a second and snaps down. One at a time. Only one will hold.
> UPSTAIRS -> radio_fuse_upstairs | flag=power_upstairs | flag=radio_on
> SIGN -> radio_fuse_sign | break=radio | flag=power_sign | eye=-1
> FREEZER -> radio_fuse_freezer | break=radio | flag=power_freezer

=== radio_fuse_upstairs
narration | sound=click: It holds. Up the stairs, the lamp's on, and the radio, mid-word, as if it had been talking all along.
narration: Him. "...and the light shineth in darkness. And the darkness comprehended it not." A pause. "Some of you are sitting in the dark tonight. I can hear it. Sit with me."
narration: Outside, the road dark. The shop sign dark. Below, the freezer silent.

=== radio_fuse_sign
narration: It holds. Through the front window, the neon stutters, and catches, red, and the snow outside goes pink.
narration: Up the road, a door opens. Then another. People on their steps, looking at the shop's sign, the only light on the road. Somebody waves. You don't know who.
narration: Upstairs, no radio. Rule seven, broken in the dark. Below you, the freezer silent.

=== radio_fuse_freezer
narration: It holds. Somewhere behind the steel door, the freezer shudders and begins to hum again.
narration: You stand in the dark with your hand on the fuse box, and listen to it, the only sound in the building. Keeping him.
narration: No radio. No sign. The road outside black.

=== radio_dark
narration: You sit in the dark. Fifteen hours of it. The cold starts coming in through the walls at once, the way water comes into a boat.
narration: Below you, the freezer, silent. Thawing.

=== kitchen

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl, in the dark.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line, you think. You can't see the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. You check them by feel.
narration | if=flag:frozen_taped: The taped one. Your fingers find the tape, stiff as bark.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. You think. You check again. Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. The whole road dark, every window, except the candles. Three in every window. They're enough to see the snow by. They were always enough.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Wednesday. The power comes back at six, all at once, every light in the building, the radio shouting a hymn.
narration | if=flag:power_sign: At the Gleaner, they're saying the Keeper kept his light on, the longest night. Mrs. Hollis says it twice.
narration | if=!flag:power_freezer: Under the freezer, on the back-room floor, a little water. Not much. You mop it up with a towel before anybody sees.
narration | if=flag:power_freezer: The freezer hummed all night. You lay awake listening to it, like a man listening to someone else breathe.
