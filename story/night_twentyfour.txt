# THE TWENTY-FOURTH NIGHT: Monday, January 3.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, preaching now, no hymn
# numbers ever again. Out of place: the Christmas cards from under the door,
# stood up on the dresser in a row, all facing the bed (odd_cards).
# Design: docs/january.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. You stand in the doorway and listen for anyone else in it, the way you do now.
narration: Your name, in his voice, in a room. You keep hearing it. It doesn't sound like yours.
paranoia: The rules. You've kept them a hundred nights. Keep them.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Monday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. Not a hymn. Not a reading. A sermon, for an hour, at night, without notes, about waiting. "Waiting is a kind of work, friends. The hardest kind. The ice is doing it. We can do it too."
narration: No numbers after it. There'll never be numbers after it again.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, his voice, an hour of it, the rise and fall of somebody explaining something kindly.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentyfour
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentyfour | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. No candles now. Just the road, and the snow, and the Gleaner's sign.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Up the hill, the church, every window lit, on a Monday.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
narration | if=flag:coat_in_shop: Passing through the shop in the dark, the coat on the back of the front door. You don't look at it. It's the shape of a man standing very still by the door, waiting to be let out.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twentyfour
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Tuesday.
narration | if=flag:saw_cards: The cards on the dresser, still in their row. You turn them to face the wall. In the morning they'll face the bed again. You know that. You do it anyway.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_cards
narration | time=+5: On the dresser, the Christmas cards from under the door. You put them in a drawer. You're sure you put them in a drawer.
narration | flag=saw_cards: They're standing up now, in a row along the top of the dresser, the robin, the church in snow, the man in the long brown coat walking out onto white. All of them turned to face the bed.
paranoia: You did it. Tired. Half asleep.
narration: You don't sleep enough to be half asleep.
