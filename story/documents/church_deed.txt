WARRANTY DEED IN TRUST

The Grantor conveys to THE CONGREGATION OF THE LAKE, KETTLE LAKE, TERRITORY OF ALASKA, all that land and the building thereon known as KETTLE LAKE PAWN & TAXIDERMY, Mile 38, Kettle Lake Road,

TO HOLD in trust for the use of the Keeper of the said Congregation, season by season, from first ice to breakup, in perpetuity.

Dated this fourth day of October, 1931.

(Signed for the Congregation: three names, the ink brown. The third is Lund.)

(Pinned behind it: a pawn ticket, the first in the shop's book. No. 0001. "One season." No name.)
