CARBON COPIES, OFFICE DESK
Ten thin sheets, held together with a rusted paper clip. The newest is on top.

September 12, 1998. Dear Thomas, Thank you for answering our notice. We are pleased to offer you the position of seasonal keeper, beginning the first week of October and ending at breakup...

September 10, 1997. Dear Anne, Thank you for answering our notice. We are pleased to offer you...

September 15, 1996. Dear Luis, Thank you for answering our notice...

September 11, 1995. Dear Margaret...

Below them, six more, older. The carbon has faded until the names are only the shapes of names. The letter underneath never changes.

Every one ends the same way:
Please come alone.
Please don't be late.
— Management
