FOR THE KEEPER
If you're reading this, somebody came. I didn't know if anybody would.

You're later than I hoped, whoever you are.

The shop is yours for the season, until breakup. Keep it open, and keep the ledger honest.

Some of this will sound foolish. Do it anyway. People here have kept to these things a long time, and they notice who doesn't.

1. Close every window before dark. Leave the back door unlocked.
2. Don't buy anything that's still warm.
3. Every item in the ledger gets a price. Even the ones you'd never sell.
4. Don't answer the phone after dark. If you do, don't speak first.
5. Feed the dog.
6. If someone asks to pawn a name, say no. Be polite about it.
7. Keep the radio on at night. The silence is worse.
8. If you see yourself in the shop window after midnight, don't wave.

The coffee is in the can above the stove. It's yours.

— a friend
