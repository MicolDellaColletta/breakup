FOR THE KEEPER, IF THE OTHER ONE ISN'T ENOUGH
(A card, in Abraham's hand, under a false bottom in the counter drawer. The ink older than the letter upstairs.)

1. Close the windows. Leave the back door. They come in by the back.
2. Nothing warm. Warm means it isn't finished with whoever had it.
3. Price everything. A thing with a price can be sold. A thing without one keeps you.
4. The phone after dark is never for you.
5. Feed the dog. There is a dog.
6. Never take a name. Never give one back.
7. The radio is company. Not his. Theirs.
8. Don't wave.
9. If he comes back, give him what he asks for.

(Below, added later, in a shakier hand: "I didn't. I won't. Tell Ruth I'm sorry about the bowl.")
