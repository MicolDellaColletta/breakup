(Unposted. Folded in four. The bundle's tied with string, eleven letters, one for every year since 1988 but two.)

March 3, 1989

Mom,

The ice is going soon. They say April. Everyone's being so kind it's like walking around in warm water.

There's a girl at the bar, Ruth, the old man's daughter. She's going to help me. She says she'll drive me to the highway the night before, when they're all at the church. She has a car nobody watches.

I don't know if I should let her. If they find out she

(It stops there. Under it, in a different pen, in Abraham's hand: "She did. God help her, she did.")
