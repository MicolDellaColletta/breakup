(A school notebook. On the cover, in a small upright hand: LUND.)

Martha Lund. b. 1941. Husband Ole, d. 1988, "the cold" (drink). Boy, Peter, 23.

Peter drinks. From the bottle behind the flour bin. She refills it on
Fridays, so he won't drive to Wasilla for worse.

Stopped going to the Gleaner, Nov. 1993. (The year of Sam's notice. Ask
why.)

Sews for the Circle. Cut the brown coat in October. Kept the scraps.

Prays for the Keeper by name every night. Means it.

She will stand up when called. She will sit where she's told. She will
thank me.
