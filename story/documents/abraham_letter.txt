Ruthie,

It wasn't your fault. I want you to have that in writing, from me, because I never could say it.

It was mine. I knew what your mother was going to do when she put her coat on that night. I let her go and sit in the shop. I could have gone instead. I was afraid.

Every Keeper since, I've kept the shop for them, and fed the dog, and written down the rules, and posted nothing. I thought if I kept it all exactly, nobody else would have to.

I'm going to go and sit down now. It's my turn. It's past my turn.

Your father
