# --- Cody Hollis, if you sold him the ice picks yesterday. ---

=== nephew_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+5: The bell, before the sign has stopped swinging. Cody Hollis, in the doorway.
narration: He's wet to the waist. His jeans have frozen stiff and crack when he walks. His lips are the color of a bruise.
nephew: Went out at first light. To the point.
nephew: It held for about forty feet. Then it didn't.
narration: The ice picks hang around his neck on their cord. One of the spikes is bent.
nephew: Those things work. Swear to God. You stab and you pull and you stab. I'd be under there.
> Ask if he's all right -> nephew_ok | lean=warmth
> Ask what happened out there -> nephew_saw | lean=appraisal

=== nephew_ok
nephew: No. Yeah. I don't know.
narration: He laughs, once. It isn't a laugh.
-> nephew_saw

=== nephew_saw
nephew: When I was in. Before I got out. I looked down.
nephew: There was something under there. A yard down, maybe. Looking up.
narration: He waits for you to ask. When you don't, he says it anyway, quietly, to the counter.
nephew: It had his jacket.
paranoia: Don't ask whose. You know whose.
nephew: I want to pawn these. The picks. I don't want them in the house.
> Write him a ticket. A few dollars. -> nephew_pawn | lean=warmth
> "Keep them. You might need them again." -> nephew_keep | lean=appraisal

=== nephew_pawn
narration | pawned=ice_picks_held | loan=6: Six dollars, half what he paid. He takes the ticket without looking at the number.
nephew: Don't sell them to anybody else. Okay? Just. Don't.
-> nephew_leaves

=== nephew_keep
nephew: I'm not going back out there. Not till it's thick. Not till spring.
narration | pawned=ice_picks_held | loan=0: He lifts the cord over his head and lays the picks on the glass, and walks away from them. You write a ticket anyway. Somebody has to.
-> nephew_leaves

=== nephew_leaves
nephew: Nobody went looking for him, you know. Nobody's going to.
nephew: They'll say the lake gives them back in May. That's what they say here. Like it's a library.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, and the cold, and he's gone. On the floor by the counter, a puddle of lake water, slowly skinning over with ice.
warmth | if=warmth>=2: He'll be standing on the shore in May. Every morning, until it does.

# --- Mrs. Hollis, if Cody went out without the picks. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+20: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, in the same parka, zipped to the chin. She doesn't stamp her boots this time.
widow: Cody didn't come home.
widow: His truck's at the point. Keys in it. Engine cold.
narration: She says it the way people read out a list at the hardware store.
widow: I don't want anything. I want you to write it in the book.
narration: She nods at the ledger, open on the counter.
widow: You write everything in there. Ray's old duck call. A box of shells. Write that he went out.
paranoia: Rule three. Every item gets a price.
> Write it. Leave the price blank. -> widow_write | write=Cody Hollis went out on the ice. | break=price | lean=warmth
> Write it. Price it at twelve dollars, what the ice picks cost. -> widow_price | write=Cody Hollis went out on the ice. $12 | lean=appraisal
> "It's a shop ledger, Mrs. Hollis." -> widow_refuse | lean=animal

=== widow_write
narration: You write it in your own hand, under yesterday's lines. Cody Hollis went out on the ice. Where the price goes, nothing.
narration: She watches every letter. When you put the pen down, she turns the book around to read it, and turns it back.
widow: Thank you.
-> widow_two

=== widow_price
narration: You write it. Where the price goes, you write twelve dollars, and you don't let yourself think about why.
narration: She reads it upside down. She looks at the number a long time.
widow: Twelve dollars.
widow: I suppose that's about right. I suppose that's what it came to.
-> widow_two

=== widow_refuse
narration: She takes the pen out of your hand. Not roughly. The way you'd take something from a child.
narration | write=Cody Hollis went out on the ice. | break=price: She writes it herself, in a round schoolteacher's hand, under yesterday's lines. No price.
widow: There. Now it's written.
-> widow_two

=== widow_two
widow: Two of them, now. Under there. My husband and his brother's boy.
widow: Nobody here goes looking. Not in October. You wait for May.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. She closes the door all the way this time, carefully, as if someone were sleeping.
paranoia: You could have sold him the picks.
paranoia: You didn't make the ice.

# --- Ezra Lund: "the usual". Someone who knew the old keeper. ---

=== ezra_enters

ezra: The usual.
narration: That's all. He waits.

=== ezra_ledger

narration: Nothing on the shelves out front looks like a box of shells.

narration: You set it on the glass.

ezra: Three Thursdays I came by. Locked. I thought he'd gone early this year.

# --- Walt Pruitt: pawning a name. Rule six. ---

=== seller_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Walt Pruitt, in the sheepskin coat. Slower than yesterday. He doesn't look at the shelves at all.
-> walt_took | if=broke:warm
-> walt_refused

=== walt_took
seller: The silver didn't help. I thought it would. Getting it out of the house.
-> walt_asks

=== walt_refused
seller: You were right not to take those. They were hers. So's this.
-> walt_asks

=== walt_asks
narration: He puts both hands flat on the glass, as if the counter were a pulpit.
seller: I'd like to pawn a name. Hers.
seller: Ida.
narration: The shop is very quiet. Even the hum seems to have stepped back.
seller: Forty-one years I've said it. Morning and night. I'd like to stop.
seller: I don't want money for it. Just take it. Write me a ticket.
paranoia: Rule six. Say no. Be polite about it.
> "I can't take that, Mr. Pruitt. I'm sorry." -> walt_kind | lean=warmth
> "Names aren't for sale. Go home, Walt." -> walt_hard | break=name | lean=animal
> Write him a ticket -> walt_accept | break=name | flag=ida_pawned | lean=unknown

=== walt_kind
narration: He nods slowly, as if you'd told him the weather.
seller: She'd have said the same. She always said I asked for the wrong things.
narration: At the door he stops, his hand on the frame.
seller: Ida.
narration: He says it once more, softly, like trying a key in a lock. Then he goes.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: He'll carry it. That's what it's for.

=== walt_hard
narration: His face doesn't change. That's the worst of it.
seller: Then I'll carry it.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, and the door, harder than he needed to.
paranoia: Rule six. Be polite, it said. That was the whole rule.

=== walt_accept
narration | pawned=name_ida | loan=0: You take a blank ticket from the drawer. You write the next number. On the line for the item, you write: One name.
narration: Then the name itself. Your pen moves. You watch it move.
seller: Thank you.
narration: He takes the stub and puts it in his coat. He turns to go.
narration: In the doorway he stops. He looks out at the road, then back at the shop, at you, at the counter.
seller: I'm sorry. I came in for something.
seller: I can't think what.
narration | sound=bell: He stands there a moment longer. Then the bell, and he's gone, walking a little straighter than when he came.
narration: In the ledger, in your own hand: One name. And after it, a word. The ink is dark and fresh. You can't read it.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=2: Ida. You wrote Ida. You can still see it, if you don't look straight at it.
unknown: There. Was that so hard?

# --- Ruth. Not a customer. ---

=== ruth_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Then nothing. Whoever opened the door is standing in it, letting the cold in, not coming further.
narration: Ruth. She's looking at the shop the way you'd look at a grave you haven't visited in a long time.
narration: Then she comes in, all at once, and drops a bundle of mail on the counter.
ruth: Post office was holding these. Dad never picks up in September. Bills, mostly. Seed catalogs. God knows.
ruth: This one's yours, I guess.
narration: A window envelope. NORTHERN LIGHTS AUTO RENTAL, ANCHORAGE. In the window, typed: J. and then a pale ghost of letters where the ribbon ran dry. c/o Kettle Lake Pawn.
narration | take=rental_letter: You put it in your pocket before she can see your face.
paranoia: John gave this address. John's still getting mail.
ruth: You look like a man who doesn't like mail.
narration: She doesn't wait for an answer. She's looking past you, at the hallway, at the foot of the stairs.
ruth: Can I go up? I left something up there. A long time ago.
narration: Her keys are already in her hand. On the ring, among the truck key and the bar keys, a small brass one with a pink plastic tag. A child's tag.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: That's the one. The locked door upstairs.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: She's carrying her childhood around on a key ring.
> "Go ahead." -> ruth_upstairs | flag=ruth_upstairs | lean=warmth
> "I'd rather you didn't." -> ruth_refused | flag=ruth_refused | lean=paranoia

=== ruth_upstairs
narration: She goes past you into the hallway without another word.
narration: You hear her on the stairs. Every step, the same complaint. Then the floor above.
narration: Then a key in a lock. A door opening, at the end of the upstairs hall. A long silence.
narration: The door closing. The key again.
narration: When she comes down, her hands are empty. Her eyes are wet, and she's angry at them for it.
-> ruth_bowl_fed | if=fed_dog
-> ruth_bowl_unfed

=== ruth_bowl_fed

ruth: The bowl. By the stove.
ruth: Where did you find that?
narration: You open your mouth. She's already shaking her head, as if whatever you'd say would be worse than nothing.
narration: She's frightened. Not of you. You've never seen anyone look so frightened in daylight.
-> ruth_goes

=== ruth_bowl_unfed
narration: At the foot of the stairs, she steps around the overturned bowl without looking at it.
narration: Very carefully without looking at it.
-> ruth_goes

=== ruth_refused

narration: You hear yourself say it. It's my building this winter.
narration: She looks at you for a long moment. Something in her face settles, like a decision being filed away.
ruth: Sure. This winter.

=== ruth_goes

narration | sound=bell: The bell. Through the window you watch her stand by her truck a while before she gets in, her hand on the door, not opening it.
