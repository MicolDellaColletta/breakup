# --- Olstad, for a signature. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+30: The bell. Olstad, in uniform, a folder under her arm. She puts it on the glass and keeps her hand flat on it.
-> trooper_case | if=flag:told_olstad_john
-> trooper_nothing

=== trooper_case
trooper: The John night, the way you told it to me. Typed. And the rest I've got.
narration | if=flag:gave_deed: The church's deed, in a plastic sleeve, under it.
narration | if=has:boy_photo: You take the photograph out of your shirt. The boy on the lake shore. She looks at it a long time. "Minnesota," she says. "Summer of '88. A girl drowned at a tent meeting. The witness was a local boy who left town. Never found." She looks up at you. "Found."
trooper: I need you to sign it. And I need you on the hall steps after the service on Sunday week, when I take him. You'll be the witness. Your name, the real one, on paper, in Anchorage. That's the price.
> Sign it. -> trooper_sign | flag=olstad_case | keep=olstad | lean=appraisal
> "Not yet." -> trooper_wait | lean=paranoia

=== trooper_sign
narration: You sign it. Your name. The real one. It looks strange in your hand, like someone else's writing.
narration: She reads it upside down. She doesn't say it out loud.
trooper: Sunday week. The steps. Don't be late.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== trooper_wait
trooper: Then it's the town's word against the town's word, and the ice gets the last one.
narration | sound=bell: She takes the folder. The bell.

=== trooper_nothing
trooper: I've got nothing I can take to a judge. You never gave me the night. Without it he's a preacher who's good with names.
trooper: Two weeks. Stay where I can see you.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

# --- Ruth. ---

=== ruth_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Ruth, in her coat, at ten in the morning, sober. She hasn't been sober at ten in the morning since you've known her.
-> ruth_offer | if=doors:ruth>=3
-> ruth_keys | if=flag:ruth_has_keys and doors:ruth>=2
-> ruth_place

=== ruth_keys
narration | unflag=ruth_has_keys | flag=keys_returned: She puts your car keys on the glass. The rental's tag. She takes her hand off them.
ruth: Take them. I'll set a place anyway. I always set a place.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== ruth_offer
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys | unflag=ruth_has_keys | flag=keys_returned: She puts your car keys on the glass.
ruth: Take me with you. When you go. I don't care where. I've been keeping this town's Keepers for eleven years. Let somebody else keep it.
> "Be at the car. Friday, the twenty-first, after dark." -> ruth_yes | flag=ruth_comes | lean=warmth
> "I can't." -> ruth_no | lean=paranoia

=== ruth_yes
narration: She nods. She doesn't smile. She looks at you as if she were memorising you, the way she does the faces on her wall.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== ruth_no
ruth: No. That's right. Somebody has to set the place.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== ruth_place
ruth: I came to tell you I'll set a place for you. At breakup. Whatever happens.
narration | sound=bell: She goes before you can answer. The bell.

# --- Mrs. Hollis, and Ray's ring. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Mrs. Hollis. She doesn't knock on the frame. She's forgotten to.
> Give her Ray's ring. -> widow_ring | lose=hollis_ring | keep=hollis | lean=warmth
> Keep it. -> widow_keep | lean=paranoia

=== widow_ring
narration: You put the ring on the glass. She looks at it for a long time without touching it.
narration: Then she puts it on her thumb. It's too big for every finger now.
widow: He'll know I've got it back. Let him.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame, remembering. The bell.

=== widow_keep
narration: You don't. She talks about the weather, and the light, and the weigh-in book, and goes. You feel the ring in your pocket all day like a stone.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

# --- Sam's minute. ---

=== sam_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Sam, in the duct-taped parka, a notebook open in his hand. A new one. The first page full already.
sam: The minute is five forty-one, on the twenty-second. I've checked it every night. I'll be at the clock.
sam: Come and stand with me. Or drive me out of here the night before. Either. I'd understand either.
> "Be at the car, Friday night." -> sam_yes | flag=sam_comes | lean=warmth
> "I'll be on the shore." -> sam_shore | lean=unknown

=== sam_yes
narration: He writes it down. Friday. The car. In his square capitals, as if it were a minute he'd measured.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== sam_shore
sam: Then I'll see you at five forty-one. Bring a watch. Not his.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

# --- Cody, and the truck. ---

=== nephew_enters

nephew: My mom says there's room in the truck if you want it. Friday the twenty-first. We're going to my aunt's in Fairbanks. We're not staying for it.
nephew: Uncle Ray's still out there. Mom says he'll come up in May. She says she doesn't want to see it.
> "I'll be there." -> nephew_yes | flag=cody_comes | lean=warmth
> "Go without me." -> nephew_no | lean=animal

=== nephew_yes
narration: He nods, very serious, the way boys do when they've been given a job.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.

=== nephew_no
nephew: Okay. I'll keep the picks on. In case.
narration | sound=bell: The bell.
