# THE THIRTY-EIGHTH NIGHT: Friday, March 31. The last of March.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: the line of holes in the ice,
# counted aloud. Out of place: lamps on the ice, one in every hole, a path
# of lights to the middle (odd_holes). Then the bridge into April.
# Design: docs/march.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:told_sam_time or flag:sam_unforgiven or flag:door_sam_3: Sam's voice in your head, asking the time.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Friday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, counting holes. "Forty-one, friends. One every ten yards, from the point to the middle. One for every Keeper. And one more."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. From the window you can count them yourself.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyeight
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyeight | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The windows.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold, and the smell of water. The ice is going soft. You can smell spring, and it smells like a drowned thing.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. You don't sleep. Three weeks.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_thirtyeight
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:00: Saturday. In the night the snow came off the roof all at once, like something let go.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_holes
narration | time=+5: Out on the lake, a line of lamps. One in every hole in the ice. From the point to the middle, a path of small lights, very straight.
narration | flag=saw_lamps: At the end of the path, in the middle of the lake, one lamp moves. Then stops. As if whoever's holding it has turned round to see if you're coming.
