=== evening
narration | show=shore | time=19:30: The lake, at night. You walk out onto it from the bank. It doesn't move. It doesn't even creak. It's a floor.
narration: The bonfire by the poles, high as a man, throwing sparks at the stars. Lanterns on the ice in a long loose ring around it. A fiddle, somewhere, sawing at a reel.
narration: And the whole town, skating. Old men with their hands clasped behind their backs, gliding like they're thinking. Children shrieking, falling, getting up. The church ladies on folding chairs with thermoses, in a row, like a jury.
narration: It's lovely. It's the happiest you've seen Kettle Lake. It's the happiest you've seen anyone in a long time.
paranoia: Enjoy it. Go on. Everybody else is.

=== shore_fire
narration: The fire. You hold your hands out to it. Somebody puts a paper cup in them: cocoa, too hot, too sweet. Mrs. Hollis.
widow: There. You'll want that. It gets cold, further out.
narration | if=flag:broke_faith_ruth: Ruth, across the fire, in a man's coat. She sees you. She looks at the flames instead, and stays on her side of them.
narration | if=!flag:broke_faith_ruth: Ruth, across the fire, in a man's coat, a flask in her mitten. She comes round to your side and stands next to you without saying anything, close enough that your shoulders touch.
narration: Olstad, on a folding chair, her hands on her belly, watching the skaters the way she watches a road.
narration | if=flag:showed_olstad_ticket: She catches your eye, and doesn't look away.

=== shore_people
narration: The skaters. Round and round, in the lantern light, with the fire on their faces.
narration: The children have made a ring, further out, at the edge of the light. Holding hands, going round, chanting. You know the words before you hear them.
narration: "...seven, the radio, eight, the glass, and nine's the Keeper, in he goes."
narration: They're skating it right on the ice. Round and round, over the same spot, out where the lanterns run thin.
narration: Further still, past the last lantern, alone, a small light pointed straight down. Somebody kneeling.
paranoia: Sam. It's Sam. What's he looking at.

=== shore_poles
narration: The poles, on the bank, the tarp thrown back. Four spruce trunks, lashed at the middle with wire. In the firelight you can see the lashing has been redone. New wire, bright.
narration: Somebody has hung a lantern from the top of them. It swings a little in no wind at all.

narration: And under the ice, a yard down, face up: a shape.
narration: Long. Dark. Arms at its sides. Wearing something heavy that spreads around it in the water, slowly, like a skirt. A long coat. Brown, in the flashlight. Brass at the front.
narration | if=flag:met_sam: Sam, on his knees beside it, his flashlight pointed down. He doesn't look up.

=== far_skate
narration | flag=saw_shape: Someone takes your arm. A child's hand, in a mitten. Then another. They pull you into the ring.
narration: Round you go, all of you, over it, round and round. You keep your eyes up. You keep your eyes on the fire. You sing, because they're singing. You know the words.
narration: "...and nine's the Keeper, in he goes."
narration: When you finally look down, you're over clear black ice, and there's nothing under it at all. Or you've drifted. Or you can't tell any more where it was.
paranoia: You sang it. You held their hands and sang it.
narration | end=night | time=22:30: Later, walking home up the bank, you realize you never once fell. As if the ice were holding you up on purpose.

=== far_olstad
narration | flag=saw_shape: You go back to the fire, and bend to her ear. She gets up off the folding chair with a grunt, and walks out with you, slow, her hand on her belly.
narration: She stands over it a long time with her flashlight. Longer than anyone needs to, to see a log.
trooper: Log.
narration: Then, quiet, not looking at you, looking at the ice:
trooper: Don't you say that word to anybody else. Not tonight. Not with all of them out here.

narration | end=night | time=22:30: Later, walking home, you look back from the bank. Olstad's still by the fire, the last one there, watching the place out past the lanterns.

=== far_kneel
narration | flag=saw_shape: You kneel on the ice next to him. It's so cold your knees burn through your jeans.
narration: He doesn't look up. He has the notebook open on the ice, and he's writing in it with a pencil, his glove off, his fingers white.
sam: October twenty-third. Four hundred meters off the point. Face up. A yard down.
sam: That's Thomas. I have his date. I have his name. The real one; I found it. I'll write it here, next to this, and then it'll be done right, once.
narration: The two of you, on your knees, side by side, while the town skates in its ring around you and the children sing.
narration: Nobody stops. Nobody asks what you're looking at. A few of them smile at you, kneeling, the way you'd smile at someone praying.
unknown: There. Now there's two of you. Now you can't both be wrong.
narration | end=night | time=22:30: Later, he walks you to the bank. He doesn't say goodnight. He says, "Write it down," and goes back out.

=== far_walk
narration | flag=saw_shape: You turn around. You walk back past the lanterns, past the fire, past Mrs. Hollis holding out a second cocoa, past the poles, up the bank.
narration: Behind you, the fiddle. The rhyme. The whole town on the ice, under the stars, skating.
narration: You stand at the top of the bank, alone, and watch them. You're the only person in Kettle Lake who isn't out there.
paranoia: They saw you go. All of them. The Keeper walked off the ice.
narration | end=night | time=22:00: You walk home. Behind you, very faint, the children start the rhyme again from one.
