# THE THIRTY-THIRD NIGHT: Saturday, February 19. After the duel.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, reading a list of
# addresses. Out of place: a child's drawing pushed under the door, a yellow
# house with a swing (odd_drawing). Design: docs/february.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:told_olstad_john: You told her. The empty chamber. All of it. A man she can put on a witness stand. You don't feel lighter. You feel weighed.
narration | if=flag:lied_to_olstad: She closed the notebook halfway through. You keep seeing the notebook close.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, reading. Not names this time. Addresses. "Lakeview Drive, Wasilla. Mile Thirty-Eight, Kettle Lake Road." Slowly, the way you'd read out a list of places you meant to visit. Then a hymn.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. You heard enough of the list through the wall to know your address was on it.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtythree
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtythree | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Olstad's cruiser at the end of the road, engine running, the baby seat in the back.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. The cruiser's exhaust going straight up. She's awake. She's watching the Gleaner.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_thirtythree
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Sunday. The scale today.
narration | if=flag:saw_drawing: The drawing, on the table where you left it. In the morning light you see what you didn't last night: a small figure in the window of the yellow house, and a big one at the gate.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_drawing
narration | time=+5: Under the front door, downstairs, a sheet of paper. You go down for it.
narration | flag=saw_drawing: A child's drawing in crayon. A yellow house. A swing in the yard. Snow. In the corner, in an adult's careful print: For the Sergeant's sister. Pretty in summer.
paranoia: Give it to Olstad. Tomorrow. Give it to her.
