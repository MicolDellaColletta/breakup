=== tripod_with_notebook
narration | show=ice_tripod | time=21:40: The ice at night. You walk out with Abraham's notebook held up in front of you, open, like a lantern that gives no light.

narration: He turns back to the clock. And you see it, close enough: his bare hand inside the case. Turning the minute hand back. A little. Carefully.
-> tripod_caught

=== tripod_caught
narration: He feels you there. He doesn't take his hand out.
sam: Setting it back to the true minute.
sam: It drifts. Cold metal. A second a night, sometimes two. And the ice goes out at the minute, every year, at the same minute, John. It does. It has to. So when it drifts, I set it back.
paranoia: Ten years. Ten years of the exact minute, written down. By a man with his hand in the clock.
sam: I'm not lying. It's the true minute. I know where it should be. I'm only putting it there.
narration: He takes his hand out. He looks at you in the lamplight as if he were waiting to be hit.
> "It's all right, Sam." -> tripod_forgive | door=sam | lean=warmth
> "You made it all up." -> tripod_refuse | flag=sam_unforgiven | lean=paranoia

=== tripod_forgive
narration: He breathes out. The breath goes up white between you and hangs there.
sam: Then help me. Three weeks. Hold the lamp. I'll tell you where the minute is, and you'll write it down, so it's not just me.
narration | time=23:30 | end=night: You hold the lamp. He sets the clock. You write the minute in his notebook, in your own hand, below his. Your initials. The ice talks under you all the way home.

=== tripod_refuse
narration: He nods. He closes the case. He doesn't argue. He picks up the lamp.
sam: Yes. All right. Then go home, John. Count it yourself. I'll be here at the minute either way.
narration | time=23:10 | end=night: You walk back across the soft ice alone. When you look round, the lamp is out, and you can't see him, or the clock, or anything.

# --- Given up: the hall cellar. ---

=== cellar
narration | show=hall_cellar | time=21:40: The hall, dark. Behind the stage, past the counsel room, a hatch in the floor with a padlock and a hasp, and a tin plate beside it with a crust on it.
narration: You kneel. Through the gap at the edge of the boards, warm air, and a smell of paraffin, and someone breathing.
sam: Who's that? Is that you? Is that John?
sam: They're kind. They bring me soup. They've given me a lamp and a Bible. They took my watch. Nobody here will tell me what time it is.
sam: What time is it, John?
> Tell him the time. -> cellar_time | flag=told_sam_time | lean=warmth
> Say nothing, and go. -> cellar_go | lean=paranoia

=== cellar_time
narration: You look at your watch. You tell him. To the minute.
narration: Through the boards, a long breath out, and a pencil moving on paper.
sam: Thank you. Thank you. Come back tomorrow and tell me again. Somebody has to keep it.
narration | time=22:30 | end=night: You walk home. You find yourself checking your watch every few minutes, as if it might drift.

=== cellar_go

sam: John? John, is it after ten? Is it after —
narration | time=22:10 | end=night: You close the hall door on it.
