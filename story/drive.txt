=== glovebox
narration: Napkins. A tire gauge. A pawn ticket, number 0447, from a shop you've never heard of.
narration: On the back, in pencil: an address. The same address as the paper in your pocket.
unknown: Two copies, sweetheart. Somebody really wanted you to arrive.

=== mirror
narration: Headlights, far back. They've kept the same distance for an hour.
narration: You look away. You look back. The road behind you is empty.
paranoia: They turned their lights off. That's what you'd do.

narration: You don't remember putting it there. You don't remember not putting it there.
paranoia: Leave it wrapped.
unknown: Not yet, sweetheart. Not yet.
