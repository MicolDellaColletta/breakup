# THE NINTH NIGHT: Sunday, October 17 (after key day 9)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the Sunday hymns; the call (he's
# still hurt, and tonight he hums along with the hymn, a little, until it
# hurts); the dog. Going down to the phone is the same walk as night two.
# Design: docs/week_two.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Sunday night. Full of roast, and somebody's hands still on the back of your neck.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:touched_coat: Your palm remembers the wool. Warm. Thick. You wash your hands. It doesn't wash off, the warmth.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Sunday hymn hour. Real hymns, nothing in them. You listen anyway, the way you'd listen to someone breathing in the next room.
narration: Between hymns, the calm voice, almost too low to hear: "See to your doors before your windows tonight, friends."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. The lake ice, far off, groaning like furniture being moved.
unknown | if=broken:radio>=2: You keep turning it off. So it's me, then. Just you and me, and the dark.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
narration: It's already on the line. Empty, clean, set straight, waiting. You didn't put it back. You're nearly sure you didn't.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_nine
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_nine | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl, the way the note says. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line. Beside it, still, the brown thread from Thursday. You haven't moved it. You can't.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows.
narration | if=flag:drawing_in_window: From inside, the back of June's drawing, taped to the glass. The coat, in reverse, walking the other way.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Nothing. The town asleep, full of roast.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser. The flannel shirts.
narration | if=has:notebook: The notebook. You open it, and there's a second line under the first, in your hand: "The coat is warm." You don't remember. You close it.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure in its long brown coat, far out on the ice. Walking away.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

# --- Bed, and the call. ---

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration | sound=ring: The shop phone.
paranoia: Rule four.
> Go down to it -> phone_go_down | lean=animal
> Let it ring -> phone_ignored | lean=paranoia

=== phone_ignored
narration | show=bedroom: It rings. You let it. It rings longer than it's ever rung.
narration | stop=ring: It stops.
-> night_dog

=== phone_go_down
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs, toward the ringing.
narration | stop=stairs: The hallway. The steel door. The phone.

=== phone_steel_door
narration: The steel door. The cold. The hum. Nothing else tonight.

=== phone_turn_back
narration | sound=stairs: You go back up.
-> phone_ignored

=== phone_down
narration: Behind the counter.
narration | stop=ring | sound=pickup | break=phone: You lift the receiver. You don't speak.
narration: The room. The clock. Behind it, a radio: the Sunday hour, the same hymn as yours, a second behind.
narration: And then, very low, under the hymn: humming. Him. Humming along, a few notes, the tune exactly right.
narration: Then the breath catches, sharp, and the humming stops, as if it had hurt to do it.
paranoia: He knows the hymns. He knows the hymns like someone who was made to learn them.
> Wait -> phone_wait
> Hang up first -> phone_hang_up | lean=animal

=== phone_wait
narration: You wait. The hymn ends on his end a second after yours.
narration | sound=hangup: A soft click.
-> night_dog

=== phone_hang_up
narration | sound=hangup: You put it down. Upstairs, the hymn goes on, without him.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_nine
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other. Slower than last week.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. A long time. Then down.
paranoia: Slower. Knees get tired. Sunday knees. Church knees.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Nothing comes up. Near dawn, something at the bottom of the stairs, patient, then gone.
narration | if=broken:dog>=2: Near three: a scratching at the bedroom door. Low down. Patient. It stops when you sit up.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Monday.
narration | if=order:back_door<windows: The windows, this morning, are clear. Every other window on the road has frost on it, in ferns. Yours don't.
narration | if=broken:back_door>=2: On the back step, a dish under a cloth. Still warm. No note.

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Back in bed. Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. Below, the back door: open, a long pause, shut. In the morning the bowl's clean, and the prints come up to the door and stop, as if whatever made them waited to be asked in.
-> morning_after
