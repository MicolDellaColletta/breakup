# THE SIXTH NIGHT: Monday (after day six)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the Monday notices on the radio
# (the Keeper's weight among them); no phone (never on Mondays); a light under
# the locked door; and, fed, the dog says something in the kitchen.
# Design: docs/days_four_to_seven.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Monday. The snow's still falling, slow, big flakes, the kind that look like they're thinking about it.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. You check everything is where you left it. Then you check it again, because you said you'd stop at once, and you didn't.
narration | if=flag:tried_town_line: Down in the lot, a car idles with its lights off. Then it doesn't. Then it's gone.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Monday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The calm voice, reading the week's notices in the same tone he reads the weather.
narration: "The plow will do the lake road Wednesday. The Ladies' Circle meets Thursday at the Lunds'. A dog's been found out by the gravel pit, black, answers to Duke."
narration | if=flag:weighed: "And our Keeper, friends, was weighed yesterday, and is in good health, praise God."
narration | if=!flag:weighed: "And our Keeper, friends, is in our prayers, as every week."
narration: "Keep your stoves fed."
paranoia | if=flag:weighed: On the radio. Your weight, on the radio, between the plow and a lost dog.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. The snow makes no sound at all. You didn't know quiet could be so loud.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The stove. The bowl on its line, or the saucer where the bowl should be.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_six
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. You set it down on the line, and stand there, and listen to the building.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut. Your hand doesn't want to let go of it.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Snow falling past the neon, red, then white, then red.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Snow drifts in onto the sill and doesn't melt. The room's that cold already.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser. The flannel shirts.
narration | if=flag:lent_shirt: One fewer. The red one's in a schoolteacher's bag, waiting for a ten-year-old to be you in it.
narration | if=!flag:lent_shirt: You count them. Five. The children like that it's always the same few.
narration | if=has:notebook: You put Sam's notebook in the drawer, under the shirts. Then you take it out again and write today's date at the top of the first page. Then nothing else.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure on the breaking ice. The small dark marks around it, like footprints.
narration: You look at it a long time. Then you turn it to face the wall. Then you turn it back, because that was worse.

=== locked_door
narration | time=+5: The door at the end of the hall. Locked.
narration: Under it, a line of light. For the first time. Yellow, the color of an old bulb.
narration: You stand very still. The light stays.
narration: You take one step toward it, and it goes out.
paranoia: A bulb on a timer. Abraham's. Ruth's. A short. Anything. It's a light. It's only a light.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door. Snow banked against the outside, halfway up.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Anyone coming tonight will have to dig.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. The snow's doing the job anyway.

# --- Bed. No phone: it never rings on Mondays. ---

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven comes. The phone doesn't ring.
narration: It never rings on Mondays. You know that now, the way you know the stairs. You still lie there and wait for it.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_six
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. The bowl. Small careful sounds, for a long time.
narration: Then, very soft, from the kitchen, a word. Just one. Maybe "there." Maybe "there, now." The way you'd say it to a child who's finally stopped crying.
narration: Or a breath. It could have been a breath.
paranoia: A breath. It was a breath. You'll write down breath, if you write anything.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Nothing comes up. Just before dawn, from the back door, the sound of someone digging. Patient. A long time. Then it stops, and they go away again without coming in.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Tuesday. October twelfth. One week.
