=== morning
narration | time=09:30 | show=hall: Palm Sunday. Sprigs of spruce laid on every bench, and on the scale, and on the lectern. The hall smells like a forest after rain.
narration: Leviticus. All of it. Then he lifts his hand, and the plate goes round.
narration: It's a baking tray. It goes from hand to hand down every bench. Money, first, folded small. Then rings. Then the deeds to three houses on the road, folded twice. The pages of the weigh-in book, torn out. And from every family, out of a jar, one child's tooth.
narration: When it comes to you, it's heavy. Everyone watches you hold it. You pass it on.
paranoia: They're paying him. For you. So he'll let go at the shore.

-> plate_refuse

=== plate_yes
narration: It comes back to him. He takes it. He laughs, out loud, the only laugh the hall has heard from him, and puts the deeds inside his coat.
narration: He already has what he wants. Now he has their houses too.
-> attempt

=== plate_confess
narration: It comes back to him. He takes it, and walks down the aisle, and hands it to you, and goes back to the lectern, and leaves you holding it all through the last hymn.

=== plate_refuse

=== way_olstad
narration: Outside, on the hall steps, the cruiser. Olstad, in uniform, out of the car, waiting. She sees you come out. She nods.
narration: He comes out behind you, the last, buttoning the good coat. She says his name. Then another name. Then a third. All of them.
narration | flag=daniel_taken: He holds his wrists out before she asks. He's smiling.
daniel: See you in court, Keeper. You'll be there. You signed.
narration: He goes quietly. He sits in the back of the cruiser beside the empty baby seat, and looks at you through the glass all the way down the road, until the road bends.
narration: The hall stands on its steps and watches, and nobody says anything, and then somebody starts the last hymn again, very quietly, and they all go back in.
-> after_service

=== way_town
narration: You walk up the aisle against the queue. Up to the front. You sit down on the anxious bench, uninvited, and open the notebook with LUND on the cover, and read it out loud.
narration: Her son. The bottle behind the flour bin. Her Fridays. In his handwriting, which everyone in the hall has seen on a hymn sheet. "She will stand up when called. She will sit where she's told. She will thank me."
narration: The hall is very quiet. Behind the stage, the door of the little room opens. He stands in it. He doesn't come out.
narration: Martha Lund stands up.
narration | flag=daniel_given: That night, from your window, the lanterns go up the outside stairs of the Gleaner. Twenty. Thirty. The way they went down to Sam's cabin. He doesn't run. He never runs. In the morning his window above the Gleaner is dark, and stays dark.
-> after_service
