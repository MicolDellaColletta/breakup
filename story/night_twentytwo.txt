# THE TWENTY-SECOND NIGHT: Wednesday, December 15, into the 16th. After the fire.
# Same format as the other nights, at dawn. The radio: the booth burned, so
# nothing but static, the whole dial. Out of place: ash on the inside of the
# windowsill, though every window's shut (odd_ash). Design:
# docs/december_revised.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment, at five in the morning. The smell of smoke in your coat, your hair, the stairwell.
narration | if=flag:tried_church: Your hands, wrapped in Mrs. Hollis's dish towel. They've started to throb, which she said was good.
narration | if=flag:saw_vestry_door: The bolt. On the outside. You keep feeling the heat of it in your palm.
narration | if=flag:sang_at_fire: The hymn, still in your mouth. You can't stop humming it. You hum it on the stairs.
paranoia: The rules. Even now. Especially now.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Static. The whole dial. The booth was behind the vestry wall. There's nothing there now to broadcast from.
narration: You leave it on anyway, hissing. The rule says on. The silence is worse, the letter said. You find out tonight that it's true.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. For the first time since October, there's nothing you'd be missing.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentytwo
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentytwo | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line. Your hands shake doing it.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Up the hill, the church, a red glow, low now, like a stove with the door open.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold, and smoke. Ash coming down with the snow, grey flakes among the white, settling on the sill.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down at six. The light won't come for four hours.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_twentytwo
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Nine o'clock, and still dark. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Nine o'clock, and still dark. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=10:00: Thursday. Ash on the snow, all down the road. Grey. The whole town grey.
narration | if=flag:saw_ash: The sill, wiped clean. More ash by noon. The windows shut.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_ash
narration | time=+5: The windowsill, inside. Every window latched.
narration | flag=saw_ash: Ash on the sill. A fine grey line of it, the length of the window, as if it had come in under the glass. You run a finger through it. It's warm.
paranoia: Gaps in the frame. Old windows. It got in.
narration: You taped the gaps. You remember taping the gaps.
