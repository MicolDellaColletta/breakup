=== evening
narration | show=shore | time=21:00: Dusk, at nine. The procession comes back down the road from the hall with its candles lit, and turns at the shop, and goes down the lake road to the shore.
paranoia: Stay on the step.
unknown: Go with them. Just to see.
> Walk with them. -> walk_with | eye=+1 | lean=unknown
> Watch from the step. -> watch_step | lean=paranoia

=== walk_with
narration: You fall in at the back. Nobody turns. A woman you've never served hands you a candle without looking at you and you take it.
narration: At the shore they don't stop. They walk out onto the ice, single file, along the path of holes, a candle beside every one. The ice is soft. It gives under your boots like a mattress. You can hear water moving under it.
narration: At the last hole, in the middle, they stop, and stand, and then they turn round, all together, and walk back. Nobody says anything. It was practice.
-> lake_three

=== watch_step
narration: Someone going past puts a lit candle in your hand, and goes on.
narration: You stay on the step. From there you can see them go out onto the ice, a line of candles, single file, along the path of holes, all the way to the middle.
narration: They stand there a long time. Then the line of lights turns round and comes back. It was practice.
-> lake_three

# --- The lake, 1988. The last piece. ---

=== lake_three
narration | show=lake_1988: And standing there, with the candles coming back toward you over the ice, it's summer.
narration: The tent. The piano. The singing. The lake flat and blue. You're on the shore. You're in the water. You're both. The girl's gone under and you're counting, forty, forty-one.
narration: The man in the white shirt, waist-deep, with his back to you, turns round.
-> face_guilt | if=background:guilt
-> face_empty | if=background:insanity
-> face_empty | if=unknown>=4
-> face_debt | if=background:debt
-> face_witness

=== face_guilt
narration: It's you. Seventeen. Wet to the waist, in a white shirt too big for you, your hands still out in front of you, pressing down on the water where she was.
narration | flag=lake_three | end=night: The candles reach the shore. Somebody takes the candle out of your hand and blows it out.

=== face_witness
narration: It's him. Thirty. Laughing. Not at the girl. At you, on the shore, watching. He holds out his hand to you across the water, the way he held out the money.
narration | flag=lake_three | end=night: The candles reach the shore. Somebody takes the candle out of your hand and blows it out.

=== face_debt
narration: It's him. Thirty. Holding out a roll of bills across the water, at arm's length, the way you'd hold out bread to a dog. "What's your price?" Eleven years, and you still haven't finished paying it.
narration | flag=lake_three | end=night: The candles reach the shore. Somebody takes the candle out of your hand and blows it out.

=== face_empty
narration: Nobody. The white shirt turns round with nobody in it, standing up in the water on its own, its empty sleeves held out over the place where she went down.
narration | flag=lake_three | end=night: The candles reach the shore. Somebody takes the candle out of your hand and blows it out. There was no candle in your hand. There was never a tent.
