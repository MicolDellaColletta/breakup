# KEY DAY 26: Wednesday, December 29, 1999. The road still shut.
# Same format as the other counter days. The store empty since the 26th; his
# flock going door to door with boxes, the threaded first. Ezra, for the
# Reverend's spectacles (for the church); him, with your groceries and the
# Bible he bought on the first, Leviticus 16 cut out; Olstad, for the same
# spectacles (evidence). See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd.
# Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== morning
narration | time=08:00: Wednesday, the twenty-ninth. Seventeen days since the road closed. The store's been empty since Boxing Day: a sign on the door in Ida's hand, ALL GONE, and a drawing of a sad face somebody's child added.
narration: Up and down the road, men with boxes. Going door to door. Ezra, the Pruitt boy, Dr. Brekke with a sack over his shoulder like a saint in a picture. The houses with a red thread on the door handle first.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: They knock at yours at seven. A box on the step: bread, tinned milk, a jar of somebody's jam.
narration | if=!flag:wears_thread: They go past yours. Twice. The second time the Pruitt boy looks at your door, and then at his father, and his father shakes his head.
narration | cash=+150: The envelope, under the door. Faced. Money's no good with nothing to buy.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=10:00: You turn the sign. OPEN. There's nothing anybody needs that a pawn shop has.

# --- Ezra, for the spectacles. ---

=== ezra_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Ezra, his hands bandaged white from the knuckles to the wrist. Burned, carrying the transmitter out of the fire, they say.
ezra: The spectacles. Elias's. The church would like them back now. To bury with what we buried.
narration | if=held:spectacles: On the back shelf, in Ezra's box, the wire spectacles. Their whole lenses.
paranoia: Olstad wants them. You know she wants them. Glass cracks in a fire that hot.
> Give them to him. -> ezra_spectacles_give | flag=spectacles_to_church | lean=warmth
> "They're held. That's what you asked." -> ezra_spectacles_keep | lean=paranoia

=== ezra_spectacles_give
narration | redeemed=spectacles: You put them in his bandaged hands. He holds them up to the window and looks through them, at the road, for a long moment.
ezra: Thank you. He'd want his glasses. He couldn't see a thing without them.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. You stand with your hand where they were on the shelf.

=== ezra_spectacles_keep
narration: He looks at you. Then at the box. Then at you.
ezra: The Keeper keeps. That's right. That's what I said.
narration | sound=bell: He goes. He doesn't touch his hat.

# --- Him, with your groceries. ---

=== daniel_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell, once. The good coat, and in his arms a cardboard box, the same as the others.
narration: He sets it on the counter. Bread. Tinned milk. A tin of peaches. Coffee, the good kind, the kind the store didn't have even before.
daniel: I brought yours myself. They didn't want to. I told them the Keeper eats too.
narration: And under the coffee, wrapped in a dish towel: the Bible. The one he bought on the first. Calfskin. The names on the flyleaf back to 1871.
daniel: I'm returning this. I've finished with it.
narration | pawned=bible_cut | loan=0: You open it to the middle. Leviticus. Fifteen, and then seventeen. Between them, close to the spine, a stub of cut pages, clean as a knife through butter. Sixteen is gone. Every page of it.
daniel: I know it by heart. It seemed a shame for it to be in two places.
narration | if=!flag:wears_thread: He looks at your bare wrist, and back at your face, and doesn't say anything about it, and that's worse.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: He looks at your wrist. He smiles.
daniel: The road opens on the second. You'll want to stay for New Year's. Everyone's staying.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, once.

# --- Olstad, for the spectacles. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Olstad. She shouldn't be walking. She's walking. A hand on the door frame, a hand under her belly.
trooper: The spectacles. The Reverend's. Ezra's box. I want them.
trooper: Glass cracks at a few hundred degrees. That fire was over a thousand. Those lenses weren't on his face when it started. Somebody put them there after. Or took them off him before.
-> trooper_spectacles_gone | if=flag:spectacles_to_church
-> trooper_spectacles_held | if=held:spectacles
-> trooper_spectacles_gone

=== trooper_spectacles_held
> Give them to her. -> trooper_spectacles_give | flag=spectacles_to_olstad | keep=olstad | lean=appraisal
> "They're held for the church." -> trooper_spectacles_keep | lean=paranoia

=== trooper_spectacles_give
narration | redeemed=spectacles: You put them in a paper bag from under the counter. She folds the top over twice and writes on it, the date and a number, and puts it inside her coat.
trooper: When the road opens I'm driving these to Anchorage myself. And then we'll see who signs what.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. Very slowly. You watch her all the way to the cruiser, and she doesn't look back at the Gleaner once, which is how you know she wants to.

=== trooper_spectacles_keep
narration: She looks at you a long time.
trooper: Held for the church. Okay. The church is a man in a good coat and a doctor who cries. Okay.
narration | sound=bell: She goes.

=== trooper_spectacles_gone
narration: You tell her. Ezra came. They're gone. To be buried with what they buried.
narration: She closes her eyes. She stands there with her eyes closed for long enough that you think she might have fallen asleep on her feet.
trooper: Okay. That's okay. That was the only thing I had that wasn't somebody's word.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. At the cruiser she sits with the door open and her feet in the snow for a long time before she can swing them in.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=15:40: Twenty to four. Dark. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: Up the road, in the Gleaner's back room, the red window. On the radio at four, his voice, reading out the households that have been fed today. One at a time. Slowly.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: Yours is on it.
narration | if=!flag:wears_thread: Yours isn't.
