# THE FOURTEENTH NIGHT: Sunday, November 21 (after the fitting)
# Same format as the other nights. The night: the Reverend announces the
# visitor; the call is a car, wipers, sleet (he's on the road MC drove in
# on); the dog. Design: docs/november.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Sunday night.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:wore_coat: Your own coat on its hook. Thin. It weighs nothing. You stand there holding it, feeling how little it weighs.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: The Sunday hymns. Then the Reverend, between two of them, reading a letter.
narration: "Our friend from the road writes. He's coming up the valley this week, if the road holds. He says he'd be honored to sit with us at Thanksgiving. He asks for nothing."
narration: A pause.
narration: "Make him welcome, friends."
narration: He says it the way you'd say the forecast is for more snow.
paranoia: He's frightened. The Reverend's frightened of a man on the radio.
narration: Under the hymn, softly, as if to himself: "The back of the house before the front, friends. Always the back first."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the hymns, from somebody else's radio, and then a man reading a letter, too far away to hear.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fourteen
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fourteen | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl, the way the note says. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.
narration | if=flag:wore_coat: Your hands smell of lanolin. You wash them. They still do.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Snow on the glass, melting down it in lines.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Up on the hill, the vestry window, still lit.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser. The flannel shirts. The thin coat.
narration | if=has:notebook: The notebook. You write: "Nov 21. It fit." Then you cross out "fit" and write "fits".

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure in the long coat, far out on the ice. You know now exactly how heavy the coat is.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration | sound=ring: The shop phone.
paranoia: Rule four.
> Go down to it -> call_answer | lean=animal
> Let it ring -> call_ignored | lean=paranoia

=== call_answer
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs, in your socks.
narration | stop=ring | sound=pickup | break=phone: The counter. You lift the receiver. You don't speak.
narration: Not a room. A car. An engine, idling. Wipers, slow, back and forth. Sleet on a windshield, ticking.
narration: The sound of the road you drove in on. The night you came. The wipers in your own car, the same rhythm.
narration: He doesn't breathe into the phone tonight. He lets you listen to the wipers.
paranoia: He's in a car. He's on the road. He's on your road.
narration | sound=hangup: A soft click.
-> night_dog

=== call_ignored
narration: It rings. You let it.
narration | stop=ring: It stops.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_fourteen
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
narration | if=broken:dog>=3: Something lies down outside the bedroom door, with a sigh, and stays till the light.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Monday.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---
narration | if=order:back_door<windows: You slept. You don't remember the stairs. You don't remember anything until the light, and the light is kind.
narration | if=fray==0: The bowl on its line, licked clean. On the pillow beside yours, a single short hair, pale. You leave it there.

=== odd_pillow
narration | time=+5: The bed. On the side you don't sleep on, a dent in the pillow. Small. The pillowcase cool.
paranoia: You rolled over in the night. You slept on the wrong side.
narration: You don't sleep on the wrong side. You plump it flat. The dent comes back, slowly, the way a cushion does when someone gets up.

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean, and the prints in the snow go out from the door toward the lake, and come back in somebody's boots.
-> morning_after
