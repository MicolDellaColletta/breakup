=== morning
narration | time=09:00: Friday, the thirty-first. The last day.
narration: The night radio's counting hours now, not days. "Fifteen hours, friends." The Anchorage station, when you turn the dial, is all banks and airplanes and computers. In Kettle Lake nobody's talking about computers.
narration | show=shop | time=13:00: The shop, open, and nobody comes. Five candles in the window, the last lit from the fourth. Through the glass the whole road is busy, carrying things up to the Gleaner. Folding chairs. A ham. A crate of something.
narration: Up the hill, the ruin, and a scaffold already going up against the black tower stump. Men working on it in the dark at noon, with red threads on their wrists, singing.
narration: At four, dark. At nine, the party. At midnight, the year turns over, and some say the world with it.
paranoia: Where will you be. Where will you be when it happens.

ruth | flag=new_year_ruth: Stay. Please. Just stay.

=== new_year_sam
narration | show=far_ice | time=23:30: The ice. A mile out, in the dark, past the shanties, past the last lantern. The tripod. The clock. And Sam beside it with a flashlight pointed at its face, and a thermos, and two cups.
sam: You came. You actually came.
narration: Behind you, on the shore, the Gleaner, lit, and the noise of it coming across the ice small and far away, like a radio in another room.
sam: Watch the minute hand. Watch it. Don't watch the town. Watch the hand.
narration: You watch the hand. He watches it. Your shoulders touch. The cold comes up through your boots.
narration: Midnight.
narration: Nothing happens. The hand moves on. On the shore, the cheer, faint, a beat late. And then, somewhere under your feet, the ice, one long low note, like a duck call a mile down.
sam | flag=new_year_sam: Did you hear that. Tell me you heard that.
narration: You heard it. You tell him. He laughs, the first time you've heard him laugh, and it's terrible and wonderful, and he writes it down, by flashlight, in the notebook, with your initials beside it.
narration: Across the ice, from the shore, faint, from the direction of the shop: a phone, ringing. Then it stops.
-> new_year_after
