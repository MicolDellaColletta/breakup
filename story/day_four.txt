# --- Hanna Lund. Something for the church. ---

=== hanna_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. A girl, sixteen maybe, in a parka with the hood down. Fair hair cut short, badly, up past her ears, as if done in a hurry with kitchen scissors.
narration: Her neck is bare and red with the cold. She touches it, and then remembers not to.
hanna: Hi. I'm Hanna. Hanna Lund. Grandpa said to bring it here.
narration: She puts a bread bag on the glass. Inside, a braid of fair hair as long as your forearm, tied at both ends with red wool.
hanna: I cut it this morning. For Sunday.
hanna: It goes in the book, and you hold it. For the church, when asked. Grandpa says you'll know how.
narration: She isn't upset. She's proud. She's the proudest person you've seen in this town.
paranoia: Grandpa. Ezra Lund. The usual.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: For the church, when asked. The same words as the jar of milk teeth on the back shelf. The same hand wrote them.
paranoia: Rule three. Every item gets a price.
> Price it. Write two dollars. -> hanna_price | write=Braid of hair, Hanna Lund. Held for the church. $2 | flag=hanna_priced | lean=appraisal
> Write it in. Leave the price blank. -> hanna_blank | break=price | flag=hanna_blank | lean=warmth
> "I can't take a child's hair." -> hanna_refused | break=price | flag=hanna_refused | lean=animal

=== hanna_price
narration | pawned=braid | loan=0: You write it. Two dollars. You don't let yourself think about what the number is the price of.
hanna: Two dollars.
narration: She tries it out, pleased, as if it were a grade.
hanna: Grandpa said Abraham wrote a dollar for Mary Kay's, the year before last. So that's good, I think.
-> hanna_goes

=== hanna_blank
narration | pawned=braid | loan=0: You write it in. Where the price goes, nothing.
narration: She leans over to read it upside down, the way everyone in this town reads the book.
hanna: No price. Is that allowed?
narration: You don't answer. She decides it's a compliment.
-> hanna_goes

=== hanna_refused
narration: She looks at you as though you'd said something in another language.
hanna: It's not for you. It's for the church. You just hold it.
narration | pawned=braid | loan=0: She takes the pen out of the cup on the counter, turns the book around, and writes it in herself, in a round careful hand. Braid of hair. Hanna Lund. Then she writes the ticket, and tears it off, and gives you the stub.
hanna: There. Now you're holding it.
-> hanna_goes

=== hanna_goes
hanna: You'll be there tomorrow? Everybody'll be there.
narration | sound=bell: At the door she knocks twice on the frame without looking at it, the way her grandfather does. The bell. She runs to a truck idling in the lot. The man at the wheel doesn't look at the shop.
paranoia: For Sunday. She cut it for Sunday. What happens on Sunday.

# --- Sam. The first time he comes into the shop. ---

=== sam_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90 | flag=met_sam: The bell. Sam Wakeman, in the duct-taped parka. He stamps his boots exactly three times on the mat.
narration: He doesn't look at the bear, or the fox, or you. He looks at the corners of the ceiling, one after another, as if counting exits.
sam: Batteries. D cells. Eight, if you have them.
narration: You have six, in a box under the counter that's been there since the Carter administration. He buys all six. He checks the date on every one.
narration: Then he doesn't leave.
narration: He takes the notebook out of his parka and puts it on the glass, open, turned so you can read it.
narration: Columns. Names. Dates. The last line on the page: October 5, 1999. "John". And after it, a blank.
sam: I start them in October. It saves time in May.
paranoia: That's you. That's you, in pencil, with a space left at the end.
sam: Everyone in this book came here with a name that wasn't theirs. And then in May, that's the name I had to write. Ten times now. I'd like, once, for the right one to be written down.
sam: Your real name. So the right one's written down. That's all.
narration | if=flag:door_ruth_1: Last night you told Ruth something true. Now this.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: He's asking because nobody else will remember it. Somebody should.
paranoia: Don't. A name in pencil in a madman's book. That's evidence.
john | if=john>=1: John. It's on the page already. Leave it.
> Tell him your real name -> sam_true | flag=door_sam_1 | lean=warmth
> "John." -> sam_john | lean=john
> Take his pencil and cross out "John" -> sam_cross | flag=sam_crossed | lean=animal

=== sam_true
narration: You tell him.
narration: It's the first time you've said it in this town. It sounds very small in the shop, and very loud.
narration: He doesn't repeat it. He writes it in pencil, small and neat, after the quotation marks, and doesn't look up until he's done.
sam: Thank you.
sam: Now there are two of us who know it. Me, and the book.
paranoia | if=flag:door_ruth_1: Three. Ruth has something true too. Neither of them knows about the other. Keep it that way.
sam: Will you help me with the others? The ten. I have the names they came with. I want the names they had.
narration: He closes the notebook. He holds onto it a moment with both hands, as if it might be taken from him.
-> sam_goes

=== sam_john
sam: John.
narration: He looks at you over his glasses for a long moment. He doesn't write anything.
sam: Then I'll leave it blank. Until May. I'm used to that.
-> sam_goes

=== sam_cross
narration: You take the pencil out of his hand. You draw a line through "John", once, hard enough to dent the page.
narration: You don't write anything after it.
narration: He looks at the line. Then at you, as if for the first time. As if you'd been standing behind someone else until now.
sam: All right. Not John. That's a start. That's data.
-> sam_goes

=== sam_goes
narration | sound=bell: He goes. He's the only one all week who doesn't knock on the frame on the way out.
paranoia: You noticed that. When did you start noticing that.
