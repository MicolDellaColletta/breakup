# THE TWENTY-SIXTH NIGHT: Wednesday, December 29. The road still shut.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, the fed households, then a
# hymn that goes on too long. Out of place: the tin of peaches from his box,
# opened, on the kitchen table, a spoon in it (odd_peaches).
# Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:spectacles_to_olstad: A paper bag inside a trooper's coat, going to Anchorage when the road opens. You keep thinking about the road opening.
narration | if=flag:spectacles_to_church: His spectacles, in the ground with whatever else they buried. So he can see.
paranoia: Two more nights in the year. The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Wednesday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him, reading the households fed. Then a hymn. Then the hymn again. Then again. The same verse, over and over, for an hour, as if nobody were there to lift the needle.
narration: At eleven it stops in the middle of a word. Then his voice, very close, very loud: "Forty-one houses fed. Four not. You know the four. Leviticus: the unclean shall dwell alone; without the camp shall his habitation be. Tie your threads, friends. I'm counting doors."
narration: You do know them. Everybody on the road does.
narration | if=!flag:wears_thread: This one. Your door, with nothing on the handle.
narration: Olstad's, at the end of the road. A trooper's door. Nobody's going to tie anything on that.
narration: Sam's, on the shore. Nobody's walked down the path to tie one on.
narration: The Gleaner's back door. Ruth wears hers on her wrist. She won't put one on her door.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: And the manse on the hill, dark. There's nobody left in it to tie one on.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the same verse of a hymn, over and over, for an hour.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentysix
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_twentysix | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. A red thread on almost every door handle on the road. You count the ones without. You can count them on one hand.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Up the road, somebody tying a thread on a door handle in the dark. You can't see who. You see the door.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== odd_peaches
narration | time=+5: The kitchen table. The tin of peaches from his box, open, the lid folded back. A spoon standing in it.
narration | if=flag:refused_groceries: His box is on the floor by the door. In from the step. You didn't bring it in. You remember not bringing it in.
narration | flag=saw_peaches: Half eaten. The syrup still cold. A chair pulled out a little, the way you'd leave it if you'd been called away in the middle.
paranoia: You ate them. You were hungry. You ate half and forgot.
narration: You hate peaches. You've always hated peaches.
