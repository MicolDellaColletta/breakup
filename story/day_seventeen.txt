# KEY DAY 17: Thursday, November 25, 1999. Thanksgiving.
# A walk-around day (scenes/visit.tscn). The shop is shut; the church hall,
# the potluck: the empty place set facing the lake window, MC's seat beside
# it (spots in spots.cfg, if=day==17). Sitting down at the table opens the
# pageant (the stage); after it, the Thursday watch on the radio on the pie
# table, his voice; then the vestry door, and the walk home (end=night).
# Design: docs/thanksgiving.md.

=== morning
narration | time=10:00: Thursday, the twenty-fifth. Thanksgiving.
narration: On the shop door, from the inside, a sign you didn't put there. Cardboard, gone yellow, the corners soft with years of tape. In the owner's hand: CLOSED FOR THANKSGIVING. THE KEEPER IS AT TABLE.
paranoia: It was in the drawer. It must have been in the drawer. You must have put it up.
narration | if=flag:sold_ezra_shells: It's Thursday. The bell doesn't ring at ten. Nobody comes for a box of shells. There isn't one to come for.
narration | time=12:40: You walk up the hill at twenty to one. Every chimney on the road is going. Every one. The smoke goes straight up and stops at the same height, as if the sky had a ceiling.
narration | show=hall | time=13:00: The church hall. The whole town, at long tables, paper on the tables, paper turkeys on the paper. Steam on the windows. Children under the tables. Forty pies on a trestle by the far wall, and a radio among them, a big brown one with a dial, already warm.
narration: At the end of the long table, in front of the big window that looks down at the lake, one place set. A plate. A glass. A chair, pulled out a little, the way you'd pull out a chair for someone.
narration: And next to it, a card folded in half with your name on it. Not your name. JOHN.
narration: Ezra stands, and the room goes quiet the way a room goes quiet for him.
ezra: For the harvest. For the ice, coming in. For the ones at table, and the ones not.
ezra: And for the Keeper, who keeps.
narration: Everyone looks at you. Then everyone says amen, and the noise comes back, all at once, like a door opened on a party.
narration | red=on: At the Reverend's right hand, carving, in shirtsleeves, the good coat on the back of his chair: him.
paranoia: Find your seat. Sit down. Don't stand in the middle of the room like a man who doesn't know where he's supposed to be.

# --- The Thursday watch: his voice on the radio in the hall. ---

=== watch
narration | show=hall | flag=pageant_done | time=21:00: Nine o'clock. The coffee urn ticking. Children asleep on coats under the tables.
narration: Somebody turns the radio on the pie table up. All the way. And the whole hall stops. Forks down. Mid-sentence.
narration: The chair at the Reverend's right hand is empty. The good coat's gone from the back of it.
narration: The radio. A breath. And then his voice, the voice from the Fridays, the one that breathes on your phone and never speaks, filling the hall from a brown box among forty pies.
daniel | on=radio: Kettle Lake. The Reverend has given me his chair tonight. Put your forks down. You've eaten. Now you'll listen.
daniel | on=radio: Leviticus. The sixteenth chapter. Two goats.
narration: He doesn't read it like a story. He reads it like a sentence being passed, each word set down hard, like a glass on a table he means you to hear.
daniel | on=radio: One goat is for the Lord. And the other, the priest lays both his hands on its head, and confesses over it all the sins of the people, all of them, and puts them on its head. And it's sent away, into the wilderness, carrying them. To a land not inhabited.
daniel | on=radio: And the people are clean.
narration: Nobody in the hall moves. A child turns over under a table, and a mother puts her hand on him without looking.
daniel | on=radio: You are not clean. I've been in this town two days, and I can smell it on you. The bottle behind the woodpile. The letter in the stove. The child at one of these tables with another man's eyes.
narration: It comes all at once, a shout, too big for the brown box. The speaker tears on the loud words. Up and down the tables, people look at their plates.
daniel | on=radio: You need someone to carry it for you. And you have one. You've had one since October.
narration: Around the hall, people nod. Slowly. Mrs. Hollis. Ezra. Ruth, by the urn, not nodding, holding the blue bowl against her chest.
narration | if=flag:told_olstad_daniel: Olstad, at the door, isn't nodding. She's looking at the vestry door.
paranoia: Don't look at you. Don't anybody look at you.
narration: Nobody looks at you. Nobody needs to.

=== hall_vestry
narration: The vestry door, at the side of the hall, ajar. Down the short passage, the booth behind the vestry wall, the door with its little window.
narration: Through the glass: the red bulb lit. ON AIR. His back, in his shirtsleeves, very straight, at the microphone. The Reverend in the chair beside him, his hands in his lap, not looking at the microphone. Looking at the floor.
narration: On the radio behind you in the hall, and through the glass in front of you, a half-second apart, the same voice.
narration: The Reverend looks up. He sees you at the glass. He doesn't do anything. He looks at you like a man at a bus window, pulling away.
narration: The man at the microphone doesn't turn round. He doesn't stop reading. But on the radio, in the hall behind you, a hundred people hear him pause, just once, in the middle of a word. Long enough to enjoy it.
paranoia: Go. Go back. Go home.
