# THE TWENTY-THIRD NIGHT: New Year's, after midnight. January 1, 2000.
# Same format as the other nights, but short and late. The radio: him, with
# the new date. Out of place: the calendar on the wall already turned to
# January, every Saturday crossed out but one in April (odd_calendar).
# Design: docs/december.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs, after one in the morning.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. 2000. It looks exactly the same. You don't know what you expected.
narration | if=flag:new_year_ruth: Whisky on your mouth, and hers. "Stay." You came home anyway. You'll go back. You both know you'll go back.
narration | if=flag:new_year_sam: Your initials in his notebook, by flashlight. Midnight, 1/1/00. A note, heard by two.
narration | if=flag:new_year_daniel: The receiver's still off the hook downstairs. You left it. You can hear him, very faintly, through the floor, breathing, keeping you company.
narration | if=flag:new_year_alone: The phone, silent, in its cradle downstairs. Four words, and you didn't answer them.
paranoia: The rules. Even now. Especially now.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. One in the morning.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. Not reading. Talking, the way you'd talk to someone in the same room.
narration: "It didn't end, friends. I know. I know you're disappointed. I'm disappointed with you."
narration: "But the Lord isn't late. The Lord is waiting. For the ice. For the spring. For the offering. And so will we."
narration: "Breakup, friends. That's the day. When the ice goes out."
paranoia: Breakup. April. He's given them a date. He's given them a date with your name on it.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the neighbor's radio, his voice, a long time, calm. You hear one word clearly, the way you'd hear your own name across a room. "Breakup."

paranoia: Rule five.

> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The candles out, all of them, all the way up the road. Somebody went along at midnight and pinched them.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Quiet. The first quiet of the year. Out on the lake, a light, low, moving, where the tripod is.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

narration | time=08:00: January the first. Saturday.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_calendar
narration | time=+5: The calendar on the wall. You turned it to December on the first. It's on January now.
narration | flag=saw_calendar: And somebody's been through it. Every Saturday, January to April, crossed out, neatly, in pencil. Every one but one. A Saturday in April, the twenty-second, left alone, with nothing written on it.
paranoia: Breakup. That's the day. They've picked the day.
narration: You didn't turn the page. You're almost sure you didn't. You turn it back to December. It falls open at January again.
