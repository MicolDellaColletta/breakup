# THE TWENTY-THIRD NIGHT: Friday, December 17. After the ruling.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio back, from the Gleaner's back
# room, on the transmitter Ezra carried out of the fire: him. Out of place: a
# red thread tied on the bedroom door handle (odd_door).
# Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. The smell of smoke's gone out of it at last. You miss it, almost. It was honest.
narration | if=flag:told_olstad_fire: You told her. Out loud. With him six feet away. Your heart's still going like you've been running.
narration | if=flag:backed_doctor: "The stove. The doctor said." You hear it in your own voice, on the stairs, over and over.
narration | if=flag:wears_thread: The thread on your wrist. Tight. You keep turning it with your other thumb.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Friday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Not static. Him. Close to the microphone, the sound of a different room around him: a bar's back room, a jukebox through a wall.
narration: "Friends. Ezra carried the transmitter out of the fire on Wednesday night, in his arms. So you'll have me still. Every night. There's no fire in this town hot enough to take me off the air."
narration: "Elias won't be cold again. Dr. Brekke has written down why, and signed it, and that's the end of it. Don't talk about the fire." Then louder, so the speaker buzzes: "Talking keeps a fire alive."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the neighbour's radio, his voice. It's back. You don't know how you know that's worse.

=== kitchen_fed

=== kitchen_unfed

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. Grey snow. Every window on the road lit.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Up the road, at the Gleaner, the back window lit red, the way a darkroom is.

=== back_door

=== odd_door
