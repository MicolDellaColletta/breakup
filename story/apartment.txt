# THE APARTMENT: FIRST NIGHT

=== arrival
narration: The stairs behind the counter creak on every step.
narration: One room. A bed, a table, a radio, a calendar on the wall. A window looking down on the empty street.
narration: A coat hangs by the door. It's your size.
narration: The toothbrush by the sink is still wet.
paranoia: Someone was here. Recently.
unknown: Make yourself at home, sweetheart. You live here now.

=== echo_answered
paranoia: You picked up a dead phone in a dead shop.
unknown: And you didn't say a word. You're learning.

=== echo_ignored
paranoia: Downstairs, the phone sits quiet. Waiting.
unknown: It'll ring again. And next time, you'll answer.

=== window
narration: The window looks down on the main street. One streetlight. Snow falling through it, slow, like it has all the time in the world.
narration: Across the street, every window is dark. Every curtain is open.
paranoia: Rule one. Close every window before dark.
unknown: Or don't. Let some air in, sweetheart. Let them see you.

=== window_closed
narration: The latch is stiff with cold. It closes with a sound like a held breath let go.
narration: For a second, in the glass, you think someone is standing behind you. It's only the coat on the hook.

=== window_open
narration: You leave it. The cold comes in slowly, the way water fills a footprint.
narration: Down in the street, under the streetlight, there are footprints in the fresh snow. They lead to the shop door. None lead away.
paranoia: Those weren't there a minute ago.

=== radio
narration: An old radio on the table, the kind with a dial that glows amber. It's unplugged, the cord wrapped neatly around it, as if someone wanted it found that way.
paranoia: Rule seven. Keep the radio on at night. The silence is worse.

=== radio_on
narration: You plug it in. Amber light, a hum, then static, then a man's voice in the middle of a sentence.
narration: "...and to our new arrival over at the pawn shop: welcome to Kettle Lake. Keep the radio on, friend. It's a long winter."
paranoia: He knows you're here. Everyone knows you're here.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it wrapped. The silence settles over the room like snow.
narration: In the silence you can hear the compressor downstairs. And under it, something else. A slow knocking, somewhere below the floor.
unknown: Hear that, sweetheart? That's the house getting used to you.

=== calendar
narration: A feed store calendar from Anchorage, still on October. Days crossed out in pencil, one by one, by someone before you.
narration: The crosses stop four days ago.
narration: Far ahead, at the end of April, a single date is circled in red. No note. Just the circle, gone over so many times the pencil tore the paper.

=== mirror
narration: A small mirror above the sink, the silver going black at the edges.
narration: Your face. Tired. Older than you remember.
narration: You turn your head. Your reflection turns too, half a second late.
paranoia: You're exhausted. That's all. That's all it is.
unknown: There you are, sweetheart. I'd know that face anywhere.

=== coat
narration: The coat by the door. Heavy canvas, lined with wool. It fits you as if it was bought for you.
narration: In the inside pocket, a Polaroid. The shop at night, taken from across the street. Your car is parked outside.
narration: On the white strip at the bottom, in neat pencil, a date. Last week.
paranoia: You were still in the city last week. So was your car.
unknown: Somebody's been expecting you for a long time.

=== sleep
narration: The sheets are cold, and smell faintly of someone else's soap.
narration: You lie awake and count the sounds of the building. The compressor. The wind. Your own heart.
narration: Somewhere around three, you stop counting.

=== wake_bed
narration: You wake in the gray half-light, in the bed, exactly where you fell asleep.
paranoia: Good. Good. Nothing happened.
paranoia: Nothing you remember, anyway.

=== wake_standing
narration: You wake standing up.
narration: You're at the bottom of the stairs, barefoot, one hand on the handle of the heavy door at the back of the shop. The compressor hums against your palm.
narration: Your feet are wet. There's snow melting on the floor around them.
unknown: Not yet, sweetheart. Not yet.
