=== morning
narration | time=06:10: December. You wake in the dark, and lie there, and know why you're awake before you know you know.
narration: A sound downstairs. Not the dog. Not the furnace. Paper, on wood.
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs in your socks. The shop dark, the neon off, the street lamp throwing the window's shape across the floor.
narration: Through the glass of the front door: boots on the step. Someone bending.
narration: The envelope comes under the door, an inch at a time, and stops.
narration: Then the someone straightens up. Sees you, through the glass, standing in the dark three feet away.
narration: Ezra.
narration: Neither of you moves. His breath on the glass, and yours, on the other side.
narration: He touches the brim of his hat. Then he goes down the step and away up the road in the dark, not hurrying, the way a man walks home from work.
paranoia: Every Wednesday. Every Wednesday since you came. Ezra. Ezra with his book.
unknown: Not only Ezra. Whoever sends Ezra. Count the pews.
narration | cash=+150: A hundred and fifty, faced. Same as always. Nothing else in it.
narration | if=flag:sold_ezra_shells: You stand there a long time thinking about the box of shells, and the ribbon round it, and whether he opened it.

# --- Olstad: the car. ---

# --- Mrs. Hollis: the first candle. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, with a candle in a jar, the kind with a paper sleeve, for a window.
widow: Advent. One a week, dear, in the window. Till he comes.
narration: She doesn't say who. She sets it on the sill herself, and lights it with a kitchen match, and blows the match out with a little puff, like a birthday.
widow | if=flag:place_duck_call: I heard it, you know. Friday night. On the lake. The call. One long note, and nobody out there.
widow | if=flag:place_jacket: The jacket's back on the hook by the door. The house is warmer. I don't know how that works.
widow | if=flag:place_bare: The place stayed bare. That's all right. I set it again on Sunday, at home. You never know.
widow | if=flag:sat_in_place: And you sat in it. Everybody saw. I was so proud of you.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. The candle stays.

# --- The man in the good coat, at the window. He doesn't come in. ---

=== window_enters
narration | time=+120: Early afternoon, and the light already going.
narration: A shape at the front window. The good coat. He doesn't come in.
narration: He stands on the sidewalk in the snow and reads your shelves through the glass, slowly, left to right, top to bottom, the way you'd read a menu in a town you didn't mean to stay in.
narration: Then he takes a small book from inside his coat, and writes something, and puts it away.
narration | if=flag:sold_ezra_shells: Ezra is with him, a step behind, carrying a box of shells with a ribbon on it.
narration | if=!flag:sold_ezra_shells: Ezra is with him, a step behind. He doesn't look at the shop.
narration: They go on up the road to the Gleaner together.
paranoia: He didn't come in. He doesn't need to come in. He's been in.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=15:47: Thirteen minutes to four, and the light's gone. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: In the window, Mrs. Hollis's candle. Up and down the road, in every window, the same small flame. Like a street of people holding their breath.
narration | if=flag:car_towed: In the lot, a long trench in the snow where the car was.
