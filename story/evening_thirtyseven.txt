# A walked evening (scenes/visit.tscn; GameState.EVENINGS) in the counsel
# room behind the hall's stage. He names his price, because he never lies:
# not the books. MC. "Come with me." Yes (needs two of his doors, or he
# laughs), confess (the lake, 1988: the thing he can't price), or refuse
# ("Then it's a race"). His one lie, at the door: "I said I didn't know why
# I let you go. I knew." (door 3). On the road home: the lake, the second
# piece.
# end=night. Design: docs/march.md, docs/daniel_v7.md.

=== evening
narration | show=booth | red=alone | time=20:30: The hall, dark. The benches stacked. Behind the stage, the little room, the door open, the one bulb. Two scorched chairs.
narration: He's in one. On the other, waiting for you: a cardboard box. Ledgers. Cannery stamps on the spines. John's handwriting, columns and columns.
daniel: The books. What John stole. What the men in Anchorage sent me north for. Take them. Burn them. Read them on the toilet. I never cared.
daniel | if=flag:has_books: The rest of them. You've had the first three in a bread bag in your drawer since January. Now you've got the set.
narration: You sit. The box on your knees. He watches you hold it.
daniel | if=flag:gone_ezra: Ezra's not at his sister's. He went out to count the holes. I counted with him.
daniel | if=flag:gone_brekke: The doctor's not in Anchorage. He went out to see how thick it was. I held the lamp for him.
daniel: I never lie. So I'll tell you my price, and you'll know it's the price. Not the books. Not the tin. Not your money, which is my employer's money anyway.
daniel: You. After breakup. Wherever I go next. There's always a next town, and a tent, and a door. I'd like you at the door.
narration | if=flag:lied_to_daniel: He tilts his head. "You lied to me in December, at your counter. About the Reverend's bolt. Your mouth did the thing. I'd like you anyway."
narration | if=flag:told_daniel_hidden: He tilts his head. "You told me the truth in December. Where the Reverend was hiding. You gave him to me. I'd like you more for it."
unknown: Yes. Say yes. Nobody's ever wanted you this much.
paranoia: He'll put you on the ice himself and call it love.
paranoia | if=has:abraham_rules: Rule nine, on the card from the drawer. If he comes back, give him what he asks for. He came back.

=== fork_yes_meant
narration: You say it. He doesn't smile. He goes very still, the way a man does when he's been handed something he didn't think he'd be given, and is deciding whether to believe it.
daniel: Then don't run. Whatever they open in April, the road, the car, the door. Don't run. Stand on the shore, and wait for me.
-> fork_door

=== fork_yes_not
narration: You say it. He laughs. Fondly. That's the worst of it: he laughs the way you'd laugh at a child promising to marry you.
daniel: You don't mean it yet. You will. I've got four weeks.
narration | unflag=fork6_yes: He takes the yes back off you, as if it were a coin you'd dropped.
-> fork_door

=== fork_confess
narration: You tell him. You don't decide to. The summer. The lake. A tent. A boy behind the tent at night, and a man counting money, laughing. A roll of bills held out. "What's your price?" Your hand taking it.
narration: The girl in the water. Forty. You counted to forty.
narration: He listens to every word without moving. When you've finished, the bulb ticks, and nothing else.
daniel: There. You've given me the one thing I couldn't buy off you. You told me yourself. I've waited eleven years for you to tell me yourself.
-> fork_door

=== fork_refuse
narration: You say no. You put the box of ledgers on the floor between the chairs.
narration: He's delighted. He laughs out loud, a real laugh, the first you've heard from him that isn't for anyone else.
daniel: Good. Good. Then it's a race. Four weeks. You and them and me and the ice. I'll be fair. I'll always tell you exactly what I'm doing. You'll still lose.
-> fork_door

# --- His one lie. Door three. ---

=== fork_door
narration: You get up. At the door, with your back to him, he says it.
daniel: I said I didn't know why I let you go, in October. I said I'd come north to find out.
daniel: I knew. I knew you the second you looked up. Eleven years older, and wet to the waist in my memory, and I knew.
narration | door=daniel: It's the only lie he's ever told you. You know it the way you know the cold.
narration | time=23:00: You walk home. The road, the dark, the slush.
-> lake_two

# --- On the road home: the lake, the second piece. ---

=== lake_two
narration | show=lake_1988: Halfway home you stop, in the middle of the road. And it's summer again. The tent, the piano, the singing.
narration: But you're not on the shore this time. You're in the water. Up to the waist. Warm. Brother Abel's hand on your shoulder, heavy, kind.
narration: And your hands are on the girl. On her shoulders. Pushing.
narration: He leans to your ear. "Good. That's it. Forty. Count with me."
narration | flag=lake_two | end=night: You come back to yourself on your knees in the slush at the side of the road, your arms out straight in front of you, pressing down on nothing.
