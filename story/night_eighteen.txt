# THE EIGHTEENTH NIGHT: Wednesday, December 1.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him, hosting now; the Reverend
# only reads the notices. Out of place: the doormat at the top of the stairs
# moved square to the door, as if someone had stood on it a long time
# (odd_mat in spots.cfg). Design: docs/december.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment. The candle in the shop window throws a little moving light up the stairwell behind you, all the way to the top.
paranoia: Ezra. Every Wednesday. You'll lie awake every Tuesday now, listening for him.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Wednesday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. Not a caller now. The host. "Good evening, friends. The Reverend's asked me to keep you company tonight. He's tired. We should let him rest."
narration: Then the Reverend, for a minute only, reading the notices in a flat voice: the Advent candles; the nativity; the teeth for the vestry jar, by Christmas, every family.
narration: Then him again, for an hour, reading. Not scripture. A list. Names, slowly, with a breath between each, like a man counting sheep. You don't know any of them. You listen to the whole list for one you'd know.
paranoia: Thirty. Thirty-one. Stop counting. Stop counting them.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the neighbor's radio, his voice, a slow list of something. Names, or numbers. You can't make it out. You keep trying.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_eighteen
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_eighteen | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The road, and every window on it with its one candle.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. The metal so cold it takes skin.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold like a hand over your mouth. Up the road, at the Gleaner, two men in the doorway, talking, their breath going up together.

=== dresser
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The dresser.
narration | if=has:notebook: The notebook, under the flannel. The pen.
> Write "Ezra." -> dresser_ezra | if=has:notebook | flag=notebook_ezra
> Write "Whoever's paying the interest is in town." -> dresser_interest | if=has:notebook and has:money_order_kettle | flag=notebook_interest
> Write nothing -> dresser_quiet | if=has:notebook

=== dresser_ezra
narration: "Ezra." Just the name. You underline it. Then you write "Wednesdays" under it, and that looks worse.

=== dresser_interest
narration: "Whoever's paying the interest is in town." You read it back. It looks like a line from a book. It looks like something a man would write right before something happened to him.

=== dresser_quiet
narration: Nothing. You put it away.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Anyone can come in. Ezra could come in. Ezra has always been able to come in.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. You test the handle.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven.
narration: No phone. You wait for it anyway, the way you'd wait for a tooth to stop aching.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_eighteen
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door, below. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Thursday.
narration | if=flag:saw_mat: The mat at the top of the stairs. You leave it where it is now. Square to the door.

=== odd_mat
narration | time=+5: The mat at the top of the stairs, inside the door. It's moved. Not much. An inch. It's square to the door now, exactly square, the way nobody leaves a mat.
narration | flag=saw_mat: Two dents in the pile, side by side. Boot heels. Pressed deep, as if someone had stood there a long time without shifting their weight.
paranoia: You stood there. Coming in. You stood there with your keys.
narration: You never stand still that long anywhere. Not in this town.
