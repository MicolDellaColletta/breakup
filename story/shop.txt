# THE SHOP: ARRIVAL
# Same format as drive.txt. The shop on the first night is four rooms you walk
# between with arrows (story/spots.cfg, scene "shop_night"): the floor, the
# counter, the hallway and the office. A room's enter_<room> section plays when
# you walk in (if it has anything to say). Clicking a thing plays its section.

=== arrival
narration | time=23:25: The key in your pocket fits the lock. You're sure it isn't your key.
narration | sound=bell: The door opens. A bell rings above it, small and bright, too cheerful for the dark.
narration | sound=door_shut | after=bell: The sound dies away. The door clicks shut behind you.
narration: The only light is the neon sign outside, bleeding red through the front windows. It reaches a few feet into the shop and gives up.
narration: No sound. Just a low mechanical hum, somewhere deeper in the dark. Steady. Patient.
narration: The air smells of cold metal, gun oil and old fur.
narration: Shapes in the dark. A glass counter. Antlers on the wall. Something standing very still in the corner.
narration: It's only a bear. It's only a stuffed bear.
paranoia: Why are you here, again?
paranoia: This place looks like it fell out of a cheap horror movie. And the longer you look at it, the less it looks like a movie.
paranoia: Don't make a sound.
paranoia: Not that it matters. There's no one here. Right?
unknown: Right. All alone.

# --- The counter. The first time you get there, the phone rings. ---

=== enter_counter
-> at_counter | if=!flag:phone_done

=== at_counter
narration: The floorboards wait until your full weight is on them, then complain. Then the cold glass of the counter, under your hands.
narration: A register, its drawer hanging open and empty. Beside it, a telephone. Black, heavy, old.
narration | sound=ring: It rings.
paranoia: Who calls a closed shop in the middle of the night?
narration: You haven't told anyone where you were going. There's nobody left to tell.
unknown: Aren't you going to get that?
> Pick up -> dead_line | break=phone
> Let it ring -> let_it_ring

=== dead_line
narration: You lift the receiver.
narration: No voice. No dial tone. Not a dead line: an open one.
narration: Somewhere on the other end, a room. A clock ticking in it.
narration: You hold your breath. On the other end, someone lets theirs out. Slowly. Like a man with all the time in the world.
narration: He doesn't speak. Neither do you.
narration | sound=deadline: Then a soft click on the other end, and he's gone. The line goes dead: a thin, steady tone in your ear, like a held note that will never end.
paranoia: Put it down.
> Hang up -> after_call

=== after_call
narration | flag=phone_done: You put it down. Carefully, as if it might notice.
-> office_door

=== let_it_ring
narration: You don't move. It rings. And rings.
paranoia: Let it. Whoever it is, let them wait.
narration | stop=ring | flag=phone_done: Then, in the middle of a ring, it stops.
narration: The silence afterwards is very loud.
-> office_door

=== office_door
narration | time=+5: Your eyes have adjusted to the dark now. Enough to notice what you missed.
narration | flag=office_noticed: Behind the counter, a door stands open a crack. A thin blade of yellow light lies across the floorboards.
paranoia: Was that on when you came in?
paranoia: It was. It must have been.

# Clicking the office door.
=== office_door_choice
narration: A metal plate is screwed to the door at eye level. You lean in close enough to read it: OFFICE.
narration: The blade of light lies across your shoes.
> Knock -> knock
> Push it open -> office

=== knock
narration | sound=knock: You knock. Twice. Softly, the way you'd knock on a hospital room door.
narration: Nothing.
narration | sound=knock_back: Then, from the other side, one knock back.
paranoia: Pipes. Old buildings knock. That's all that was.
unknown: Come in. Don't be rude.
-> office

=== office
narration | time=+5 | flag=office_open: You push the door. It swings inward without a sound, as if someone oiled the hinges for you.
narration | light=office: The light hurts your eyes. A bare bulb, far too bright, hanging from a cord.
narration: A small room. A wooden desk. An office chair, pushed back, as if someone just stood up.
narration: Above the desk, a ram's head, mounted, its horns curling back on themselves. Nothing else on the walls. Nothing else at all.
narration: And a smell. Sweet and heavy, underneath the dust. You can't name it. You don't want to.
paranoia: Breathe through your mouth.
narration: On the desk, squared neatly in the middle, an envelope. Propped up and waiting, as if it was put there for you to find.

# Clicking the envelope on the desk.
=== envelope_held
narration: It's heavier than a letter should be. The paper is soft, as if it's been handled many times.
narration: Three words on the front: FOR THE KEEPER. The handwriting presses so hard you can feel it through the other side.
paranoia: Whoever wrote this was very sure you'd come.
narration: Under the bulb, the ink is sharp and black. You won't have any trouble reading it.
> Open it -> @open_letter

=== after_letter
-> after_letter_answered | if=answered_phone
-> after_letter_ignored

=== after_letter_answered
paranoia: Rule four.
paranoia: You'd broken it before you even knew it existed.
unknown: You didn't speak first, though. Good instincts.
narration: Out in the dark, the hum goes on. Now you can place it: behind a heavy steel door at the very back of the shop. A compressor. A freezer, maybe.
-> upstairs

=== after_letter_ignored
paranoia: Rule four. You didn't answer.
paranoia: Not knowing saved you. It won't, next time.
unknown: They'll call again. They always call again.
narration: Out in the dark, the hum goes on. Now you can place it: behind a heavy steel door at the very back of the shop. A compressor. A freezer, maybe.
-> upstairs

=== upstairs
narration | time=00:10 | flag=letter_read: Your legs are heavy. You've been driving since before it got dark, and for a long time before that.
narration: The offer said there'd be a room upstairs. Room and board.
paranoia: It said that to John.
paranoia: Go up. Lock the door. Don't think about it.

# --- Walking around (spots.cfg). Until the letter's read, everything points
# back to the office. ---

=== look_bear_night
narration: The bear in the corner, reared up on its hind legs, taller than you. Its mouth open on a roar nobody heard.
narration: The neon catches one glass eye. Only one.
paranoia: It's only a bear. Say it again.
paranoia: It's only a bear.

=== look_furs_night
narration: Fur coats on racks along the wall, hanging from their shoulders. In the dark they stand like people waiting their turn.
paranoia: Don't count them.

=== door_early
-> door_before_phone | if=!flag:phone_done
-> door_before_letter

=== door_before_phone
narration: The door, behind you. You locked it. You think you locked it.
paranoia: You just got here.

=== door_before_letter
narration: You put your hand on the cold glass of the door. Outside, your car, already going white.
narration: Behind you, across the whole dark shop, that blade of yellow light under the office door.
paranoia: You came all this way. Look first. Then run, if you want.

=== door_late
narration: Outside, your car, going white. The road, going nowhere you can see.
paranoia: And go where? You haven't got anywhere. That's why you're here.

=== look_phone_night
narration: The phone. Quiet now. You don't touch it.
paranoia: It'll ring again. Don't wait for it.

=== look_register_night
narration: The register drawer hangs open. Not a coin in it. Not even a paper clip.
paranoia: Whoever cleaned it out took their time.

=== look_shelves_night
narration: Shelves behind the counter. Things with paper tags on strings: a rifle, a box, a clock that's stopped. Held, not for sale. Left here as a promise to come back.

=== stairs_not_yet
narration: The stairs climb into the dark along the wall. The offer said there'd be a room up there.
paranoia: Not yet. Whatever's in that office was left for somebody.
paranoia: You're somebody.

=== stairs_up
narration: You climb. Every step has its own complaint.

=== look_fuse_night
narration: A gray fuse box on the wall. Three switches held down with yellowed tape. Under them, a label: SHOP LIGHTS. DON'T.
paranoia: That's why it's so dark. Somebody wanted it dark.

=== steel_door_early
narration: The heavy steel door at the end of the hallway. The hum is behind it, louder here. You can feel it in your teeth.
narration: The handle is cold enough to hurt.
paranoia: Not tonight. Not before the office.

=== steel_door_late
narration: The steel door. The hum behind it, steady as breathing.
narration: A compressor, you tell yourself. A freezer.
paranoia: Not tonight.

=== look_ram_night
narration: The ram's head above the desk. Its horns curl back on themselves until they nearly close. Its glass eyes hold the bulb, two small hot points.
paranoia: Somebody put real care into this one.

=== look_chair_night
narration: The chair, pushed back from the desk at an angle, as if someone stood up in a hurry.
narration: You put your hand on the seat. It's cold. Of course it's cold.
