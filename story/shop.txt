# THE SHOP: ARRIVAL

=== arrival
narration: The key in your pocket fits the lock. You're sure it isn't your key.
narration | sound=ring: The door opens on a dark that smells of cold metal, gun oil and old fur. Somewhere at the back, a phone is ringing.
paranoia: Who calls a closed shop in the middle of the night?
narration: Shapes in the dark. A glass counter. Antlers on the wall. Something standing very still in the corner.
narration: It's only a bear. It's only a stuffed bear.
narration: The phone sits on the counter by the register. Black, heavy, old. Still ringing.
unknown: Aren't you going to get that, sweetheart?

=== dead_line
narration: You lift the receiver.
narration: No voice. No dial tone. Just a thin, steady beep, as if the line was never connected to anything.
narration: For a moment you think you hear breathing on the other end. Then you realize it's yours.
paranoia: Put it down.

=== after_call
narration: You put it down. Carefully, as if it might notice.

=== let_it_ring
narration: You don't move. It rings. And rings.
paranoia: Let it. Whoever it is, let them wait.
narration | stop=ring: Then, in the middle of a ring, it stops.
narration: The silence afterwards is very loud.

=== envelope
narration: Under the phone, an envelope. Not your name. Just three words, in that same handwriting: FOR THE KEEPER.

=== after_letter_answered
paranoia: Rule four.
paranoia: You'd broken it before you even knew it existed.
unknown: You didn't speak first, though. Good instincts, sweetheart.
narration: Somewhere at the back of the shop, behind a heavy door, a compressor hums.

=== after_letter_ignored
paranoia: Rule four. You didn't answer.
paranoia: Not knowing saved you. It won't, next time.
unknown: They'll call again, sweetheart. They always call again.
narration: Somewhere at the back of the shop, behind a heavy door, a compressor hums.
