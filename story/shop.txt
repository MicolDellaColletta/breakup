# THE SHOP: ARRIVAL

=== arrival
narration | time=23:25: The key in your pocket fits the lock. You're sure it isn't your key.
narration | sound=bell: The door opens. A bell rings above it, small and bright, too cheerful for the dark.
narration | sound=door_shut | after=bell: The sound dies away. The door clicks shut behind you.
narration: The only light is the neon sign outside, bleeding red through the front windows. It reaches a few feet into the shop and gives up.
narration: No sound. Just a low mechanical hum, somewhere deeper in the dark. Steady. Patient.
narration: The air smells of cold metal, gun oil and old fur.
narration: Shapes in the dark. A glass counter. Antlers on the wall. Something standing very still in the corner.
narration: It's only a bear. It's only a stuffed bear.
paranoia: Why are you here, again?
paranoia: This place looks like it fell out of a cheap horror movie. And the longer you look at it, the less it looks like a movie.
paranoia: Don't make a sound.
paranoia: Not that it matters. There's no one here. Right?

narration: Your hands find the cold glass of the counter. A register, its drawer hanging open and empty. Beside it, a telephone. Black, heavy, old.
narration | sound=ring: It rings.
paranoia: Who calls a closed shop in the middle of the night?
narration: Nobody knows you're here. You didn't know you'd be here until an hour ago.
unknown: Aren't you going to get that, sweetheart?

=== dead_line
narration: You lift the receiver.
narration: No voice. No dial tone. Not a dead line: an open one.
narration: Somewhere on the other end, a room. A clock ticking in it.
narration: You hold your breath. On the other end, someone lets theirs out. Slowly. Like a man with all the time in the world.
narration: He doesn't speak. Neither do you.
paranoia: Put it down.

=== let_it_ring

paranoia: Let it. Whoever it is, let them wait.

=== office_door
narration | time=+5: Your eyes have adjusted to the dark now. Enough to notice what you missed.
narration: Behind the counter, a door stands open a crack. A thin blade of yellow light lies across the floorboards.
paranoia: Was that on when you came in?
paranoia: It was. It must have been.
narration: A metal plate is screwed to the door at eye level. You lean in close enough to read it: OFFICE.

=== knock
narration | sound=knock: You knock. Twice. Softly, the way you'd knock on a hospital room door.
narration: Nothing.
narration | sound=knock_back: Then, from the other side, one knock back.
paranoia: Pipes. Old buildings knock. That's all that was.
unknown: Come in, sweetheart. Don't be rude.
-> office

=== office
narration | time=+5: You push the door. It swings inward without a sound, as if someone oiled the hinges for you.
narration | light=office: The light hurts your eyes. A bare bulb, far too bright, hanging from a cord.
narration: A small room. A wooden desk. An office chair, pushed back, as if someone just stood up.
narration: Above the desk, a ram's head, mounted, its horns curling back on themselves. Nothing else on the walls. Nothing else at all.
narration: And a smell. Sweet and heavy, underneath the dust. You can't name it. You don't want to.
paranoia: Breathe through your mouth.
narration: On the desk, squared neatly in the middle, an envelope. Propped up and waiting, as if it was put there for you to find.

paranoia: Whoever wrote this was very sure you'd come.
narration: Under the bulb, the ink is sharp and black. You won't have any trouble reading it.
