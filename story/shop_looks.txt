# THE SHOP AFTER CLOSING: things to look at (story/spots.cfg)
# Same format as drive.txt (see the notes at the top of that file).
# Shared by every day at the counter: the counter loads the day's story, then
# adds these. Lines with | if=day>=2 only show from day two on. Some things
# aren't quite where they were the day before (shop_art.gd draws them moved):
# the bear's head, the balance, the office chair. The ledger gets a line.
# The front door (a button, not a story line) leads out to the town map.

=== look_counter_drawer
narration | time=+5 | sound=drawer: The counter drawer sticks, then gives. Receipt books. Rubber stamps. A box of blank pawn tickets, numbered in order.
narration | if=day==1 and !broke:warm: You thumb through them. 0432, 0433, 0434. They pick up right where the ledger's numbers stop.
narration | if=day==1 and broke:warm: You thumb through them. 0433, 0434, 0435. The stub of 0432 is still in the book, in your handwriting. Walt Pruitt.
narration | if=day>=2: You thumb through them. The stubs of this week's tickets, in your handwriting, and then the blanks, waiting.
narration | if=!flag:ezra_satisfied | flag=seen_shells: Pushed to the very back, a single box of .22 shells. A slip of paper under the rubber band, in the owner's hand: THURS.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Nothing anywhere near 0527. Somebody skipped ahead to write that one. On purpose.
paranoia: Then where did yours come from?

=== look_balance
narration | time=+5: A brass balance at the end of the counter: two shallow pans on a beam, the kind for weighing gold dust.
narration | if=day<2: Both pans are empty. The left one sits a little lower than the other anyway.
paranoia | if=day<2: It's old. It's out of true. That's all it is.
narration | if=day>=2: Both pans are empty. Today it's the right one that sits lower.
paranoia | if=day>=2: Yesterday it was the left. You're sure it was the left.
appraisal | if=day>=2 and appraisal>=1: A balance doesn't drift from one side to the other. Something has to be put in the pan.

=== look_bear
narration | time=+5: The bear in the corner. Up close it's bigger than you thought, reared up, mouth open on a roar nobody heard.
narration: A cardboard tag hangs from one claw, in the owner's small hand: NOT FOR SALE. NEVER.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: It has no price in the ledger, either. Only a year. Every item gets a price, the letter said. Even the ones you'd never sell.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: Somebody stood this where they'd have to look at it every day. Not love. Something owed.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=2: The seams along the belly were sewn twice. Years apart. The newer thread is a different color.
narration | if=day>=2: Its head is turned a little further toward the counter than you remember. Toward where you stand all day.
paranoia | if=day>=2: You remember wrong. You've been here two days. You don't remember anything yet.

=== look_front_window
narration | time=+5: The front window. The neon on the snow, your car going white in the lot, the dark line of the trees.
narration: In the glass, your own reflection, faint, laid over all of it. It does what you do.
paranoia: It's not midnight. Rule eight is for after midnight.
paranoia: Remember that.
narration | if=day>=2: Low in one corner of the glass, in the frost, a handprint. Small. A child's, or a woman's.
narration | if=day>=2: You put your own hand against it, to be sure. It's on the inside.

=== look_ram
narration | time=+5: The ram's head above the desk. Its horns curl back so far they nearly close into circles.
narration: Someone has wedged a sprig of dried spruce into one horn.
narration: Its glass eyes are set exactly right. Whoever mounted the fox didn't mount this.

=== look_chair
narration | time=+5 | if=day<2: The office chair, rolled back from the desk, turned a little toward the door. As if someone stood up in a hurry and never came back to sit down.
narration | time=+5 | if=day>=2: The office chair is pushed in to the desk. Square. Neat.
narration | if=day>=2: Yesterday it was rolled back, turned toward the door. You didn't touch it. You'd remember touching it.
paranoia | if=day>=2: Someone sat here last night. Someone tidied up after.

=== look_desk
narration | time=+10 | sound=drawer: The desk drawers. Pens, a stapler, a ring of small keys that don't look like they open anything in this room.
narration: In the bottom drawer, a sheaf of thin carbon copies, clipped together.
narration | take=carbon_copies: Typed letters. You know the first line of them by heart. You take them.
paranoia: Read them later. Or don't.

=== look_fuse_box
narration | time=+5 | sound=latch: In the hallway, a gray fuse box. Most of the switches are up. Three are held down with yellowed tape.
narration: Under them, a label in the owner's hand: SHOP LIGHTS. DON'T.
paranoia: That's why it's always dark in here. He wanted it that way. Forty winters, sitting down here in the dark.
paranoia: You will too. You already are.

=== look_steel_door
narration | time=+5: The heavy steel door at the end of the hallway. Behind it, the hum.
narration: You put your palm flat on it. The metal is cold, and it shivers, very slightly, in time with the hum.
paranoia: Not now. Tonight, if you have to. Not now.

# --- The back room, behind the steel door (from day two) ---
# The freezer is padlocked until December (docs/shop.md). Nothing here is
# taken yet: the bench's tools, the crates and the furnace come later.

=== look_freezer
narration | time=+5: The freezer. A walk-in, its door as big as a closet. The hum comes out of it, steady as breathing.
narration: A padlock on the hasp. Heavy, brass, new: the shackle still bright, a scrap of price sticker on the body. Nothing else back here is new.
narration: You put your hand flat on the door. Cold. Colder than the room, colder than the snow outside. Frost has grown out of the seal in little white ferns.
narration: On the floor in front of it, the boards are darker than the rest, as if they'd been wet and dried. A patch the size of a man lying down.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: A Master lock, the kind the hardware store in Palmer sells off a card. Whoever locked this went and bought a lock for it.
paranoia: Don't listen at it. Whatever you do.
> Put your ear to the door -> freezer_listen | flag=listened_freezer | lean=animal
> Take your hand away -> freezer_leave | lean=paranoia

=== freezer_listen
narration: The steel against your ear, so cold it burns. The hum, all through your skull.
narration: And under the hum, once, a small dry sound. Like someone on a crate shifting their weight.
appraisal: Compressors tick as they cool. Ice settles. Everybody knows that.
paranoia: Once. It only did it once. Because it heard you.

=== freezer_leave
narration: You take your hand away. The print of it stays on the frost a long moment, then fills in.

=== look_bench
narration | time=+5: The owner's workbench. A pegboard over it, every tool hanging inside its own outline, painted in white. Knives, scrapers, awls, curved needles, a fleshing beam.
narration: Every outline full but one. Something small, with a hook at the end.
narration: On the bench, a stoat in its white winter coat, half drawn over its form. The stitches stop in the middle of the belly. The needle's still threaded, hanging from the last one, turning slowly in the draught.
narration: Pinned to the pegboard, a feed-store calendar on October. The third is circled, twice, in pencil.
paranoia: October third. Two days before you came. He put down the needle in the middle of a stitch.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: The tools are clean. Oiled. Somebody loved this work.

=== look_glass_eyes
narration | time=+5: A baby-food jar of glass eyes, sorted by size, small at the top. Amber for owls. Black for bears. A yellow-green that can only be a fox.
narration | flag=seen_human_eyes: At the very bottom, one pair that isn't any animal's. White, with a brown iris, the veins painted in, fine as hair.
paranoia: People's. Those are people's eyes.
appraisal: Doll makers buy them. Mannequins. The supply catalogs sell them by the pair.
paranoia: Brown. Whose were brown?

=== look_back_room_door
narration | time=+5: The back door. A deadbolt, and a keyhole under it, and the cold coming in around the frame.
narration: Just inside it, the snow that blows in under the door has been swept into a neat pile by the frame. You've never swept it.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked at night. Something uses this door.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Somebody with a broom and a key. Olstad has a key.

=== look_furnace
narration | time=+5: The furnace. Squat, black iron, its belly glowing through the grate. The heat of it on your face from three steps away.
narration | if=flag:fed_furnace: It's lit. It's always lit. You've fed it once, one night, three shovels. The coal scuttle beside it is full to the brim, and the ash pan's been emptied.
narration | if=!flag:fed_furnace: It's lit. It's always lit. You've never once fed it. The coal scuttle beside it is full to the brim, and the ash pan's been emptied.
paranoia: Somebody comes in and feeds it. While you sleep. While you stand at the counter.
appraisal: Somebody with a key could do it on their rounds. A furnace this old wants feeding twice a day.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: Somebody doesn't want you cold.

=== look_crates
narration | time=+5: Crates, stacked in the corner, stencilled with the names of towns. PALMER. WASILLA. KNIK. The lids are nailed down.
narration: Unsorted stock. Whatever's in them is the shop's too, and isn't in the ledger yet.
narration: One lid has been opened and nailed shut again. The new nails are bright.
narration: A crowbar leans against the stack.
paranoia: Not tonight. Whatever's in that one can wait.
