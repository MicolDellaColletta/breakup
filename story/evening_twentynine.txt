# THE EVENING OF KEY DAY 29: Saturday, January 15. Olstad at the ruins.
# A walked evening (scenes/visit.tscn; GameState.EVENINGS), in the ruins of
# the church. A week after the baby, Olstad brings MC up the hill at night to
# show him what she's kept: the vestry bolt, in an evidence bag. What she's
# building, and whether MC will say it in front of them, when it comes to it.
# end=night. Design: docs/december_revised.md, docs/january.md.

=== evening
narration | show=ruins | time=19:00: The ruin, at night. The scaffold against the tower stump, and on top of it now, finished, a thing of new pine: a cross, or a mast, or a gallows. You can't tell from below. The snow inside the walls trodden flat, and the candles in it out.
narration: Olstad, waiting by the black square where the vestry was. A week since the baby. She's thin in the face, and her coat closes now, and she's holding a paper evidence bag the way you'd hold a hymn book.
trooper: I left her with my sister in Wasilla. First night I've been away from her. I came back for this.
-> evening_bolt_told | if=flag:told_olstad_fire
-> evening_bolt_found

=== evening_bolt_told
trooper: You told me about the bolt. With him sitting there. So I came up here the next morning and I took it off what was left of the post with a hacksaw.
-> evening_bolt

=== evening_bolt_found
trooper: Nobody told me about the bolt. I found it myself, the morning after, with my hands, in the ash. Shot. On the outside. Nobody bolts a door from the outside unless there's somebody in.
-> evening_bolt

=== evening_bolt
narration: She opens the bag. An iron bolt, black, a piece of charred wood still on it. Drawn across. Fused that way.
narration | if=flag:spectacles_to_olstad: "And the spectacles. Anchorage says ordinary glass, ordinary lenses, no cracks. They won't say more till somebody official asks them to. I'm somebody official. They don't think so."
narration | if=flag:spectacles_to_church: "The spectacles would have done it. Ezra buried them. In the ground. In January. Do you know what it takes to dig a grave in January?"
trooper: Brekke signed it. The county'll read what he signed, because there was nobody else to sign anything. And he's got a whole town that'll say they saw an old man go in to pray and fall asleep.
trooper: I need one person to say different. Out loud. In front of them. When it comes to it.
narration: She closes the bag.
trooper: Will you? When it comes to it?
paranoia: In front of them. In front of him. You saw the town sing.
