# THE EVENING OF KEY DAY 32: Friday, February 11. Ruth's lie.
# A walked evening (scenes/visit.tscn; GameState.EVENINGS) in the shut
# Gleaner. Ruth, drunk, with her mother's bowl and her father's scarf: the
# 1989 Keeper didn't run. She drove him to the highway herself, and her
# mother died that night in the empty shop. Read differently if MC has the
# 1989 letter, her diary, or has seen her room. The car keys on the table.
# Forgive her (her third door) or don't. Daniel at the end of the bar, the
# whole time. end=night. Design: docs/february.md, docs/forks.md.

=== evening
narration | show=gleaner | time=21:00: The back door of the Gleaner, unlocked. Nobody locks anything now. The kitchen, one bulb, and through the swing door, the bar, dark, the chairs up on the tables like legs.
narration: And one table down, in the middle, with a candle. Ruth. A bottle. Her mother's blue bowl in front of her, empty. Her father's scarf.
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys: And on the table, by the bowl, a ring of keys. Yours. The rental's tag.
ruth: You came. Sit down. Don't take a chair down, sit on the table, I don't care.
narration: The wall of faces, in the dark, behind her. The tinsel still on some of them.
-> evening_letter | if=has:letter_1989
-> evening_tell

=== evening_letter
narration: You take the letter out of your coat. March 1989. You lay it on the table by the bowl, face up, the way you'd lay down a card.
narration: She reads it. "She says she'll drive me." She reads it again. She puts her finger on the place where it stops.
ruth: He gave it to Dad to post. Dad kept it. Of course Dad kept it.
-> evening_lie

=== evening_tell
ruth: I want to tell you something. I've been drinking since four so I could tell you something.
-> evening_lie

=== evening_lie
ruth: I told you he ran. The Keeper. Eighty-nine. I tell everyone he ran.
ruth: He didn't run. I drove him. The night before breakup, when they were all at the church. I had Dad's truck. I was thirty and I was so in love with him I couldn't see.
ruth: I drove him to the highway. I watched him get a ride with a trucker. I watched the tail-lights. I came home at three in the morning and I was happy. I was so happy.
ruth: And the shop was empty. No Keeper. And Mom knew. Mom knew what happens if the table's not set. So she went and sat in the shop. In his chair. In the dark. So it wouldn't be empty.
narration: She stops. She drinks.
ruth: They found her at breakup. In the morning. On the ice, off the point, where they always are. In her nightdress.
ruth: Every Keeper since. Every one. I keep them in town. I keep their keys. I take their picture. So it's never empty again. So it's never her again.
narration | if=has:ruth_diary: You know. You read it in her diary, in her room: "Mom says she'll go and sit in the shop so it's not empty." You don't tell her you know. Or she sees it in your face.
narration | if=flag:saw_locked_room: She looks at you. "You've been in my room. Haven't you. You saw his coat. I couldn't give it back. I couldn't give it to the shop."
narration | if=has:keeper_photo: You put the photograph on the table, by the bowl. The young man on the shop step, in red flannel, squinting.
ruth | if=has:keeper_photo: David.
narration | if=has:keeper_photo: She touches the edge of it. Not his face.
ruth: He didn't even take his case. He got in the truck in his shirt. Dad put the case in a crate and never said a word about it.
ruth: Mom painted that one over your armchair. That spring. She sat out on the point every afternoon with her paints. There was nobody on the ice when she did.
ruth: The bowl started the winter after. The steel one, in your kitchen. Dad put it down the first winter without her and filled it every night. I've never been able to look at it.
ruth: And every fall he went away. To Anchorage, to the paper. He'd come home with a name.
ruth | if=doors:ruth>=2: I still come and sit. At night, at his table. I've got the keys. I tape the bell so it won't wake you. I make two cups. I sit in his chair till it's warm and I go home.
ruth | if=doors:ruth>=2: The ledger. "Oct 12. One week." The card on the furnace. That's not him. I did that. I've done his hand since I was nine.
ruth | if=doors:ruth>=2: I wanted it to be his shop one more winter. That's all. One more.
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys: She pushes the ring of keys an inch toward you. Then stops, with her hand flat on them.
narration | red=on: At the end of the bar, in the dark, where the light from the candle doesn't reach, a chair creaks. Somebody's been sitting there the whole time. Neither of you looks.
paranoia: Him. He's been here the whole time. He heard all of it.

=== evening_refuse

=== evening_home
narration: At the end of the bar, the chair creaks again. A man stands up in the dark, and puts on a good coat. On his way past he stops at your table, lifts the candle, and holds it up to Ruth's wet face, then to yours, a while each, as if comparing them.
narration | red=off: He sets the candle down where it was and goes out through the kitchen without a word. The back door, softly.
narration | end=night | time=23:30: You walk home. Behind you, the candle in the Gleaner goes out.
