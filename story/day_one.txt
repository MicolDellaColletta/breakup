# Same format as drive.txt (see the notes at the top of that file).
# Counter scenes also understand:
#   | sold=duck_call     the item is sold: off the shelf, into the till and the ledger
#   > Let me look -> @browse   the player looks for an item to offer
# A customer's sections: <id>_enters, then <id>_given_<item> or
# <id>_given_other when something is offered, and <id>_nothing when refused.
# When a section ends with nowhere to go, the next customer comes in.

# --- Walt Pruitt. Pawning thirty silver dollars. Rule two. One or two visits. ---

=== seller_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. An old man in a sheepskin coat, moving slowly, as if every step had to be argued for.
narration: He doesn't look at the shelves. He comes straight to the counter and sets down a paper roll of coins.
seller: Walt Pruitt. I'd like to pawn these. Thirty silver dollars. Morgans, every one.
narration: You pick up the roll.
narration: It's warm. Not pocket-warm. Warm the way a cup is warm a minute after someone has set it down.
paranoia: Rule two. Don't buy anything that's still warm.
seller: They were my wife's. She collected them. She won't be needing them now.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Thirty Morgan dollars. Worth a great deal more than thirty, to the right buyer.
> Offer him fifteen -> seller_low | lean=appraisal | break=warm
> Offer him thirty -> seller_fair | lean=warmth | break=warm
> Offer him forty-five -> seller_high | lean=john | break=warm
> "I can't take these." -> seller_refuse | lean=paranoia

=== seller_low
narration | pawned=silver_dollars | loan=15: He looks at the fifteen dollars for a long time. Then he takes it, folds it once, and puts it away.
seller: Fifteen. All right. She always said I never could bargain.
-> seller_ticket

=== seller_fair
narration | pawned=silver_dollars | loan=30: Thirty dollars. He nods, as if that were the number he'd expected all along.

-> seller_ticket

=== seller_high

=== seller_ticket

=== seller_refuse
