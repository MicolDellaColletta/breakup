# DAY ONE
# Same format as drive.txt (see the notes at the top of that file).
# Counter scenes also understand:
#   | sold=duck_call     the item is sold: off the shelf, into the till and the ledger
#   > Let me look -> @browse   the player looks for an item to offer
# A customer's sections: <id>_enters, then <id>_given_<item> or
# <id>_given_other when something is offered, and <id>_nothing when refused.
# When a section ends with nowhere to go, the next customer comes in.

=== morning
narration | time=08:40: Morning comes late this far north. At twenty to nine it's still full dark outside.
-> morning_fed | if=fed_dog
-> morning_unfed

=== morning_fed
narration: The bowl by the stove is empty. Licked clean, and pushed a few inches from where you left it.
paranoia: Mice. Big mice.
-> opening

=== morning_unfed
narration: The bowl by the stove is where you left it. Empty.
narration: On the door at the bottom of the stairs, low down, there are long pale scratches in the paint. They weren't there last night.
paranoia: You didn't feed it.
paranoia: There is no it.
-> opening

=== opening
narration: You go down into the dark shop. Something pale lies on the mat inside the front door.
narration: An envelope, pushed through the gap underneath. FOR THE KEEPER, typed on a label, like the job offer.
narration | cash=+150: Inside, three fifties, folded once. Nothing else.
paranoia: Somebody knows you're here. Somebody's paying you to stay.
narration: You turn the sign in the window. OPEN. The neon outside buzzes and goes pale as the sky starts to gray.
narration | time=09:00: Behind the counter, everything is where you left it. The phone. The register, still open, still empty. The shelves.
paranoia: Just act like you know what you're doing.

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+40: The bell over the door. A woman stamps the snow off her boots on the mat, and keeps stamping long after it's gone.
narration: Sixties, maybe. A man's parka, much too big for her, zipped to the chin.
widow: You're the new one.
widow: John, they said.
paranoia: Answer to it.
> "That's right." -> widow_ask | lean=john
> Say nothing -> widow_silent | lean=paranoia

=== widow_silent
widow: Quiet one. That's all right. Ray was quiet.
-> widow_ask

=== widow_ask
widow: I need something. For my husband. Ray. Ray Hollis.
widow: He went out on the lake three weeks ago, before the ice was any good. They found the truck. They haven't found him.
> Tell her you're sorry to hear it. -> widow_kind | lean=warmth
> Ask what it's worth to her. -> widow_price | lean=appraisal
> [John] "Ray mentioned you. Said you'd come in." -> widow_lie | needs=john | lean=john

=== widow_kind
widow: Everybody's sorry. Sorry doesn't find anybody.
narration: But she looks at you a moment longer, as if this time it counted for something.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: She hasn't said he's dead. She won't be the first to say it.
-> widow_need

=== widow_price
widow: Whatever it costs. That's what money's for, isn't it.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Ones and fives, folded in her fist. She counted them before she came in, and she's counting them again.
-> widow_need

=== widow_lie
widow: Did he.
widow: Ray didn't talk about me to anybody. Not in forty years.
narration: She studies your face, as if she's trying to place it.
john: Too much. Pull it back. Smile.
paranoia: She'll remember that. They always remember the lie, not the face.
-> widow_need

=== widow_need
widow: The pastor says I should put something in the ground anyway. Something he loved.
widow: Every fall since he was a boy, he sat in a blind out on the north shore. Before dawn. Talking to them, he said. He said they talked back.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Before dawn, hidden, talking to them. Not with a gun. With something that sounds like them.
widow: Last winter was bad. He sold things, one at a time, and didn't tell me. Some of it ended up here, I'd bet.
paranoia: She means something specific. Look.
> Let me look -> @browse
> I don't think I can help -> widow_nothing

# --- Sgt. Marit Olstad, Alaska State Troopers. Not a customer. ---

=== trooper_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+50: The bell. Cold air, and a woman in a Trooper's parka who doesn't bother to stamp her boots.
narration: Late thirties. Pregnant, six months or more. The parka won't close over her belly anymore, and she's let it hang open as if the cold were beneath her notice.
narration: She takes her time looking at the shop before she looks at you.
trooper: Ohh, so you're the new one.
trooper: Sergeant Olstad. Troopers. Anything out past the town line is mine, and that's most everything, hon.
trooper: John, ya?
> "That's right." -> trooper_john | lean=john
> Take a second too long. -> trooper_slow | lean=paranoia
> "Who's asking?" -> trooper_who | lean=animal

=== trooper_john
trooper: Ya. John. Real good.
narration: She says it the way you'd test a step on a frozen pond.
-> trooper_car

=== trooper_slow
narration: You open your mouth. Nothing comes out for a moment that goes on too long.
trooper: Oh, for cute. Takes a while to get used to, a new name. Or a new place. I meant a new place.
paranoia: She didn't mean a new place.
-> trooper_car

=== trooper_who
trooper: Who's asking. Ohh, I like that.
narration: She taps the badge on her chest, twice, without looking down at it.
trooper: The State of Alaska's asking, Johnny. But nicely. For now.
-> trooper_car

=== trooper_car
trooper: Real cold one last night. Guess you're not used to it yet. You'll learn.
trooper: Or you won't. Some don't.
trooper: Saw a sedan come up the road late. One headlight dimmer than the other. That yours, Johnny?
> "It's a rental." -> trooper_rental | lean=appraisal
> "Must have been somebody else." -> trooper_somebody | lean=john

=== trooper_rental
trooper: Ohh, I know it's a rental, hon. Northern Lights, Anchorage. Little sticker on the bumper.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: She read the bumper in the dark, from a moving cruiser. Or she's seen the paperwork.
-> trooper_looks

=== trooper_somebody
trooper: Huh. Somebody else. Out here.
trooper: Funny, there's only the one car parked outside. Blue. Dim headlight. Must be a real popular model.
paranoia: Stop talking. Every word is a mile of rope.
-> trooper_looks

=== trooper_looks
narration: She wanders while she talks. Picks up the brass compass, weighs it, sets it down a little to the left of where it was.
narration: She stops at the fox and leans in until she's nose to nose with it.
trooper: Oh, for cute. Eyes are wrong. Old man always got the eyes wrong.
narration: Then she's behind the counter, where she has no reason to be, at the back shelves. Her fingers find the tin box with tag 0527 and stay there.
trooper: What's in the tin, Johnny?
> "I don't know. It was here when I came." -> trooper_tin_truth | lean=warmth
> "That one's not for sale." -> trooper_tin_keeper | lean=john

=== trooper_tin_truth
trooper: Ya. Lot of things here were here when you came. That's kind of the job, isn't it.
-> trooper_leaves

=== trooper_tin_keeper
trooper: Everything's for sale in a pawn shop, hon. That's the whole idea.
narration: She sets it back down. Exactly where it was, this time.
-> trooper_leaves

=== trooper_leaves
trooper: Folks get together at the pub tonight. Eight o'clock. You should come. People like to see the new Keeper.
trooper | set=invited_to_pub: Gives them something to talk about besides the weather.
narration: At the door she stops, digs in her parka, and comes back. A key on a red tag lands on the glass.
trooper | take=back_door_key: Old man gave me this a few winters back. Spare for the back door. In case.
trooper: Figured you'd want it.
trooper: Don't lock it, though. He never did.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, and the cold, and she's gone.
paranoia: Why do the police have a key to this place?
unknown: She likes you, sweetheart. You can tell.

=== look_counter_drawer
narration | time=+5: The counter drawer sticks, then gives. Receipt books. Rubber stamps. A box of blank pawn tickets, numbered in order.
narration: You thumb through them. 0432, 0433, 0434. They pick up right where the ledger's numbers stop.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Nothing anywhere near 0527. Somebody skipped ahead to write that one. On purpose.
paranoia: Then where did yours come from?

=== look_balance
narration | time=+5: A brass balance at the end of the counter: two shallow pans on a beam, the kind for weighing gold dust.
narration: Both pans are empty. One of them sits a little lower than the other anyway.
paranoia: It's old. It's out of true. That's all it is.

=== look_bear
narration | time=+5: The bear in the corner. Up close it's bigger than you thought, reared up, mouth open on a roar nobody heard.
narration: A cardboard tag hangs from one claw, in the owner's small hand: NOT FOR SALE. NEVER.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: It isn't in the ledger, either. Every item gets a price, the letter said. Even the ones you'd never sell.
warmth | if=warmth>=1: Somebody loved this ugly thing.

=== look_front_window
narration | time=+5: The front window. The neon on the snow, your car going white in the lot, the dark line of the trees.
narration: In the glass, your own reflection, faint, laid over all of it. It does what you do.
paranoia: It's not midnight. Rule eight is for after midnight.
paranoia: Remember that.

=== look_ram
narration | time=+5: The ram's head above the desk. Its horns curl back so far they nearly close into circles.
narration: Someone has wedged a sprig of dried spruce into one horn, as if the ram had been caught in a thicket.
narration: Its glass eyes are set exactly right. Whoever mounted the fox didn't mount this.

=== look_desk
narration | time=+10: The desk drawers. Pens, a stapler, a ring of small keys that don't look like they open anything in this room.
narration: In the bottom drawer, a sheaf of thin carbon copies, clipped together.
narration | take=carbon_copies: Typed letters. You know the first line of them by heart. You take them.
paranoia: Read them later. Or don't.

=== look_fuse_box
narration | time=+5: In the hallway, a gray fuse box. Most of the switches are up. Three are held down with yellowed tape.
narration: Under them, a label in the owner's hand: SHOP LIGHTS. DON'T.
paranoia: That's why it's always dark in here. He wanted it that way.
paranoia: Or something did.

=== look_steel_door
narration | time=+5: The heavy steel door at the end of the hallway. Behind it, the hum.
narration: You put your palm flat on it. The metal is cold, and it shivers, very slightly, in time with the hum.
paranoia: Not now. Tonight, if you have to. Not now.
