# KEY DAY 20: Monday, December 6, 1999. The first miracle.
# Same format as the other counter days: Mrs. Hollis with the news (the
# doctor's hands, at the Gleaner, last night); Dr. Brekke himself, to show
# MC; and at closing, him, with his father's watch, stopped at nine: "What
# will you give me for this?" See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd.
# Design: docs/december_revised.md.

=== morning
narration | time=07:30: Monday, the sixth. In the night, every door on the road was left open. You saw it from the window at three: a whole street of doors standing open into the dark, and nobody in them.
narration: In the morning they're shut again. Snow drifted in over every threshold, a little white tongue on every floor.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked. That's your rule. Not theirs.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:00: You turn the sign. OPEN.

# --- Mrs. Hollis, with the news. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, out of breath, her coat buttoned wrong.
widow: You'll have heard. You haven't heard. Dr. Brekke. At the Gleaner, last night.
widow: His hands. Thirty years his hands have shaken, he can't hold a cup, he gave up delivering babies in ninety. And last night he was sitting at the back table and the minister just reached across and held them. Both of them. For a minute. Didn't say anything.
widow: And they stopped. Still as a table. He picked up his coffee and drank it and didn't spill a drop and then he put his face in his hands and cried.
narration: She's crying a little too. She doesn't stop to.
widow: I've never seen anything. In my whole life. I've never seen one thing.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. The bell.

# --- Dr. Brekke. ---

=== brekke_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+120: The bell. An old man in a fur hat, tall, stooped, with a doctor's bag he doesn't need to carry anymore. Dr. Brekke. He comes to the counter and doesn't buy anything.
brekke: You're the Keeper. I wanted to show you. I've been showing everyone. I'm sorry. I can't stop.
narration: He takes his gloves off and holds his hands out over the glass, palms down. Old hands, spotted, the knuckles big.
narration: They don't move. Not a tremor. You watch them a long time. They don't move.
brekke: I delivered half this town. Hanna Lund. The Pruitt boy. Ruth, God help me, in that bar, in a snowstorm. And then my hands went and I couldn't anymore, and I sat in my kitchen for nine years.
brekke: He didn't say a word. He just held them. Like you'd hold a bird.
appraisal | if=appraisal>=1: Tremors come and go. Stress, cold, a new pill, a good night's sleep. It doesn't have to be anything.
unknown: It doesn't have to be anything. It is anyway.
brekke: I'd do anything for him now. Anything he asked. Isn't that a strange thing to feel, at seventy-eight?
narration | sound=bell: He puts his gloves back on, carefully, finger by finger, the way you'd put away something you'd borrowed. The bell.

# --- Him: the watch. ---

=== daniel_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+120 | red=alone: The bell, once. Twenty to four. The good coat. He takes something from his waistcoat pocket and lays it on the glass.
narration: A pocket watch. Steel, on a short chain, the case worn smooth by a thumb. Stopped. Nine o'clock.
daniel: What will you give me for this?
narration: The same words. The same patient face. As if the bullet had been a rehearsal.
narration: You put your hand out. It's warm.
paranoia: Rule two. Again. He keeps bringing you warm things. He keeps checking whether you'll take them.
> Write him a ticket for it -> daniel_watch_take | break=warm | flag=took_watch | lean=appraisal
> "I don't buy things like that." -> daniel_watch_refuse | flag=refused_watch | lean=paranoia
> "What's it worth to you?" -> daniel_watch_worth | lean=unknown

=== daniel_watch_take
narration | pawned=father_watch | loan=3: Three dollars. You write it in the ledger. He folds the stub and puts it inside his coat, over his heart, where the other one is.
daniel: My father's. It stopped the night I stopped believing him. I've never had it fixed. It works perfectly. It just won't go past nine.
narration: On the back shelf now, beside the tin. Warm. Stopped.
-> daniel_watch_goes

=== daniel_watch_refuse
narration: He nods. He picks it up, and winds it, three turns, and holds it to your ear across the counter.
narration: It ticks. A whole minute. You count. Then it stops.
narration: He opens the case and shows you. Nine o'clock.
daniel: It does that. Every time. It's very faithful.
narration: He puts it back in his waistcoat.
-> daniel_watch_goes

=== daniel_watch_worth
daniel: Everything I had at twelve.
narration: He waits to see if you'll ask what happened at twelve. You don't. He watches you not ask, the way you'd watch a dog decide against the meat.
daniel: Then I'll keep it. I only wanted to see your face when I said it. It did what I hoped.
narration: He puts it back in his waistcoat pocket. Through the cloth, very faintly, you'd swear you hear it ticking.
-> daniel_watch_goes

=== daniel_watch_goes
daniel: The doctor came to show you his hands. They all will, now. I held them for a minute, and he'll give me what's left of his life for it. That's the going rate up here. I'd have paid more.
daniel: Good evening.
narration | sound=bell: The bell, once. Up the road, the Gleaner. Through its window, every head turning to the door as he comes in, like flowers.

# --- Closing. ---

=== closing
narration | time=15:45: Quarter to four. Dark. You turn the sign. CLOSED.
narration: Up the road, through the Gleaner's window, the clock over the bar. Stopped at nine. You've never seen it stopped. You look at your own watch. It's going. You keep looking at it to make sure.
