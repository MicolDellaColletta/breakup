# THE FORTY-FIRST NIGHT: Sunday, April 16. After Palm Sunday.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: whatever's left on it, by what
# happened today. Out of place: the freezer's hum, changed (odd_freezer).
# Design: docs/april.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:daniel_shot: Your hands smell of the freezer. They'll smell of it for the rest of your life.
narration | if=flag:daniel_taken: The cruiser's tail-lights, going down the road, round the bend. You keep seeing them go.
narration | if=flag:daniel_given: Up the road, his window, dark.
narration | if=flag:gun_failed: He has the gun. He said he'd see you on the shore.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Sunday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click | if=flag:daniel_taken or flag:daniel_given or flag:daniel_shot: The clock. Only the clock, ticking, all night, with nobody's voice over it.
narration | sound=click | if=!flag:daniel_taken and !flag:daniel_given and !flag:daniel_shot: Him. "Six days, friends." Then, quieter, the way he talks to you: "Six days, Keeper."

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. The silence is the same.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fortyone
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_fortyone | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The windows.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold, and water, and somewhere far out, the ice making a sound like a door.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. You don't sleep.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_fortyone
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:00: Monday. Five days.

narration | time=+5: From downstairs, through the floor, the freezer's hum. It's changed.
narration | if=flag:daniel_shot | flag=heard_freezer: Deeper. Working harder. As if it had more to keep cold.
narration | if=!flag:daniel_shot | flag=heard_freezer: Higher. Thinner. As if it were waiting for something to keep cold.
