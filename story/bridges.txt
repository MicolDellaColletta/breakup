# BRIDGES: the days between key days (from week two on)
# Same format as the other story files. Each section is the bridge before a
# key day (GameState.BRIDGES). A line with card=... puts a title card up, in
# the town's voice, white on black like a silent film; the line's own text
# plays under it. When the section ends, the key day starts.
# Design: docs/week_two.md.

# --- Before key day 8 (Thursday, October 14): Wednesday the 13th. ---

=== bridge_wednesday
narration | card=Wednesday. The Keeper is paid. | cash=+150 | time=07:30: The envelope, under the door before dawn. A hundred and fifty dollars, every bill turned the same way up.
narration: Folded in with the money, this week's bulletin. Three hymn numbers circled, in pencil. One. Five. Nine.
paranoia: Nine. There's no rule nine. There's a rhyme with a nine in it.
narration | if=has:revolver: The revolver, where you left it. You check that it's where you left it. You check twice.
narration | if=!has:revolver: The snowbank by the back door, smooth and white. You don't look at it. You look at it.
narration | if=flag:hanna_priced or flag:hanna_blank or flag:hanna_refused: On the back shelf, the braid, still. Nobody's asked for it. Yet.
narration | time=22:00: At night, through the wall, the radio. Not the watch. The Reverend, preaching, a sermon he must have recorded on Sunday.
narration: "Seventy-nine days, friends. Seventy-nine days until the year turns over, and some say the world with it. I don't say that. I say: keep your stoves fed. Keep your doors the way you keep them. Keep each other."
narration: You lie awake counting. Seventy-nine. He's right. You don't know why you checked.

# --- Before key day 9 (Sunday, October 17): Friday and Saturday. ---

=== bridge_weekend
narration | card=Friday. The Keeper dresses the window. | time=08:40: The W on the calendar. The index card in the drawer, soft at the corners. You don't need to read it now.
narration | if=flag:drawing_in_window: June's drawing, taped inside the glass: the Keeper in the long brown coat, walking out onto blue ice. Everybody stopped. Everybody. Some of them twice.
narration | if=!flag:drawing_in_window and window:empty: The window, empty. Two people stop to look at the nothing in it. One of them crosses herself.
narration | if=!flag:drawing_in_window and !window:empty: The window keeps what you left in it. People slow, and look, and drive on to the Gleaner.
narration | card=Saturday. The Keeper is seen.: The Gleaner on a Saturday. Your stool.
narration | if=flag:broke_faith_ruth: Ruth serves you without looking at you, all night. Your glass never goes empty. Her eyes never come near it.
narration | if=flag:broke_faith_sam: Sam isn't in his corner. Sam is never in his corner. You notice anyway.
narration | if=!flag:broke_faith_ruth: Ruth leaves the bottle where you can reach it, which from her is a kind of speech.
narration: Both nights, after three: the stairs. One and then the other.
paranoia: Two nights you didn't play. Two nights you lived anyway. You don't remember them as well as you should.

# --- Before key day 10 (Saturday, October 23): Monday to Friday. ---

=== bridge_monday
narration | card=Monday. The Keeper is in good health. | time=22:00: The radio notices, Monday. The plow. A lost glove. The Ladies' Circle, Thursday at the Lunds'.
narration | if=flag:weighed or flag:weighed_again: "And our Keeper, friends, is in good health, and a little heavier, praise God."
narration | if=!flag:weighed and !flag:weighed_again: "And our Keeper, friends, is in our prayers. And in our thoughts on Thursdays."
narration | card=Tuesday.: The calendar by the stove. You've started crossing off the days. Nobody told you to.
narration: You look at the crosses a long time. The same pen. The same angle. The same little hook at the end of each stroke as Abraham's, all through September, up to the third.
paranoia: You copied him. Without looking. Your hand learned it from the wall.
narration | card=Wednesday. The Keeper is paid. | cash=+150: The envelope, under the door before dawn. A hundred and fifty, faced.
narration: And with the money, folded in, a strip of brown wool. Two inches long. Cut clean, as if snipped off a hem. No note.
paranoia: A swatch. They're showing you the cloth. The way a tailor would.
narration | card=Thursday. The Circle sews.: Ezra, the usual.
narration | if=flag:refused_tooth or flag:refused_tooth_public: He pays without a word, and looks at the jar the whole time.
narration: The Lunds' windows lit all evening. From the shop, if you stand still, you can just hear the sewing machine.
narration: The call, at eleven. The room, the clock. And footsteps, now. Slow. And something tapping the floor with them. A stick, or a cane. He's up.
narration | card=Friday. The road closes.: Snow all day, thick and soft and sideways.
narration: At four, the radio: the pass is drifted in. The road's closed till Sunday. Kettle Lake is an island.
narration | flag=road_closed: You stand at the window and watch the snow fill in the road, and understand that for two days there's no out. Not even the bad one.

# --- Before key day 11 (Saturday, October 30): Sunday the 24th to Friday. ---

=== bridge_sunday
narration | card=Sunday. The Keeper is asked.: The bell. The pew. The hands.
narration | if=flag:refused_tooth_public: On the steps after, Ezra again, in front of everyone: "H.L. Eighty-nine. It's asked." Every Sunday, until.
narration | if=flag:gave_tooth: On the steps after, nothing. Ezra nods to you. Hanna waves.
narration: Lunch. The roast. Your place.
narration | if=flag:saw_shape: You don't eat much. Mrs. Hollis notices, and puts more on your plate.
narration | card=Monday. The road opens.: The plow, at dawn. The road out, a white trench between banks taller than the car. You could leave.
narration: You drive to the end of town and back, to see if you still can. You can. You come back anyway.
narration: (The end of week three. Week four is still being written. Thank you for playing.)
