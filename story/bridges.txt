# BRIDGES: the days between key days (from week two on)
# Same format as the other story files. Each section is the bridge before a
# key day (GameState.BRIDGES). A line with card=... puts a title card up, in
# the town's voice, white on black like a silent film; the line's own text
# plays under it. When the section ends, the key day starts.
# Design: docs/week_two.md.

# --- Before key day 8 (Thursday, October 14): Wednesday the 13th. ---

=== bridge_wednesday
narration | card=Wednesday. The Keeper is paid. | cash=+150 | time=07:30: The envelope, under the door before dawn. A hundred and fifty dollars, every bill turned the same way up.
narration: Folded in with the money, this week's bulletin. Three hymn numbers circled, in pencil. One. Five. Nine.
paranoia: Nine. There's no rule nine. There's a rhyme with a nine in it.
narration | if=has:revolver: The revolver, where you left it. You check that it's where you left it. You check twice.
narration | if=!has:revolver: The snowbank by the back door, smooth and white. You don't look at it. You look at it.
narration | if=flag:hanna_priced or flag:hanna_blank or flag:hanna_refused: On the back shelf, the braid, still. Nobody's asked for it. Yet.
narration | time=22:00: At night, through the wall, the radio. Not the watch. The Reverend, preaching, a sermon he must have recorded on Sunday.
narration: "Seventy-nine days, friends. Seventy-nine days until the year turns over, and some say the world with it. I don't say that. I say: keep your stoves fed. Keep your doors the way you keep them. Keep each other."
narration: You lie awake counting. Seventy-nine. He's right. You don't know why you checked.

"Clear as a window, friends. Come down and see."

# --- Before key day 14 (Sunday, November 21): two weeks. ---

=== bridge_mid_november
narration | card=November. The minister calls again.: He calls the night radio twice more that week, and the week after. The same beautiful voice, from a different payphone each time, a different town each time, nearer.
narration: The Gleaner talks about him. The post office talks about him. Mrs. Hollis says she sat by the radio with her hands folded and cried, and didn't know why.
narration: On the nights he's on the radio, the shop phone doesn't ring. On the nights he isn't, it does: a car, wipers, sleet on a windshield. The road you drove in on.
narration | card=The Keeper is paid. | cash=+300: Two envelopes, two Wednesdays. Faced. The wool swatch again in the second, a little darker, as if it had been handled.
narration | card=The road closes. Three days.: Snow, and the pass shut, and the lake road shut. Nobody in, nobody out. The minister calls anyway, from somewhere the snow hasn't reached. "I'll be with you when the road opens, friends. Keep a light on."
narration: The Reverend, on the air, after him, quietly: "Our thanks to our caller." Nothing else. He doesn't sound grateful. He sounds like a man listening to a weather report.
narration | card=Saturday. The count.: The Reverend's count, Saturday night. "Forty-one days, friends." He's lost his place. He corrects himself. "Forty. Forty days."
paranoia: Forty days. The flood. The wilderness. He didn't mean it. Did he mean it.
narration | if=flag:ruth_has_keys: Your car in the lot, under the snow. You haven't driven it in two weeks. You couldn't if you wanted to. Ruth has the keys.

# --- After key day 38: into April (the clocks go forward). ---

=== bridge_into_april
narration | card=Saturday. April.: The first of April. Three weeks. The clocks go forward tonight. Everyone on the road changes them at the same minute; you hear the town do it, a hundred little clicks.

# --- Before key day 40 (Saturday, April 8): Monday the 3rd to Friday. ---

=== bridge_early_april
narration | card=Monday. The road.: Trucks on the road for the first time since December. A propane delivery. The mail van from Wasilla, with three months of mail in it. Nobody on the road goes out to meet it.
narration: In your box, under three months of catalogues: a postcard from the rental company in Anchorage. "Your vehicle is now 151 days overdue."
narration | card=Wednesday. The Keeper is paid. | cash=+150: The envelope, under the door. A hundred and fifty, faced. The last one, it says, in pencil on the back. "Last."
narration | card=Friday. Fasting.: Nobody eats. At noon the radio plays the clock, and the whole road stands on its porches in the sun to listen.

# --- Before key day 41 (Palm Sunday, April 16): Sunday the 9th to Saturday. ---

=== bridge_palm
narration | card=Sunday. One more week of light.: The hall. The anxious bench. Names. You go, or you don't. The count on the door goes to 1, a week early, and somebody paints over it and puts back the 2.
narration | card=Tuesday. The ice.: The ice off the point goes grey, then black, then grey again. At night it talks in long sentences, and the whole town lies awake listening, and in the morning nobody says what it said.
narration | if=flag:ruth_comes or flag:sam_comes or flag:cody_comes: Every night this week you pack a bag and unpack it again.
narration | card=Friday. Fasting.: Nobody eats. The children go round with the goat and a basket, collecting teeth.

# --- Before key day 42 (Good Friday, April 21): Monday the 17th to Thursday. ---

=== bridge_holy_week
narration | card=Monday. Four days.: The straw man taken down off the post and carried round the road on a pole, door to door, and every door opens to let him look in.
narration | if=flag:daniel_taken: The radio says the preacher's in custody in Anchorage. The radio says it once, and then plays the clock for the rest of the day, as if to make up for it.
narration | if=flag:daniel_given: Nobody says where he is. His room above the Gleaner is let to nobody. The cigar box is gone from the sill.
narration | if=flag:daniel_shot: The freezer hums. Nobody asks where the preacher went. Mrs. Hollis says, "Gone on ahead," and smiles, and pats your hand.
narration | card=Thursday. The last supper.: Every house lit, every table laid, every family eating for the first time in a week, all at once, at six. Through every window, the same meal. At the Gleaner, a place laid for you.

# --- After key day 42: breakup. ---

=== bridge_breakup
narration | card=Saturday. Breakup.: Five o'clock. The light already up. Out on the lake, the ice talking in one long sentence that doesn't stop.
