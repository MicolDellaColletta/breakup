=== morning
narration | time=09:30: Sunday, the twenty-third. Light at twenty past nine. Sunset after half past four. Every day a little longer, and every day the radio counts down instead of up.
narration | time=10:00 | show=hall: The church hall, the part that didn't burn. Full. Benches in rows where the long tables were at Thanksgiving. A lectern made of crates, and above it, through the big window, the ruin on the hill and the new pine thing on top of the tower stump.
narration: He reads the lesson from the lectern. Leviticus 16. Every Sunday now. The congregation says the last line with him, without being asked. Then he shuts the book, and his voice comes up until the big window hums with it.
daniel: Confess over him all the iniquities! All of them! Not the ones you've picked out for the occasion! Dr. Brekke signed a paper in December with a hand that shook, and he's been drinking since Advent to steady it. Come up, Doctor. The front pew. The anxious bench. Sit where they can look at you.
narration: Dr. Brekke gets up and walks to the front pew, the one they've cleared, and sits on it alone with his hands between his knees. Nobody looks at him. Everybody looks at him. The hall prays over him out loud, and he has to sit through all of it.
narration: Then, after the blessing, nobody leaves. Not barred doors this time. A queue. It forms along the side of the hall, quietly, in coats, to the little door behind the stage: the old props room, one bulb, two chairs. The counsel room, they call it now. He's going to see them one at a time.
narration: You walk up the aisle to find the end of it. Every head in the pews turns as you pass.

narration | if=flag:accused_on_air: In the queue, a space opens round you. A yard either side. Nobody says anything. Since the night on the radio, everyone in town knows you aren't John. Nobody knows what you are instead, so nobody calls you anything.

# --- The queue. ---

=== queue_widow
narration: Mrs. Hollis, three ahead of you, in her best coat, the brooch.

widow: I don't know what I'll confess. I've nothing left. That's the strange part. I've been carrying something forty years and I put it down at Christmas and I can't remember what it was.

# --- The counsel room, or not. ---

=== booth_confession
narration | show=booth | red=alone: Your turn. The door. Inside, one bulb, two chairs, scorched, knee to knee: the ones from the ruin. He's in one.
daniel: Sit. Closer. Mrs. Hollis was in that chair before you. She confessed to forgetting what she came to confess. I gave her a penance for it, and she thanked me.
narration: You sit. Your knees touch his. He watches your face, all of it, the way you watch a needle settle on the scale.
daniel: I've been here longer than you think. I stood in those trees across the road the week before you came. I took a picture of John's car. I rang once on a Saturday, to see if you'd jump.
daniel: You jumped.

=== confess_small
narration: You tell him something small. The candle you lit again when nobody was looking. The coat you touched, on its hook, when you thought you were alone. A lie you told as John that didn't matter to anyone.
narration: He listens. When you're done, he says it back to you. Exactly. Word for word, in your rhythm. Then again, with the weight on a different word. Then a third time, and it's a different sin.
daniel: What did that cost you? Nothing. Bring me something dearer next time. Go home.
-> booth_after

=== confess_john
narration: You open your mouth to tell him. The motel, the parking lot, the doorway, whichever it was. The empty chamber.
narration: He puts two fingers against your mouth. Not hard. Just enough.
daniel: Not yet. That one I'll want in March.
daniel: Carry it till then. I like watching you carry it.
-> booth_after

=== confess_accuse
narration: You don't confess anything. You ask him about the vestry. The bolt. The bell.
narration: He's delighted. He leans back in the scorched chair as if you'd brought him a present.
daniel: Eleven minutes on the rope. You know that. You want me to say it in a church hall with the door open and the whole town on the other side of it. I would. I'll say it to anyone who asks. Do you know how many have asked? You. Only ever you.
daniel: That's what they come in here for. Not to tell me. To not ask.
-> booth_after

=== confess_nothing
narration: You say nothing. He says nothing. The counsel room is very quiet. Through the door, the queue shuffles.
narration: After a long time he leans in until his knee is pressed against yours, and keeps it there, and smiles.
daniel: Nothing. You stood in that queue an hour to bring me nothing, and your leg's going like a rabbit's. That's plenty. I'll take it.
-> booth_after
