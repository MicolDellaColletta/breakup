# THE THIRTY-SEVENTH NIGHT: Saturday, March 25. After his price.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: nothing, for the first time;
# the dial hums. The dog comes up every night this week, fed or not. Out of
# place: the gun (or the round) on the kitchen table where you didn't put
# it (odd_six). Design: docs/march.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment.
narration | if=flag:fork6_yes: You said yes. You keep saying it again, under your breath, to hear if it sounds like you.
narration | if=flag:fork6_refuse: A race. Four weeks.
narration | if=flag:fork6_confess: You told him. Eleven years. You told him, and he said he'd waited.
paranoia: The rules.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Saturday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Nothing. The dial hums. For the first time since November, no voice. Only the hum, and the room, and you, waiting for him to speak first.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. The silence is the same either way.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyseven
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_thirtyseven | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The windows.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. The ice on the lake, under the moon, has gone the colour of an old tooth.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. You don't sleep.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_thirtyseven
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. Then down.
narration | if=flag:fork6_yes: At the top of the stairs it stops, and stays, and breathes, the way it did the first week.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. You didn't fill it. Three o'clock. The stairs, anyway.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door. In the morning the bowl is clean.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:00: Sunday.
narration | if=!flag:bowl_door: The dog came up, as it has every night this week. Fed or not. It stood at the bedroom door until it was light.

narration | if=flag:gun_six: John's gun. Six in the cylinder. Laid square to the table's edge, the grip toward your chair.
narration | if=!flag:gun_six: The flattened round, standing on its end, like a chess piece.
