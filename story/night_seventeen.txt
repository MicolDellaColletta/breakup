# THE SEVENTEENTH NIGHT: Thursday, November 25. Thanksgiving night.
# Same format as the other nights. The radio: him again, the late repeat.
# Out of place: on the back step, a plate under foil, still warm, from the
# potluck: "For the Keeper's dog." There's no dog (odd_plate in spots.cfg,
# downstairs at the back door). Then the bridge to December.
# Design: docs/thanksgiving.md.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs. Full. Too full. Mrs. Hollis's foil plate in your hands, warm through the foil.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment, cold after the hall. Everything where you left it.
narration | if=flag:sat_in_place: You sat in it. You keep feeling the warmth of the seat, at the backs of your legs, as if you hadn't stood up.
narration | if=flag:refused_pie: You didn't eat the pie. You keep coming back to it, like a tooth.
paranoia: The rules. Same as always.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio. Thursday.
paranoia: Rule seven.
> Turn it on -> radio_on | flag=radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration | sound=click: Him. The late repeat. The same reading, the same breath before the first word, the same pause in the middle of a word, exactly where you stood at the glass.
daniel | on=radio: You need someone to carry it for you. And you have one. You've had one since October.
narration: Then, after it, where the Reverend would come on to say goodnight, nothing. A minute of the hiss of an open microphone in an empty room. Then off.
paranoia: They kept the pause. They kept the pause where you were standing.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it off. Through the wall, the neighbors' radio, the same voice, a half-second ahead of the one across the road, a half-second behind the one up the hill.
narration: You put the pillow over your head. You can still count the words.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl. On the counter, the foil plate. Turkey, stuffing, a slice of pie wrapped separately.
paranoia: Rule five.
> Fill it -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_seventeen
> Take it down to the back door -> kitchen_door | if=has:note_bowl | set=fed_dog | flag=fed_seventeen | flag=bowl_door
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_door
narration: Down to the back door with the bowl. You set it inside the door, on no line at all.

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles. On the line. You think about putting some turkey in. You don't. It isn't in the rules.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The front windows. The town going dark, house by house, up the hill. The church last. The room above the Gleaner, not at all.
paranoia: Rule one.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window | flag=window_open

=== windows_closed
narration | sound=latch: Latched. Twice.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold. Woodsmoke. Far off, from the lake, a single low flat note, like a duck call. Nobody hunts at night.

=== painting
narration | time=+5: The painting. The figure far out on the ice, in the long brown coat. You've always thought of it as a man.
narration: Tonight, in this light, it's small. A boy's height. Bowing.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs, through the steel door. The back door.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Through the glass, the step, and something on it, square, pale. You don't open the door to see.

=== back_door_locked
narration | sound=latch: The key turns. Through the glass, the step, and something on it, square, pale. You locked it in, or out.

=== sleep
narration | time=+20 | show=bedroom: You lie down. Eleven. Twelve.
narration: No phone. You know now it won't. He doesn't need it.
-> night_dog

=== night_dog
-> night_dog_door | if=flag:bowl_door
-> night_dog_fed | if=flag:fed_seventeen
-> night_dog_unfed

=== night_dog_fed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration | sound=stairs: Three o'clock. The stairs. One and then the other.
narration | stop=stairs: The kitchen. A long time at the bowl. Then down, and the back door, and then a sound from the step you've never heard it make: eating. Slowly. Something with all night to do it.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_unfed
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door. Nothing comes up. From downstairs, at three, from the back step, a sound like eating. Slowly.
-> morning_after

=== night_dog_door
narration | show=bedroom: Facing the door.
narration: Three o'clock. The back door, below. A long time. In the morning the bowl is clean, and the foil from the step is folded, very small, into a square, and set inside it.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: Friday.
narration | if=flag:saw_plate: The back step. The plate, gone. The foil, folded into a square, very small, set on the step where the plate was.

# --- Something out of place (docs/nights.md). ---

=== odd_plate
narration | time=+5: The back step, through the glass. You open the door an inch, because it's yours, because it's your step.
narration | flag=saw_plate: A paper plate, under foil, crimped tight round the edge. Still warm. Turkey. Stuffing. A slice of pie.
narration: On the foil, in marker, in a hand you don't know, a careful adult's print: For the Keeper's dog.
paranoia: There's no dog. Everybody knows there's no dog. You've never told anyone about the dog.
narration: The snow round the step is trodden flat. A lot of feet. Children's, some of them. None of the prints lead away.
