# KEY DAY 28: Monday, January 3, 2000. The Disappointment.
# Same format as the other counter days: Mrs. Hollis brings the coat to hang
# in the shop; Ruth, and the freezer come due (if MC didn't bring her, Daniel
# told her: one chance at the truth); then, at closing, him, in the customer
# chair, speaking to MC as himself for the first time. He quotes the
# notebook. See VISITS in scripts/counter.gd. Design: docs/january.md.

=== morning
narration | time=07:00: Monday, the third of January. The year two thousand, and it looks like every other Monday.
narration: The radio, before light. Not hosting now. Preaching. "Two thousand years, and you thought He'd come for a party. He's coming for a reckoning, and He'll start with this town. Breakup, friends. Mark the day."
narration: The calendar on the wall, still on January. The one Saturday in April with nothing on it.
narration | if=flag:new_year_ruth: On the counter downstairs, a paper hat from the party, flattened. You don't remember bringing it home.
narration | if=flag:new_year_sam: Through the door, a page torn from a notebook. Midnight, 1/1/00. Heard by two. Your initials, and his.
narration | if=flag:new_year_daniel: The receiver back on its hook, downstairs. Wiped. You left it off. You're sure you left it off.
narration | if=flag:has_books: John's books in the drawer under the shells, in their bread bag. You haven't read them. You've read the first line. That's enough.
narration | if=flag:locker_lapsed: Somewhere in a box marked LOST, in Anchorage, John's books. You think about the box more than you thought you would.
-> opening

=== opening
narration | time=09:00: You turn the sign. OPEN. The light comes at ten. Four minutes earlier than yesterday. Nobody says it's good news.

# --- Mrs. Hollis: the coat comes to the shop. ---

=== widow_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+60: The bell. Mrs. Hollis, the candles gone from her arms now, carrying something long in a dry cleaner's bag over her shoulder, like a body.
widow: He says April. Breakup. I've never minded waiting. I waited forty years for Ray to come in for supper.
narration | if=fray>=4 or unknown>=4 or flag:new_year_ruth or flag:new_year_sam or flag:new_year_daniel: She pats your hand. "And thank you, dear, for what you said at the potluck. About Ray. Nobody's ever said that to me." You said nothing at the potluck. You're almost sure you said nothing.
narration: She unzips the bag. The coat. Long, brown, the brass buttons, the hem done.
widow: It should hang here. In the shop. So it's handy. On the back of the door, where Abraham's used to go.
> "All right." -> widow_coat_yes | flag=coat_in_shop | lean=warmth
> "Not in the shop. Please." -> widow_coat_no | flag=refused_coat_hang | eye=+1 | lean=paranoia

=== widow_coat_yes
narration: She hangs it on the hook on the back of the door herself, and smooths the shoulders, and stands back.
narration: Every time the door opens now, the coat swings a little, as if somebody had just come in, or was just about to go out.
narration | sound=bell: She knocks twice on the frame. The coat sways.

=== widow_coat_no
narration: She looks at you a long time. Then she zips it back into its bag.
widow: I'll keep it at mine, then. It's no trouble. It'll be there.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. By noon, Ezra knows the Keeper wouldn't have his coat in the shop. By Sunday, everyone.

# --- Ruth: the freezer comes due. ---

=== ruth_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=+90: The bell. Ruth.
-> ruth_grieving | if=flag:ruth_saw_father
-> ruth_closed | if=faithless:ruth
-> ruth_told

=== ruth_grieving
narration: Her father's scarf round her neck. She comes to the counter and doesn't buy anything.
ruth: I've been telling people he's dead. Out loud. The way you'd practise a word in another language.
ruth: Nobody's surprised. That's the thing. Everybody nods. Like they knew. Like they were waiting for me to catch up.
narration: She puts her hand flat on the counter, near yours, not touching.
ruth: You were the only one who showed me. Everybody else let me think Anchorage.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. The scarf, a dark stripe up the white road, all the way to the Gleaner.

=== ruth_closed
narration: She doesn't come to the counter. She stands just inside the door with her coat buttoned.
ruth: He told me. About the freezer. About you, standing there.
ruth: I don't know why I came in. I wanted to see your face. I've seen it.
narration | sound=bell: She goes.

=== ruth_told
narration: She doesn't take her gloves off. Her face is a shut door.
ruth: He told me. Last night, at the bar. He never lies, he said, so I'd better sit down.
ruth: My father. In the freezer. Since October. And you knew. Since the twenty-first. He said you stood there with your hand on the lid until it was warm.
narration: She waits.
paranoia: Lie. Say you didn't know. Say you never opened it. She wants you to lie. She wants to believe you.
> "I knew. I didn't know how to tell you." -> ruth_truth | flag=ruth_told_late | lean=warmth
> "I never opened it. He's lying." -> ruth_lie | betray=ruth | flag=ruth_lied_freezer | lean=paranoia

=== ruth_truth
narration: She closes her eyes.
ruth: No. Nobody knows how. That's why nobody does it.
narration: She opens them. Something has gone out of them, and something has stayed.
ruth: I'll get over it. I won't get over it. Both.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. She doesn't slam the door. You'd rather she had.

=== ruth_lie
narration: She looks at you for a long moment. Then she nods, slowly.
ruth: He doesn't lie. That's the thing about him. That's the only thing about him.
narration | sound=bell: She goes. You know, watching her walk up the road, that she won't come in again. Not to buy, not to bring your glass, not for anything.

# --- Him, at closing. As himself. ---

=== daniel_enters
narration | sound=bell | time=15:40 | red=alone: The bell. Twenty to four. The good coat. He closes the door behind him and stops the bell's tongue with two fingers, so it rings only once.
narration | if=flag:coat_in_shop: The coat on the back of the door swings as the door closes. He watches it settle. He looks pleased.
narration: He doesn't stand at the counter. He sits in the customer chair by the stove, the way Olstad did, and crosses one leg over the other, and looks at you.
daniel: I'm not here to buy anything. You've had me on the radio and in the pulpit and down a phone. I wanted to see what your face does when it's only me.
narration: And then he says your name. Not John. Yours. The real one. And the shop won't hold it. The stove, the clock, the wind at the door all go out at once, a hiss like a needle at the end of a record, and inside the hiss his mouth shapes it, every letter, and you know every letter, and you hear none of them.
narration: Then the clock again. You haven't heard it aloud since October. You still haven't. He watches your face while you don't.
daniel: There. That. Do that again.
