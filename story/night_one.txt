# THE FIRST NIGHT AS KEEPER (after day one)
# Same format as drive.txt (see the notes at the top of that file).
# The apartment at night: radio, kitchen, windows, back door, bed. When a
# section ends with nowhere to go, you're back in the apartment.

=== arrival
narration | sound=stairs: Up the stairs from the back of the hallway. Every step, the same complaint.
narration | stop=stairs: The apartment is colder than you left it. Your breath shows, a little, in the kitchen corner.
paranoia: The rules. All of them. Before you sleep.

=== radio
narration | time=+10: The radio on the side table, amber dial, the cord still where you left it.
paranoia: Rule seven. Keep the radio on at night. The silence is worse.
> Turn it on -> radio_on
> Leave it off -> radio_off | break=radio

=== radio_on
narration: Static. Then, for a few seconds, the static thins and something clear comes through.
narration: That calm voice again. Unhurried, like someone sitting up with a sick friend.
narration: "...and a reminder for our friends out at the edge of town, on a cold night like this one. A back door is for coming in. Not for keeping out."
narration: Then a hymn, very far away, and the static closes over it.
paranoia: He's talking to you. He can't be talking to you.

=== radio_off
narration: You leave it. The quiet settles over the room like snow.
narration: In the quiet, the freezer downstairs. And under it, so faint you could be making it up, a slow tapping. Like a fingernail on glass.

=== kitchen
narration | time=+10: The bowl by the stove. Empty again. Licked clean, as if it had never been anything else.
paranoia: Rule five. Feed the dog.
> Fill the bowl -> kitchen_fed | set=fed_dog
> Leave it -> kitchen_unfed | break=dog

=== kitchen_fed
narration: The kibble rattles into the steel. You set the bowl back exactly where it was. You've learned that much.

=== kitchen_unfed
narration: You roll the sack shut. The bowl stays empty.
paranoia: You know what happened last time. Or you don't, and that's worse.

=== windows
narration | time=+10: The two front windows, the neon below painting the frost on the glass red.
paranoia: Rule one. Close every window before dark. It's been dark for hours.
> Check the latches -> windows_closed
> Open one, just a crack, for air -> windows_open | break=window

=== windows_closed
narration: Both latched. You check them again anyway, then a third time, until the checking feels like a rule of its own.

=== windows_open
narration: Cold pours in at once, sweet and sharp. Down in the lot, nothing moves.
narration: Then, at the edge of the trees, something does. Or the wind does.

=== back_door
narration | time=+20 | sound=floorboards: Down the stairs again. At the bottom, the hallway, and the heavy steel door at the end of it.
narration | sound=door_shut: It's colder past the steel door, and louder. The back room: shelving, crates, a mop gone stiff in its bucket.
narration: And the freezer. A walk-in, its door as big as a closet, its handle furred white with frost. The hum comes from inside it, steady as breathing.
paranoia: Not tonight.
narration: The back door is at the far end, past the freezer. A deadbolt, and a keyhole under it.
narration | if=!has:back_door_key: There's no key in the lock. Whoever had one took it with them.
paranoia: Rule one. Leave the back door unlocked.
> Leave it unlocked -> back_door_unlocked | lean=paranoia
> Lock it with the key on the red tag -> back_door_locked | if=has:back_door_key | set=locked_back_door | break=back_door | lean=animal

=== back_door_unlocked
narration: You leave it. Every part of you that has ever lived in a city wants to lock it.
narration: You walk back past the freezer without looking at it. It's harder than it should be.

=== back_door_locked
narration: Olstad's key turns with a clean, heavy sound. You try the handle. Locked.
narration: You stand there with your hand on it longer than you need to.
unknown: She told you not to, sweetheart. Didn't she.

=== sleep
narration | time=+10: The sheets are just as cold as last night. You lie there and count the rules you kept.
narration | time=03:10: Somewhere after three, a sound wakes you. Downstairs. Not the freezer. Not the wind.
narration: Then quiet. The kind of quiet that's waiting for you to move.
> Go down and look -> go_down | lean=animal
> Stay in bed -> stay_in_bed | lean=paranoia

=== stay_in_bed
narration: You pull the blanket up and lie there with your eyes open.
narration: It doesn't come again. You almost wish it would. Not knowing sits on your chest until the window goes gray.
-> morning_after

=== go_down
narration | sound=stairs: Down the stairs in your socks. You don't turn on a light.
narration | stop=stairs: You take the shortcut through the shop: behind the counter, out across the floor, past the front windows.
narration: The neon is still on. In the dark glass of the window, there you are. A shape against the red.
narration: You stop. The shape stops.
narration: Then your reflection raises one hand, slowly, as if to say hello.
paranoia: Rule eight. If you see yourself in the shop window after midnight, don't wave.
paranoia: Your hands are at your sides. Both of them. You checked.
> Wave back -> waved | break=wave | lean=unknown
> Don't wave -> didnt_wave
> Look away -> didnt_wave | lean=paranoia

=== waved
narration: You lift your hand. In the glass, your reflection lowers its own, as if it had only been waiting for you.
narration: Then it's just your reflection again. Doing what you do. You think.
unknown: There. Was that so hard, sweetheart?
-> back_room

=== didnt_wave
narration: You keep your hands at your sides. After a long moment, the hand in the glass goes down.
narration: You walk the rest of the way without looking at the window again.
-> back_room

=== back_room
narration | sound=door_shut: Through the hallway. Through the steel door. The freezer hums.
-> back_room_locked | if=locked_back_door
-> back_room_open

=== back_room_locked
narration: The back door is shut and locked, the way you left it.
narration: As you watch, the handle turns. Slowly, all the way down, from outside.
narration: It holds there. Then, just as slowly, it comes back up.
narration: Nothing knocks. Nothing tries again.
paranoia: Somebody knows the old man never locked it.
-> morning_after

=== back_room_open
narration: The back door stands open an inch. Snow has blown in across the floor in a pale tongue.
narration: In the snow, footprints. Boots, big ones. They come in from outside.
narration: They go to the freezer door.
narration: They don't come back out.
paranoia: Close the door. Go upstairs. Don't open the freezer.
paranoia: Don't open the freezer.
-> morning_after

=== morning_after
narration | time=07:30: In the morning you can't say for sure how much of it you dreamed.
narration: (End of day one. Day two is still being written. Thank you for playing.)
